## P1 — Current Principal semantic corrections / ratifications

Source: current 2026-09-15 Principal review of the greenfield recovery. These are recorded explicitly so future workers do not reconstruct them from conversational memory. They are authority-bearing corrections to this work session, not independent historical evidence.

### Definition-contract requirement — RATIFIED

A durable semantic definition must carry at minimum:

- Term
- Positive definition — what it is
- Structural function — what distinction it preserves
- Discriminator/test — how to tell it from neighboring concepts
- Must-not-collapse-with
- Canonical questions
- Worked example
- Boundary/counterexample
- Dependencies — which other definitions it presupposes
- Standing/version/provenance
- Supersession history

Prompts/tools should reference the installed definition/closure rather than reconstructing it by paraphrase.

### MAP / TERRITORY — RATIFIED

Definition: A map/projection is a representation of a referent produced under particular mapping conditions. It is not the referent itself.

Structural function: Prevent representation, interpretation, evidence, or model state from acquiring the identity or standing of what it represents.

Discriminator: Could the representation change while the referent remains unchanged? Could two validly produced representations differ while referring to the same territory? If yes, they are maps, not territory.

Must not collapse with: Referent, mapper, access, reference frame, quadrant.

### REFERENCE FRAME — RATIFIED

Definition: The indexing relation relative to which a referent is represented.

Not the mapper. Not the access mechanism. Not the representation.

Structural function: Makes explicit relative to what a claim or representation obtains.

Discriminator: If mapper and evidence source remain fixed but the index relative to which the referent is considered changes, the reference frame changed.

### MAPPER — RATIFIED

Definition: The actor or process producing the map/projection.

Discriminator: Change who or what constructs the representation while holding referent and reference frame constant.

### ACCESS — RATIFIED

Definition: The evidentiary or observational path available to the mapper.

Discriminator: Same mapper, same referent, same reference frame, different sensory/tool/testimony/inference path.

### QUADRANT — RATIFIED

Definition: One of the four structurally available indexing relations through which the same focal referent can be interrogated.

Core invariant: **Changing quadrant does not change the referent.**

### UL — RATIFIED

Definition: The focal referent under a self-indexed reference frame.

`Internal` is not the defining property.

Epistemic consequence: Every UL representation available to ECOS remains a map produced by a mapper through some access path. The mapper does not acquire the focal referent's self-indexed access merely by modeling it.

Worked human example: a person's first-person report can provide unusually strong evidence for a self-indexed map; ECOS possesses the testimony, not the person's phenomenology.

Boundary example: a thermostat's self-indexed register may concern what it itself senses/registers. Conscious experience is not required by the structural definition.

### HOLOARCHIC LEVEL — RATIFIED CORE

Definition: Relative structural level determined through asymmetric existence dependency.

Canonical questions:

1. What must continue to exist, and in what organization, for this referent to continue existing as this referent?
2. If this referent disappears, what can continue existing as itself?

Canonical intuition: atoms -> molecules -> cells.

Canonical worked fixture: physical hand + MTG cards + holding organization. Hand and cards can each survive loss of the higher-order physical handful; the handful disappears when the constitutive holding organization disappears.

### MASTER KEY — CURRENT RATIFICATION / GREENFIELD CONVERGENCE

Inquiry without a Master Key may be underdetermined even when territory is held nearly constant. Master Key governs which distinctions matter to the present inquiry, required resolution, completion, and relevance; it does not create the Referent or its structural capacities.

Canonical contrast for this work:

A. a person is commissioned to hold a deck of MTG cards for as long as possible;
B. a person holds carefully selected MTG cards they intend to play effectively.

A card leaving the physical hand may be failure under A and success under B.

### DRIVES / CAPACITIES — PRINCIPAL CORRECTION

Drives and Capacities are two expressions/readings of the same directional tendencies of a holon. They are not two independent axes. Lines are categorically different.

For the current Register-B probe, four directional tendencies remain structurally available: preservation/agency, adaptation/participation, transcendence, dissolution.

### TRANSCENDENCE — CURRENT SHARPENING / TO BE REGISTER-B QUALIFIED

Directional hypothesis: transcendence is vertical emergence, not generic improvement. Better persistence or performance at the same holoarchic level is insufficient by itself.

Boundary intuition: changing spherical marbles to easier-to-hold cubed marbles can increase preservation capacity without thereby constituting transcendence.

A transcendence claim should require a higher-order organization/capability that is not merely better persistence/adaptation of the current organization.

Standing: PRINCIPAL-SUPPLIED SHARPENING; requires executable discrimination before promotion beyond Register-B qualified semantics.

### DISSOLUTION — CURRENT SHARPENING / TO BE REGISTER-B QUALIFIED

Directional hypothesis: dissolution concerns loss of the organization by which the focal holon continues to exist as that holon; constituents may survive.

The same event may therefore be dissolution at one holoarchic level while participating in higher-order emergence at another.

Standing: PRINCIPAL-SUPPLIED SHARPENING; requires executable discrimination before promotion beyond Register-B qualified semantics.

### QUESTION FORWARD — PRINCIPAL STRENGTHENING

Low/no signal is not an acceptable terminal result for a required structural dimension.

Required return when a dimension is low/no signal:

`CURRENT SIGNAL -> CALIBRATED QUESTION -> ANSWER/DETECTION CRITERIA -> PLAUSIBLE ACTIVATION SCENARIO -> RE-ENTRY TRIGGER / CONSEQUENCE`

The activation scenario may be actual or invented, but an invented scenario must be explicitly marked as calibration material rather than evidence. Prefer a plausible case near a decision boundary when that better tests whether the distinction is actually understood.

This strengthens, rather than replaces, the ECO-92/93 and BUILD 7 Question-Forward contract.
