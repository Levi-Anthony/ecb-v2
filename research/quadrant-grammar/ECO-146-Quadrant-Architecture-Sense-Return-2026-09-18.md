## 2. Declared operating context

**Focal module:** the bounded quadrant inquiry capability within SIGMA/ECOS/ECB v2. It serves situated inquiry about a referent; it is not the full URG, an ingestion redesign, a general planner, or an action executor.

The module is invoked or relied upon when an account is preserved, constructed, assessed, revised, carried to another worker, or used as support. The focal R may be a person, rock, device, abstraction, composite or bounded episode. A report, property or constituent remains about that R until explicit refocus makes it the subject. Novel consequential classes remain subject to Q's qualification boundary.

**Roles relevant at this resolution:** a contributor constructs or revises an account; an evaluator assesses its meaning, evidence or sufficiency; a consumer interprets, decides or acts using it; governing sources supply the basis for authority where required. One participant can occupy several roles, provided their distinct basis and limits remain inspectable. These are functional roles, not assignments to humans, models, software or separate components.

**Consequential reliance** occurs when a result, coverage claim or sufficiency disposition is treated as adequate support for an interpretation, decision, action, promotion or successor use whose meaning or permissibility changes if the account is wrong, underspecified, stale or outside its qualification. Reliance therefore includes interpreting and handing off an account as adequate, not only dispatching an external action.

Raw preservation, exploratory construction and authorized clarification can continue under an incomplete basis to the degree G permits. They do not silently become reliable classification, semantic acceptance or permission. This boundary prevents both premature reliance and unnecessary prohibition of useful evidence collection.

**Bounded objective:** expose enough responsibilities and design choices for a later Shape to arrange a conforming system without changing R01–R10. The current decision needs no latency target, deployment topology, end-user interface, fixed automation ratio, throughput promise or automatic global-change detector. Those details can alter later allocations; their absence does not alter the required responsibility set. No such capability is promised here.

**Outside the module:** full URG distinction development; naming; global co-reference policy; metaphysical adjudication; raw-intake/bootstrap redesign; real-world authority creation; planning and effect execution; and semantic installation. Surrounding identity, source custody, orientation, governance and execution capabilities may provide inputs. The quadrant module must establish that the inputs it relies on satisfy its scoped obligations; their mere existence is insufficient.

**Authority and stopping:** K authorizes this derivation and persistence of its return. It does not authorize choosing architecture or opening Shape. Register B sufficiency is reached when all requirements have intelligible responsibilities, control points and material open choices; every remaining branch is architectural rather than a hidden semantic/authority gap. An ambiguity in the above context that changes required behavior would trigger §12/U3.

## 8. Information, standing and recoverability obligations

The following are recoverable meanings, not prescribed fields or objects. They may be distributed, provided the combination supports the particular reliance.

| Information that must remain recoverable | Standing to preserve / result if unavailable |
|---|---|
| Original input/source; focal R; consequential map/mapper distinction; warranted identity and unresolved co-reference | Attribution and partiality survive later refinement. Missing identity basis limits the claims requiring it; it does not prove the referent absent. |
| Operative boundary/rationale; constituent/co-relatum standing; relevant relation; time/state; known context and discovery criterion | Keep historical and currently applicable scope distinguishable. Missing or incompatible scope blocks affected seating/reuse until clarified. |
| G/use, consequential frames/priorities, ideal outcome, stop/fail/reentry and governing authority where needed | A historically valid G is not necessarily applicable now. Orientation can be composed; authority must come from a governing source rather than the account carrying it. |
| Original/clarified/decomposed question, correspondence, seat, burden and fourfold coverage meanings | Preserve ambiguity and unconsidered versus unresolved versus warranted deferral versus warranted answer. Filled positions do not establish closure. |
| Evidence origin/access, claim role, premise/implication limits, qualification basis and correction conditions | Preserve shared origin and scoped negative evidence. Missing acknowledgment does not prove nonoccurrence; changed support does not make a claim automatically false. |
| Material unknown, calibrated question, contrasting signals, notice route, consequence, allowed interim use and reentry | Preserve unanswered and presently unanswerable standing. Recording a question neither answers it nor guarantees eventual detection. |
| Change and prior basis, affected-scope reasoning, unresolved applicability and disposition | Old answers remain under their original scope. “Requalified” requires its supporting assessment; unknown impact cannot be presented as a complete impact analysis. |
| Sufficiency judgment, intended use, limitations and evidence supporting that judgment | Distinguish requirements qualification, architecture qualification, conformance and runtime observation. An unestablished claim stays unestablished even if its document is retrievable. |

**Historical-content limit:** retaining a digest can establish that bytes differ; it cannot recover the lost bytes or what the old evidence established. Where reconstruction of an earlier answer is needed, the chosen composition must provide that meaning through recoverable sources. If it cannot, expose the historical-recovery limit and block the reliance that requires it. No global retention policy is invented.

**Currentness:** “current” is applicable standing for a declared use, not newest timestamp. Several individually retrievable sources may belong to incompatible scopes. B/F/G therefore have to establish their joint applicability at reliance, not merely that each link works. This earns Q2; it does not choose snapshots, versions, locking, caching or a distributed-consistency mechanism.

## 9. Composition and delegation boundaries

A–G are obligations of the bounded capability in context. They need not all be realized inside one component.

| Composition possibility | What may be supplied elsewhere | Boundary that remains accountable |
|---|---|---|
| Directly held information | Original question, an answer, source excerpt, scope declaration or assessed disposition | Holding it does not establish authenticity, applicability, completeness or authority. |
| Resolution by reference | R identity, boundary account, G, evidence, prior judgment, history or governing source | The necessary meaning and standing must be recoverable when relied upon. A pointer is not satisfaction; incompatible or unavailable dependencies need an explicit use consequence. |
| Inheritance from a surrounding ECOS responsibility | Identity continuity, source custody, orientation, authority resolution, qualification policy or action permission | Identify what is inherited, from which source and under what scope. Do not redesign that system here, infer it is installed, or treat its output as universally sufficient. |
| Delegation to an already-qualified capability | A bounded structural check, formal consequence, source recovery or scoped semantic assessment | Recover the delegated proposition, inputs, method/qualification limits, result and reentry. Qualification for one role does not imply qualification for another. No present capability is declared to discharge all A–G. |
| Non-mechanized judgment with inspectable standing | Boundary adequacy, natural-language burden, relevance, support interpretation or sufficiency | Preserve its basis, consequence and correction route. This is not an exception allowing an activated consequential transition to depend solely on remembered instructions. Shape must resolve that enforcement obligation. |

No mandatory giant object, exhaustive graph, universal relation taxonomy, central validator or separate reviewer follows. Composition is admissible only if it preserves the distinctions, recoverability and reliance behavior. Neither “all in prose” nor “fully typed” receives automatic adequacy or simplicity credit.

The module cannot grant itself execution authority. A surrounding authority mechanism may supply an applicable basis; if that basis is absent, affected action/promotion reliance is blocked while other authorized inquiry remains possible. This is an interface obligation, not a commission to implement governance or reopen bootstrap.
