### 2.1 Grammar and non-substitution

R is the focal referent; G is the Governing Orientation. Ordinary traversal holds R, G, the consequential boundary, time/state scope, and relevance criterion fixed. Constitutive inquiry concerns R as bounded, including its constituents. Participatory inquiry concerns that same R through a specified relation crossing or exceeding its boundary. Governing burden asks what may, must, or cannot obtain; determinate burden asks what obtains in the case.

| Seat / burden | Governing | Determinate |
|---|---|---|
| Constitutive | UL | UR |
| Participatory | LL | LR |

These are working coordinates, not final names, evidence bins, or prescribed storage cardinalities. Four obligations must be available; four answers or exactly four question records are not required. Ambiguous or compound questions are clarified, decomposed with lineage, or left explicitly unresolved. Wording, access method, consciousness, enclosure, scale, number, confidence, and lexical rule/fact classes cannot select a coordinate.

The mapper's purpose must not silently become R's purpose. Selected map contents must not become R's exhaustive contents. Known co-relata are not the universe. Registering a UUID does not establish real-world co-reference, subject uniqueness, completeness, standing, warrant, authority, or currentness. A datum reused for several roles remains one source origin. Report is not occurrence; necessary is not sufficient; unknown is not nonexistent; unresolved is not invalid.

Consequential reliance includes treating an account as adequate support for interpretation, decision, promotion, handoff, or action whose meaning or permissibility would change if the account were wrong, stale, unsupported, or outside scope. It is broader than execution. Permitted custody and exploratory inquiry may continue while affected reliance is blocked.

A-G are responsibility families, not seven components. A preserves focal continuity and situated context. B resolves applicable orientation and standing. C makes question burdens and fourfold coverage inspectable. D preserves warrant for each claim role. E discriminates material uncertainty and reentry. F reconciles change, affected applicability, and history. G bounds consequential reliance and conformance claims.

CP1 establishes/changes focal subject or context. CP2 forms, classifies, clarifies, or claims inquiry coverage. CP3 uses evidence or strengthens a claim. CP4 defers, stops, or routes uncertainty. CP5 discovers context or learns of change. CP6 reports sufficiency, relies, or transfers as adequate support. These may coincide and recur; they are not six compulsory approval ceremonies.

### 2.3 Four roles and six crossings

Situated Basis owns A/B context, not evidence adequacy or global identity resolution. Inquiry State owns C/D/E question, warrant, and uncertainty, not final use permission. Applicability Reconciler owns F observed-change applicability, not perfect change detection. Reliance Qualifier owns G use-level composition and disposition, not execution or authority creation.

| Contract | Meaning that crosses | Required negative discrimination |
|---|---|---|
| C1 Situated Basis resolution | R, boundary, G/use, scope, source standing, context, relevant authority references; explicit available/unavailable/historical/incompatible/unresolved | All references resolve but incompatible G/boundary does not pass joint applicability |
| C2 Inquiry/coverage | Original/refined question lineage, seat/burden, claim roles, all four coverage meanings | Four filled unresolved cells do not prove adequate inquiry |
| C3 Warrant/assessment | Proposition, exact inputs, origin, method/worker/checker, result, standing, scope, limits, correction | Stored PASS without discriminating method evidence remains unsupported |
| C4 Change/requalification | Surfaced change, before/after semantic payload, affected dependencies/use, historical result, unknown impact, scoped response | Digest change without old meaning does not establish historical requalification |
| C5 Reliance/disposition | Explicit use, exact applicable basis, coverage/warrant/QF, applicability, separately resolved authority where required | Current ACTION/HOLD, retrieval, or a judgment record cannot self-authorize |
| C6 Handoff/reuse | Recoverable exact/equivalent lineage, use envelope, disposition, QF/reentry/currentness limits | Reversible-trial qualification does not cover a new permanent-use request |

### 2.4 Frozen Q1-Q7 answers

Q1: stable focal identity plus recoverable situated-basis revisions; refocus is explicit, not enrichment. Q2: exact/equivalent source composition and joint-applicability assessment, not link success. Q3: mechanically inspectable cross-product structure plus semantic clarification with preserved lineage. Q6: historical meaning and scoped impact, not latest-wins/global invalidation. Q7: qualify each consequential use; changed consumer intent re-enters qualification.

## 5. Selected persistence, identity, history, and source of truth

### 5.1 Three bounded private control surfaces

Only a later implementation commission may create schema `ecb_quadrant` and these surfaces.

**`channels`**: `id uuid` primary key and Referent foreign key; `epoch bigint >= 0`; `head_receipt_id uuid` pointing to the selected operation binding, null only for a new epoch-zero channel. One channel coordinates an inquiry; it is not its focal real-world R. Row locking and expected-epoch comparison serialize operational selection. Selecting a changed basis/inquiry or material/possibly material notice increments the epoch. Retaining an unselected candidate, assessment, or reliance result does not. The head selects inputs, never semantic truth or adequacy. Explicit refocus opens a linked new channel.

**`records`**: immutable `receipt_id uuid` primary key/foreign key to `ordinary_operations`; `artifact_id uuid` foreign key to `text_artifacts`; nullable `channel_id`; bounded `record_role`; `profile_version`; nullable observed/result epochs and reviewer capability reference; `validator_revision`. The ordinary operation's result Referent must equal `artifact_id`. Channel is mandatory for basis/inquiry/change/selection/reliance and their channel-specific assessments; standalone snapshots and capability administration records may have none. Cross-channel reuse references the same Artifact rather than duplicating source identity. Index by channel/result epoch/receipt, by Artifact, and by reviewer capability for declared impact lookup. Head references must name an allowed selection/change binding in that same channel and agree with its resulting epoch. Deferred foreign keys allow an atomic new-channel/head insert. Updates/deletes/truncation are denied to runtime roles and rejected by immutable-record controls.

**`reviewer_capabilities`**: Referent-backed UUID; private 32-byte key digest; exact immutable method/remit/qualification-basis Artifact references; expiry; nullable monotonic revocation-receipt reference. Remit states allowed proposition family, profile, environment, and scope. Method identifies code/prompt/version and actual qualification evidence. A separately commissioned administrator provisions it; ordinary authoring cannot create, broaden, renew, or revoke it. Renewal creates a new capability. Revocation retains an immutable reason/basis record. Tokens never enter model prompts, Artifact content, or logs.

A reviewer capability authenticates the submitted producer/method remit, not competence, truth, action authority, or the adequacy of a particular conclusion. Provisioning is technical delegation under an existing commission, not a new constitutional authority root. A governing method may permit one participant in several roles while keeping their distinct bases inspectable; no universal two-person gate is imposed.

### 5.2 Exact submitted text and server-issued envelope

Retain the installed immutable text Artifact table unchanged. Each committed role record is one server-generated UTF-8 JSON envelope stored exactly as an Artifact. It contains the exact submitted payload text as a string (when there was a submission), its parsed interpretation/version, resolved/generated Referent IDs, authenticated attribution, and operation-result manifest. Submitted text is recovered by decoding that string, not by pretty-printing the parsed object. No externally provided producer identity is silently upgraded to authenticated identity.

This explicit envelope solves result recovery: server-assigned IDs, role validation, and any generated Claim/Evidence Link references are preserved in the same result Artifact before commit. The client need not guess IDs or reconstruct them from a lost response. `ordinary_operations.result_referent_id` and the protected binding identify that exact result. The request digest binds the original request, not nondeterministic server timestamps/UUIDs. On replay the server returns the existing Artifact without regenerating it.

Raw source bytes may instead already exist in an ordinary immutable Artifact, referenced by the envelope. No automatic extra copy is required. For non-text sources this design preserves a declared textual extract with its exact limits or an exact accessible source reference; it does not invent binary Artifact support. A use requiring unavailable exact binary history must be held for a newly earned physical extension.

An Artifact revision is another Referent-backed Artifact. There is no generic artifact key, latest lookup, native semantic version family, or native supersedes column. `profile_version` versions the parser contract, not semantic standing. Original source identity, source version, representation Artifact identity, operation identity, and inquiry-channel identity remain distinct.

### 5.3 Historical semantic payload

Snapshot decisive content whose historical meaning would otherwise be lost: original source locator/Referent, exact version when available, captured content or bounded extract, access method, observation/capture times, standing at capture, and omissions. Relevant mutable Claim wording/scope and standing-event basis are included when needed for later interpretation. Prefer references to already immutable exact content over duplicate copies.

The database is authoritative for the committed bytes/control records it retains. Original governing sources remain authoritative for their own decisions. A snapshot establishes what was retained, not source authenticity, current source standing, completeness, or truth. A digest detects mismatch but cannot recover absent old meaning. Missing decisive historical content yields an explicit loss/narrowed claim, never reconstruction from the current row or memory.

### 5.4 Claim and evidence extensions

Preserve the installed assertion/relation split and `origin='ecb_inference'`. Raw source custody does not automatically become an inference Claim. For an attributed statement, distinguish a Claim that the source reports P from a Claim asserting P.

Add only these relation predicates when semantic lineage is asserted:

| Predicate | Scope-preserving meaning |
|---|---|
| `quad_revises` | The subject representation explicitly revises the identified predecessor in a declared scope; not automatic acceptance/invalidation |
| `quad_refocuses_from` | The subject inquiry explicitly changes focal subject from the identified prior inquiry; not ordinary same-R traversal |
| `quad_refines_question` | The subject question representation clarifies/decomposes the original, exposing transformation and any changed burden |

They remain unassessed until warranted through the appropriate standing route. Existing `depends_on` is used only for actual asserted dependency. Payload inclusion and transactional foreign keys are structural references, not an alternative semantic relation truth store. A relied-upon semantic relation names its Claim ID.

Extend Evidence Link validation with `ecb_text_artifact_v1_sha256`, using domain-separated, length-framed SHA-256 of exact UTF-8 Artifact content. Evidence Referent identity remains separately bound; equal bytes do not prove identical source origin. Native type and full payload availability must be checked. Preserve the original Thought scheme unchanged. **Both `prepare_evidence_link` and `prepare_claim_standing_transition` must dispatch through the same scheme-aware resolver**; adding Artifact evidence only at insertion leaves an invalid standing-transition path.

Every independently referenced persistent first-class question, Claim, Artifact, operation, channel, or capability has Referent identity. Embedded list entries may use `(artifact_id, local_handle)` until independent first-class treatment is actually required; this is not one-ID-per-word or automatic global co-reference.

## 6. Payload profiles and use manifests

The envelope profile is `ecb.quadrant/1`. Validation rejects duplicate JSON object keys, invalid UTF-8, unsupported versions, duplicate local handles, missing required structural references, and wrong native types. It never silently discards surplus content to make the input pass. Exact submitted text is preserved as described in 5.2; derived interpretation is explicitly distinguished.

| Role | Required recoverable payload meaning |
|---|---|
| `source_snapshot` | Original source identity/version/content/access/standing and extract limits |
| `basis` | R/map/mapper, identity uncertainty, boundary/rationale, constituents/co-relata, open-context relevance, exact G/use/time/state/standing, stop/reentry |
| `inquiry` | Original/refined question references and relation Claims, seat/burden, four coverage meanings, role-specific warrant/shared origin, limits, QF |
| `assessment` | Proposition, exact input/use manifest, method/version, declared and authenticated producer/checker, evidence/controls/reasons/result/standing/limits/correction |
| `change` | Before/after exact basis, surfaced change, classification, affected scope, justified unaffected use, unknown impact, required reassessment |
| `selection` | Exact operational basis/inquiry/change choices, predecessor receipt, expected/result epoch, relied-upon semantic lineage Claims |
| `reliance` | Requested and permitted use, exact applicable basis/fences/assessments, QF, overall disposition/reasons, separate support/authority findings, reentry, handoff closure |
| `reviewer_revocation` | Capability/remit, observed reason/basis/time, affected assessment reentry; no deletion of earlier judgments |

Coverage meanings use working tokens `unconsidered`, `unresolved`, `deferred_for_this_use`, `answered_with_warrant`. Their presence does not prove their adequacy. Overall use disposition is `supported`, `narrowed`, `suspended`, or `indeterminate`. A narrowed result states a distinct permitted use; the original broader request is not approved.

The applicable-basis manifest names exact Artifact/Claim/receipt IDs, their role/scope, source standing or historical snapshot, and currentness mode. `historical_exact` retains evidence under its original scope. `must_revalidate` requires a present source/fence observation adequate for the declared use. The manifest includes channel and relevant standing/reviewer fence values; one timestamp or global current flag is insufficient. A claimed equivalent basis requires a scoped assessment connecting both exact versions and the intended use.

QF entries contain calibrated question, why under G, contrasting findings and consequences, discriminator, actual route/reference, present restriction, permitted interim use, and reentry. Route types are `on_use`, `explicit_evidence_return`, and `no_present_route`. A desired future route is not an available signal or promise.

## 7. Concrete interfaces and ownership

These are proposed interface families, not installed tools. Use the existing runtime and versioned guarded RPC wrappers named `quadrant_v1_*`, with fully qualified private implementation routines. No arbitrary SQL, caller-selected trusted-result bit, or effect dispatch is exposed.

| Interface | Producer/consumer and inputs | Output, pre/postconditions, and standing |
|---|---|---|
| `quadrant_record` | Contributor/evidence-return host; stable operation UUID, role, exact submitted text, declared reference set, channel/new-channel instruction, expected epoch when selecting/changing | One immutable issued envelope and ordinary-operation receipt, optionally selecting a head. Custody/profile validation does not qualify semantic use |
| `quadrant_resolve` | Authorized reader/qualifier; exact receipt or Artifact IDs and requested closure, or channel to locate operational head | Pure read of payloads, bindings, reference availability, history, and observed fences; no embedding repair, model call, latest-as-valid fallback, or auto-qualification |
| `quadrant_assess` | Commissioned reviewer host or bounded local deterministic evaluator; stable operation UUID, authenticated method/remit, exact proposition/input/use manifest, output/reasons/controls/limits | Immutable assessment plus protected producer/method binding. Authentication records whose method submitted it, not universal correctness |
| `quadrant_qualify` | Consumer requesting qualified use; operation UUID, exact requested use, applicable-basis manifest, expected fence vector, assessment/change/current-source references | Immutable overall supported/narrowed/suspended/indeterminate disposition with handoff closure. Structural issuance conditions are checked in the database; no action executes |

### 7.1 Role and trust separation

`quadrant_record` may bind only source_snapshot, basis, inquiry, change, and selection. It cannot bind assessment, reliance, or reviewer_revocation, even with perfectly shaped JSON. Only `quadrant_assess` binds assessment; only `quadrant_qualify` binds reliance; a separately commissioned private administration path binds revocation. Enforce these allowlists in SQL as well as the server.

Reviewer authentication is an out-of-band host/transport credential, not a model-visible argument. A nonsecret capability ID may identify the remit; possession must be checked using a separate bounded credential that ordinary brain/custody access does not supply. The host may not convert an arbitrary model-selected method ID into access to a server-held credential. A local deterministic method issues only the result it actually computes within its provisioned remit. An external semantic host attests the observed method/input/output and its limits, not an invented checker execution. Both use the same scoped assessment-binding contract; no new model provider is required.

Profile validity, identity/native type, exact references, operation-kind/digest consistency, expected fences, and authenticated role are write preconditions. Required semantic adequacy is not inferred from them. The database independently validates protected envelope/profile fields, role allowlists, exact dependencies, and gate conditions before creating a binding. Duplicate-key rejection must occur before conversion to a representation that discards duplicate keys, including at direct-RPC entry; server-only rejection is insufficient.

### 7.2 Atomic result and errors

The payload envelope, protected binding, any new scoped Claims/Evidence Links, ordinary-operation result, and any head update commit together or not at all. Server-assigned IDs are recorded in the result envelope, so a lost response does not strand outputs. Internal helpers reuse ordinary-operation patterns inside the outer transaction; do not invoke an independently committing Artifact operation with the same outer UUID.

Errors distinguish `operation_conflict`, `revision_conflict`, `unsupported_profile`, `input_limit`, `wrong_native_type`, `reference_unavailable`, `historical_payload_missing`, `unsupported_assessment`, `scope_incompatible`, `authority_unresolved`, `reviewer_unavailable_or_revoked`, and `persistence_unavailable`. A transient database abort reports retryable transaction failure, not a committed negative result. A negative disposition counts as committed only when an ordinary receipt and exact result exist.

Replay unchanged committed requests for result recovery under current read/recovery authorization. Changing an input requires a new operation UUID. Recovery does not renew the original result's eligibility. A revoked reviewer credential cannot create a new assessment or gain access merely by requesting replay; an independently authorized reader can still recover historical records. Reentry repairs the source/warrant or reruns affected C4/C5, not overwrites the original result.

## 8. Concurrency, idempotency, and ordering

Select PostgreSQL **READ COMMITTED plus explicit locks and compare-and-swap**, not an unspecified choice between event sourcing and queues. Reuse `ordinary_operations`; create no second operation ledger.

The write protocol is:

1. Validate bounded input/profile and collect the declared reference/fence set. External source reads or cognition occur outside database locks and return pinned evidence, not an already-completed transition.
2. Authenticate the applicable transport/remit. Acquire the existing ordinary-operation advisory transaction lock keyed from the operation UUID. Bind operation kind and a domain-separated length-framed digest of all semantic request inputs, including exact submitted bytes, channel, reference/fence set, method identity, and use. Exclude secret credential bytes.
3. An existing matching operation returns its original immutable result with replay status if the caller retains appropriate access. Changed kind/inputs conflict. This does not issue a fresh current-use approval.
4. For a new operation, lock all declared channels in stable UUID order. Lock required current reviewer/standing state and mutable source rows using a documented compatible order; use the strongest needed row lock from the start. Re-read protected values after obtaining locks. Current standing is fenced through the corresponding current native state, not by locking only an old immutable event.
5. Verify expected epochs, role/native types, complete exact closure, and required current-state observations. If discovering a new dependency expands the lock set, abort and replan rather than acquire an inconsistent order. Immutable Artifact content needs no invented mutable version head.
6. Evaluate the structurally decidable issuance conditions and applicable scoped assessment references. Atomically create the envelope/native bindings/relations/ordinary result and any selected successor. The qualifier alone creates a supported/narrowed use result.
7. Commit and return exact receipt/result IDs. No implicit provider dispatch or external effect follows.

Same operation plus different JSON whitespace is a different byte request and conflicts; clients retain original request bytes. Semantic equivalence is a separate warranted judgment, not transport idempotency. Unique keys and same-channel head constraints enforce one operation result and one structural head.

Two concurrent writers selecting from the same expected epoch cannot both advance that head. The first commit determines operational selection, not semantic superiority. The loser can preserve its candidate through a distinct custody-only operation and pursue explicit reconciliation. There is no automatic semantic last-write-wins merge.

A surfaced material or possibly material change increments the fence before complete impact analysis, preventing blind reuse. This does not invalidate all historical meaning or every use: a scoped C4 assessment can demonstrate unaffected applicability and a new C5 result can permit that use. A reliance result issued just before a later change remains historical at its bound epoch; it is not a perpetual token.

Deadlock/lock-timeout or serialization-style aborts are recoverable transaction failures. Retry the entire unchanged request with the same operation ID and bounded caller-controlled backoff; do not retry only the failed statement, change expected epochs silently, or amplify evidence. If the first attempt actually committed, replay recovers it. Lock ordering lowers risk but is not a claim that interactions with every existing routine are deadlock-free; S1/S3 must test those interactions.

Shared sources are bound by exact source snapshots and explicit current-observation obligations, not by locking every inquiry that ever mentioned them. Known affected channels can be named and locked; unrecorded dependencies and the external world are not atomically observed. Unknown decisive present standing leads to a restricted/indeterminate use, not fabricated currentness.

Implementation references for the selected SQL primitives: PostgreSQL 17 documentation, `https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/explicit-locking.html` and `https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/transaction-iso.html`. These support database lock/isolation semantics, not semantic adequacy or tested correctness of this proposed module.

## 9. Judgment, reliance enforcement, authority, and security

### 9.1 Judgment allocation and evidence

Mechanically decidable relations are checked by callable code. Boundary adequacy, unrestricted question interpretation, source support, premise completeness, joint G/scope compatibility, consequentiality, and sufficiency remain semantic/hybrid. The producer is a commissioned cognition worker in an authorized host, human or model; no particular provider, mandatory human gate, or universal two-reviewer process is selected.

An assessment identifies its proposition; exact input/output and intended use; source origins; method/prompt/code revision; worker/checker with declared versus authenticated attribution; observed findings and reasons; discriminating negative control or equivalent method evidence; result/standing; limits; and correction/reentry. Method evidence can be reused only for its actual version/proposition family. It is not a demand for a new empirical experiment for every ordinary judgment, nor permission to claim an unrun checker succeeded.

Method qualification bottoms out in an explicit governing remit and inspectable bounded evidence, not an infinite automatic reviewer-of-reviewer chain. A credential alone does not qualify the method. Unknown/failed qualification prevents positive claims dependent on it while still allowing custody and negative/indeterminate observations. Independence is required where the governing use/method requires it; the same actor can fill multiple roles only with distinct bases and limits exposed.

Deterministic validators can reject wrong inputs, unauthorized producer, missing/failed required controls, duplicated declared source origins offered as independent, and mismatched use. They cannot establish that fluent reasoning is sound. A well-formed but unsupported argument may need semantic evaluation to detect. Dishonest, correlated, or mistaken reviewer error is an explicit residual; the design does not call authentication or JSON completeness semantic proof.

### 9.2 Protected use-level issuance

The mechanically protected condition for positive issuance is:

`exact_typed_closure AND use_binding_matches AND required_fences_match AND scoped_qualified_assessments_present AND no_known_decisive_gap AND authority_disposition_consistent`

This includes references to semantic findings; it is not a database truth oracle. The qualifier requires assessment of C1 joint applicability, C2 coverage, C3 warrant, C4 surfaced-change/currentness, material QF, and C5 sufficiency for that use. A no-change case carries an explicit no-surfaced-change statement with observation limits, not a universal assurance.

SQL independently verifies exact references, native types, protected assessment bindings, method/remit and qualification evidence references, expiry/revocation, expected fences, required findings, and requested/permitted-use consistency. The semantic assessment of whether the use requires authority is itself exactly bound and qualified; callers cannot bypass it by setting an `authority_required=false` flag.

A reliance record separates the **overall requested-use disposition**, evidential-support findings, and authority findings. Adequate evidence with unresolved required authority is not an overall supported action. Narrowing explicitly states the permitted analysis/trial/etc. and excludes the broader request. The protected result records the validator revision and exact assessments that led to it.

An ordinary Artifact containing the words PASS or supported is only text. A verified module-issued binding establishes origin of the disposition; present eligibility additionally requires a new current-use check when relied upon. Direct raw fetching remains evidence access. The module cannot prevent a human misreading downloaded text and does not claim to intercept arbitrary external behavior. Any later activated machine consequential transition must use a current gate plus independently required authority, not an old receipt or agent memory.

### 9.3 Separately closed effect authority

This design activates no external-effect transition. The selected real-action authorization/execution adapter is explicitly **NONE / CLOSED**. An execution request cannot be dispatched or converted into permission by current ACTION/HOLD, runtime access, a reviewer capability, or the presence of BUILD 6 tables. Governing sources may be recovered and assessed for the declared nonexecuting use; that assessment does not create external authority.

A use whose permissibility depends on an unavailable authority path is suspended/indeterminate, even if the evidence supports a narrower interpretation. Custody and permitted analysis remain available. A later authority/effect installation requires its own commission and live source reconciliation, specifying actor/operation/target/remit, governing basis, revocation/expiry, and the relevant effect atomicity. No uninstalled BUILD 7-10 capability is treated as available to close this design gate.

### 9.4 Security boundary

Keep new tables private. Deny direct runtime DML and immutable-record UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE; do not rely on RLS alone. Guarded SECURITY DEFINER routines use fully qualified objects and a fixed safe search path, with explicit EXECUTE grants and no public helper bypass. Revoke default public execution of private helpers in the same migration transaction that creates them. Avoid privilege expansion of the existing ordinary key.

Use separate bounded reviewer credentials only after authorized provisioning. Payload text is data, not executable SQL, prompt authority, or instructions to connected tools. Parameterize database calls and redact secrets from errors/receipts. Bound request bytes and closure expansion; exceedance is explicit `input_limit`, never silent omission or fabricated completeness. An initial disposable test profile may cap one payload at 256 KiB and closure at 256 references; these are provisional engineering limits, not measured production sizing or ontology limits. Traversal tracks visited IDs, exposes cycles, and does not infer semantic invalidity from a cycle alone.

Provider administrators/superusers remain outside application-level immutability. Privileged destruction or unavailable backup is evidence loss; a checksum cannot recover the content. Backup/recovery service levels, performance, and provider reliability need later operational evidence. No secret, account, provider, production grant, or configuration is installed by this specification.

## 10. Change, QF, and cold recovery flows

On use, explicit evidence return, or contributor notice, preserve before/after inputs and classify enrichment, refocus, boundary revision, changed G/use/time/state, source/warrant standing, clarification/decomposition, or uncertain classification. Record a possible consequential notice and increment its channel fence without waiting for complete semantic localization. Reconcile affected claims/uses or the smallest warranted conservative scope; unknown impact is explicit, and unaffected use requires reasons. Requalify only affected obligations, then issue a new C5 disposition for the intended use. Do not delete or relabel old results as current.

Refocus opens a linked channel. A changed boundary for the same R revises its basis rather than silently assigning a new subject. Discovery may enrich the account under its existing criterion, challenge that criterion, or affect prior answers; no global graph expansion follows automatically.

`on_use` means the qualifier checks declared QF and present-source obligations when use is requested. `explicit_evidence_return` means an actor supplies a real source/admission receipt and branch reference to `quadrant_record`; the notice is then selected/fenced. Merely capturing a Thought or putting an issue number in producer_context does not automatically deliver evidence to this inquiry. There is no global router or promised idle-time observation.

`no_present_route` preserves the unresolved question and why available evidence cannot distinguish its alternatives. If decisive, it narrows/suspends dependent use, not every unrelated use. A new requirement for guaranteed idle-time notice routes to ECO-146/Principal before adding a scheduler.

Starting from a channel or exact reliance receipt, the pure resolver recovers selected inputs, exact immutable semantic closure, source/Claim/standing evidence, relevant changes, QF, method, use, and limitations. Missing decisive content yields incomplete closure and restricted use; no conversation or model memory repairs it. Handoff contains that closure or exact accessible references, permitted use, unresolved QF, standing/currentness limits, and reentry. Changed use re-enters C4/C5; equivalent basis requires a scoped assessment, not equal digests or successful retrieval.
