## 0. Episode result

[ECO-191](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-191/sense-formalization-sigma-recursive-referent-system-derive-minimal) began by asking whether already-qualified SIGMA/ECOS behavior around referent reseating, self-application, PGO, SSMM, currentness, dependency and reentry could be represented by a compact formal core that produced real constraint/checker leverage rather than decorative notation.

The episode now closes with a bounded Register-B contract:

> **When a decision-bearing seat or basis materially changes, preserve the source exactly, declare the change, transport only explicitly bridged material, separately recheck affected old support, expose destination-specific obligations, and independently verify current-use legality/authority before positive reliance.**

The central practical correction is that “does the old PASS still hold?” is under-specified. A lawful system must ask at least two different questions:

1. **What support used by the old conclusion was affected by this change?**
2. **What requirements are now active at the destination/use that the old qualification never had to answer?**

Only then can current-use legality, currentness and authority be checked.

This behavior survived the recovered fixtures and a fresh bounded structural checker audit.

## 1. Standing changes produced by Shape + qualification

### Promoted from Sense candidate to Register-B Shape contract

* the partial indexed engagement state remains the governing inspection grammar;
* q is decision-bearing and must remain explicitly recoverable;
* material change is a typed guarded transition with declared write set and retained predecessor/history;
* referent continuity must distinguish same Referent/revised account, different existing Referent and newly individuated composite;
* historical assertions remain bound to their original subject/index;
* prior material may be retrieved or bridged without thereby becoming current destination warrant;
* old-support review and new-destination coverage are separate mandatory reconciliation duties;
* blocking unknown destination obligations fence positive reliance;
* currentness, SSMM legality and authority remain independent gates;
* SelfFocus uses the same referent grammar and does not self-authorize;
* bootstrap may preserve alternatives or HOLD rather than fabricate a unique warranted orientation;
* deterministic checking should enforce exact bindings and declared liabilities without pretending to perform semantic discovery.

### Sharpened during Shape

The strongest refinement came from reconciling [ECO-192](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-192/probe-translation-frontier-fiberr-indexed-semantics-test-lawful) with [ECO-189](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-189/architecture-shape-tri-axial-coordination-realization-canonical-brain):

> **Pure matched-record transport is a valid bounded operation, but full lawful reseating is not reducible to transport.**

This prevents two opposite errors at once:

* discarding all prior structure whenever the seat changes; and
* keeping old qualification merely because records or IDs can be carried forward.

[ECO-191](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-191/sense-formalization-sigma-recursive-referent-system-derive-minimal) also adopts [ECO-189](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-189/architecture-shape-tri-axial-coordination-realization-canonical-brain)'s preservation/loss accounting and dependency-local requalification posture, while keeping external reality distinct from canonical ECOS observations and reliance records.

### Explicitly not promoted

The episode does not establish:

* a globally minimal formal axiom basis;
* universal semantic completeness;
* an exhaustive requirement-discovery algorithm;
* arbitrary-depth/concurrent recursion qualification;
* universal transport functoriality for semantic judgments;
* a canonical category-theoretic ontology for SIGMA;

These remain hypotheses, later Move questions or Register-A research.

## 2. Qualification evidence assimilated

The bounded qualification did not merely replay the prose. It tested the shaped failure boundaries.

The contract correctly rejected:

* same-looking PASS under a changed exact question/use/basis;
* old load evidence as sufficient for a destination permission obligation;
* parent continuation when an independent dependency changed despite child PASS;
* currentness restoration from endpoint equality after intervening revoke/restore history;
* authored self-PASS as authority;
* blocking UNKNOWN as satisfaction;
* bare NOT_APPLICABLE without an applicability basis.

It correctly preserved:

* old source claims/history when destination reliance failed;
* process identity and parent/child nesting when focal scale role changed;
* the ability to transport frozen matched historical records compositionally;
* unresolved bootstrap alternatives when no warranted unique selector existed.

The finite guard model again produced one positive reliance assignment out of 64: the case where every required independent guard was true. This is an illustration of the contract's conjunction, not a claim that six Boolean fields exhaust real semantics.

No fixture forced reopening of the Sense laws or [ECO-189](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-189/architecture-shape-tri-axial-coordination-realization-canonical-brain) architecture.

## 3. Reconciliation with the current ECOS architecture

[ECO-191](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-191/sense-formalization-sigma-recursive-referent-system-derive-minimal) and [ECO-189](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-189/architecture-shape-tri-axial-coordination-realization-canonical-brain) now divide responsibility cleanly.

[ECO-191](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-191/sense-formalization-sigma-recursive-referent-system-derive-minimal)**:** semantics and proof obligations of material indexed change/reseating/requalification.

[ECO-189](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-189/architecture-shape-tri-axial-coordination-realization-canonical-brain)**:** canonical custody, reality-contact/reliance semantics, reconstitution and realization boundaries.

Together they imply the following implementation requirement:

> A material-change receipt that becomes decision-bearing must be recoverable from the canonical ECOS lineage with enough exact source, dependency, transformation, coverage, currentness and authority information to reconstruct why the present reliance is or is not allowed.

This does not require one monolithic “transition object” or “coordination object.” The logical receipt may be represented across typed canonical constituents so long as the full contract is reconstructible and no replaceable external surface becomes the sole keeper of a governing commitment.
