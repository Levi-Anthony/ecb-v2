## 0. What would physically change if this contract were installed

A material change of focal Referent, boundary, frame, PGO, question, episode/basis, use, authority or other decision-bearing basis would no longer be allowed to carry an old PASS/current qualification forward by resemblance, shared labels, or endpoint equality.

Instead, before positive reliance, ECOS would emit one attributable change receipt that:

1. preserves the exact source index, source claims and history;
2. declares the proposed transition and its write set;
3. states what kind of continuity exists between source and destination;
4. transports only explicitly matched prior material, without promoting retrieval into current qualification;
5. rechecks old support affected by the change;
6. discloses requirements that become active at the destination;
7. records coverage, including decisive unknowns;
8. independently checks currentness, phase legality and authority for the intended use; and
9. returns a bounded reliance result plus reasons, Question Forward and reentry route.

The contract does not make semantic discovery mechanical and does not select a database schema. It defines the behavior a physical realization must preserve.

## 1. Selected Shape

The selected architecture is a **guarded indexed-change and reconciliation contract** over the already-earned partial-state grammar.

The central Shape decision is:

> **A complete lawful reseating/change event is not one transport operation. Transport is a bounded sub-operation inside a larger guarded transition that also performs destination-sensitive disclosure, requalification and current-use reconciliation.**

This preserves [ECO-192](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-192/probe-translation-frontier-fiberr-indexed-semantics-test-lawful)'s useful indexed-transport result without forcing full SIGMA semantics into a stronger mathematical correspondence than the evidence supports.

A material transition therefore has the logical form:

**MaterialChange = Transition declaration + Source retention + Optional qualified transport + Affected-old review + Destination disclosure + Coverage + Current-use reconciliation + Receipt**

No term may silently stand in for another.

## 2. State and indexing surfaces

[ECO-191](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-191/sense-formalization-sigma-recursive-referent-system-derive-minimal) Sense carries forward the inspection state:

**S = (K, H; r, b, f, g, q, t, u; e, w, a, z)**

where K is the recoverable partial account, H the typed lineage, r focal Referent, b boundary/grain/scope, f mapper/frame/access, g PGO, q the present question/answer burden, t SSMM episode/phase/return basis, u declared use/consumer/environment, e evidence, w assessment/warrant, a actor/custody/authority and z unresolved apertures/QF/reentry.

Shape introduces no new ontology. It names two useful projections.

### Engagement index

**ι(S) = (r, b, f, g, q, t, u)**

READ: the engagement index says exactly what is being addressed, from what boundary/frame, under what governing orientation and question, in what episode, and for what use.

OPERATIONALLY: two equal-looking PASS payloads are not interchangeable when a decision-bearing part of this index differs.

### Qualification basis

For a proposition p used for u, **β(p,u)** is the exact evidence, assessed inputs, method/warrant, source editions, relevant lineage/currentness and authority/authorization basis actually required for that reliance.

READ: the qualification basis records why this particular conclusion was acceptable for this particular use.

OPERATIONALLY: returning to the same displayed seat or text does not restore an earlier qualification if its history, evidence or authority basis changed.

### Disclosure interface

**Φ(S) = Disclose(K,e,z | ι(S))**

Φ remains an open-world semantic task interface. It returns a partial account of relevant relations, dependencies, requirements and unresolved discovery obligations. Its output is not asserted to exhaust reality.

PGO constrains what is consequential for the declared outcome; it does not create or erase existence.

## 3. Composite transition receipt

Every material indexed change must produce a receipt with the following logically distinct sections.

| Receipt section | Minimum content | What it prevents |
| -- | -- | -- |
| 1. Transition declaration | operation identity; source index/current binding; proposed target; reason; declared write set; actor/authority basis | silent mutation and endpoint-only reasoning |
| 2. Continuity decision | same Referent/revised boundary-account; different existing Referent; or newly individuated composite; exact bridge/formation basis | identity-by-label and subject substitution |
| 3. Source retention | exact source claims, support, versions, lineage and historical standing | rewriting old assertions to fit the destination |
| 4. Transport/bridge ledger | exact source slot/item; destination slot; comparison/bridge basis; transformation/loss class; transport status | treating copied/retrieved material as current warrant |
| 5. Affected-old review | changed premises; dependency-local impact; unchanged/affected/unknown disposition; requalification result | global invalidation and old-graph false confidence |
| 6. Destination disclosure | disclosure method/version; destination index/use; newly active requirements; material unknowns; bounded sufficiency claim | assuming the source graph enumerated destination obligations |
| 7. Coverage ledger | requirement; blocking status; SATISFIED / UNSATISFIED / UNKNOWN / NOT_APPLICABLE; exact basis; QF/reentry | decisive gaps hidden behind an old PASS |
| 8. Current-use fidelity | exact index/basis match; currentness; SSMM legality; authority/authorization; consumer/environment where consequential | PASS → permission, phase → authority, historical → current collapse |
| 9. Reconciliation return | reliance eligibility; typed reasons; remaining apertures; permitted next transition(s); receipt identity | a generic checker inventing parent routing policy |

[ECO-189](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-189/architecture-shape-tri-axial-coordination-realization-canonical-brain) loss-accounting vocabulary is reused where useful: **PRESERVED, DERIVED / REFORMULATED, ADDED, STRIPPED / REJECTED, DEFERRED**.

The transport status must additionally distinguish:

* **HISTORICAL RETRIEVAL** — source material has been recovered at the destination view;
* **PROPOSED REUSE** — a bridge has been supplied but current applicability is not yet established;
* **QUALIFIED DESTINATION REUSE** — the relevant destination checks have passed.

These statuses are intentionally not synonyms.

## 4. Continuity and transport rules

### Same Referent, revised boundary/account

Referent identity is retained. The old claim remains indexed to its old boundary/account. Boundary-dependent relations, scale roles and applicability are redisclosed. No qualification automatically migrates merely because r is stable.

### Different existing Referent

Both identities remain intact. An assertion about the source Referent remains about the source Referent. Cross-seat reuse requires an explicit typed relation/bridge and destination assessment. Subject substitution is forbidden.

### Newly individuated composite

Constituent identities and formation lineage remain recoverable. The composite must have an explicit organizing/identity criterion sufficient for the current semantic resolution. Formation does not automatically establish a constitutional Level, a physical row, truth, authority or focus.

### Pure transport law

For an explicitly frozen, qualified slot comparison, matched historical record transport may obey ordinary identity/composition laws. This is a useful checker property.

It does **not** follow that complete operational reseating commutes or composes the same way. PGO enrichment, discovery, currentness, authority, requirement exposure and requalification can make order matter.

## 5. Reconciliation law after Shape

The Sense law is retained and sharpened:

**Reconcile(S → S', u) = CheckAffected(Affected_old(Δ)) AND CheckCoverage(Req(S',u), Accounted(S',u))**

Shape adds an independently inspectable current-use gate:

**RelianceOK(S → S', p, u) = TransitionWellFormed AND CheckAffected AND CheckCoverage AND CurrentUseOK**

where CurrentUseOK checks the exact basis required for the declared effect, including currentness, applicable SSMM transition legality and scoped authority/authorization where consequential.

This is a necessary-condition contract at the bounded reliance surface. It is not a theorem that the declared requirements are globally complete.

### Coverage semantics

For a blocking destination requirement:

* SATISFIED may pass if its exact basis is valid.
* UNSATISFIED blocks positive reliance.
* UNKNOWN blocks positive reliance and must expose a QF/reentry route.
* NOT_APPLICABLE may pass only with an explicit applicability basis; absence is not non-applicability.

A nonblocking unknown may remain open if the current PGO/use explicitly permits proceeding without it.

The checker may verify coverage of the **declared** requirement set. It may not claim that no undiscovered material requirement exists merely because every declared row is green.

## 6. Semantic judgment versus mechanical enforcement

### Semantic / judgmental responsibilities

Keep these semantic unless later evidence earns stronger physicalization:

* identifying or individuating the consequential Referent;
* deciding whether a boundary, frame or continuity relation is adequate;
* PGO formation/enrichment;
* question applicability and answer burden;
* deciding whether an evidence/claim bridge is substantively valid;
* destination requirement discovery and materiality;
* assessing evidence applicability under changed context;
* deciding what parent routing consequence follows from a substantive result;
* forming new Questions Forward.

### Mechanically enforceable liabilities

A physical checker may enforce:

* exact identifiers, editions, hashes/digests and source bindings;
* declared write-set discipline;
* predecessor/history retention;
* explicit continuity-mode declaration;
* bridge/slot attribution for transported records;
* separation of historical retrieval, proposed reuse and qualified reuse;
* dependency-local stale flags over declared dependencies;
* coverage-row completeness for the declared requirement set;
* blocking UNKNOWN/UNSATISFIED behavior;
* explicit basis for NOT_APPLICABLE;
* exact question/use binding;
* currentness and phase legality checks where rules are deterministic;
* authority/authorization presence and scope where technically decidable;
* loss/addition accounting;
* idempotent operation identity and exact receipts.

The system should physically prevent malformed bookkeeping while preserving semantic freedom where interpretation remains load-bearing.

## 7. Operator integration

The Shape does not replace the Sense operator inventory. It supplies the common change/reconciliation behavior beneath it.

* **Seat / Reseat:** full composite receipt; redisclosure mandatory.
* **ChangeFrame:** preserve r/b/g unless separately changed; requalify frame-dependent evidence and claims; redisclose when consequential.
* **Reorient:** preserve world-claims/identity; redisclose requirements/relevance under the new PGO; requalify use.
* **Enrich:** retain seat unless boundary criterion is violated; recheck affected warrant and redisclose only if the new material changes consequential requirements.
* **Return / Reenter:** exact parent/dependency/question/basis binding; returned success addresses only the bound dependency; current parent conditions are rechecked.
* **Requalify:** creates a new bounded assessment; never rewrites the old one.
* **Bind:** acts only after exact current qualification plus independent bind authority; no truth mutation.
* **Project:** read-only/loss-declared representation; projection cannot become authority by being convenient.
* **SelfFocus:** invokes ordinary Reseat on the structure itself and therefore uses this same receipt.
* **Bootstrap:** uses the guarded special entry behavior in §9 below and does not fabricate a completed index.

A compound operation retains the receipts of its component changes. Convenience may package them, but packaging must not erase distinct effects.

## 8. Self-application contract

For an addressable structure x:

**SelfFocus(S,x,j) = Reseat(S, seat(ref(x)), j)**

where ref(x) designates x itself, not the thing x represents.

When this [ECO-191](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-191/sense-formalization-sigma-recursive-referent-system-derive-minimal) report becomes R, its authored PASS text is a claim contained in the artifact. It is not evidence that the report was accepted, installed, authoritative or correct.

If the parent actually depends on x, bounded recursive inquiry binds:

* exact parent;
* exact dependency;
* x and its version;
* child question and criteria;
* remit/authority;
* return location.

A child return updates only the named dependency. Parent reentry then checks independent dependencies, currentness and applicability. This preserves BUILD 9's load-bearing behavior without granting a general self-certification or self-amendment right.

## 9. Bootstrap contract

Bootstrap remains a guarded relation rather than a guaranteed convergent function.

A declared bootstrap kernel/remit may:

* recover an existing orientation;
* preserve candidate Referents/PGOs;
* make attributed provisional designations;
* ask a discriminating question;
* acquire missing access/evidence;
* return READY for a declared bounded next inquiry; or
* return HOLD with QF/reentry.

It may not invent semantic facts, binding authority or a unique warranted selection from perfectly symmetric insufficient input.

Therefore a content-neutral deterministic selector is not required to return one candidate when the evidence supports several. Preserving the candidate set is lawful.

## 12. Falsifiers and reentry triggers

Reenter Shape if implementation evidence shows any of the following:

1. a legitimate material change cannot be represented without erasing stable source identity/history;
2. a required current destination qualification can lawfully transfer with no attributable bridge, disclosure or applicability basis in a way this contract forbids;
3. destination requirement discovery cannot expose a bounded sufficiency claim or material unknowns usable by a human/agent checker;
4. dependency-local requalification systematically misses required impacts even when declared dependencies are accurate;
5. SelfFocus requires a separate meta-grammar to preserve representation/subject/authority distinctions;
6. the receipt model forces semantic completeness claims merely to operate;
7. the bookkeeping burden materially exceeds its discriminating value and a simpler representation preserves every tested anti-collapse property;
8. physical realization exposes an upstream contradiction with [ECO-189](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-189/architecture-shape-tri-axial-coordination-realization-canonical-brain)'s canonical-state/reality-contact contract.

Ordinary schema inconvenience is not an upstream semantic defect.
