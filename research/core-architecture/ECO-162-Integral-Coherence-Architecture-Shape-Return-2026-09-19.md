## 1. Shape disposition

The strongest architecture is **tri-axial with a cross-cutting fidelity contract**.

This is deliberately not a “three primitive ontology.”

The three macro axes are irreducible families of characteristic work:

1. **Semantic / Open-World Axis** — what may be coherently represented, related, situated, and kept incomplete without a closed domain ontology. Current URG work is the principal architectural lineage.
2. **Governing / Telic Axis** — what is consequential for the present engagement, why it matters, what counts as sufficient/failure, and what conditions require reconsideration. Master-Key / Governing-Orientation lineage is the principal source.
3. **Temporal / Coordination Axis** — how standing may legitimately change through causal time: phase, branch, suspension, return, binding/currentness transition, requalification, and Metabolize. SSMM is the principal lineage.

The axes are not exclusive modules and do not own every concern that touches them.

Faithful operation additionally requires a **cross-cutting fidelity contract** preserving independent coordinates, standing, lineage, authority, and apertures across axis interactions.

The selected architecture therefore is:

> **Three irreducible macro dimensions + independently variable fidelity coordinates + typed transitions + composable projections.**

### Why not a fourth “context/fidelity” macro axis?

Frame, focal seat, warrant, authority, worker identity, and QF state can vary independently and are load-bearing. But they do not collectively form one coherent fourth characteristic-work family. They qualify mappings, reliance, authority, and transfer *across* the three macro axes.

Promoting them into one fourth “context” axis would merely rename the cross-cutting problem and create false unity.

The Shape therefore preserves them as independently classed coordinates/invariants rather than forcing a fourth bucket.

### Why not one unified orientation object?

Prior evidence demonstrates independent changeability among focal selection, frame qualification, Master Keying, qualification/currentness, and authority. A single orientation object may be useful as a view, but cannot be the semantic source of truth without false coupling.

### Why not separate URG / PGO / SSMM silos?

Current URG already carries frame, QF, boundary and orientation-envelope obligations. Current SSMM uses Master-Key-governed thresholds. Current orientation lifecycle depends on Referents and grammar. Exclusive module ownership would contradict actual dependency structure.

The architecture is **orthogonal enough to distinguish; integrated enough to compose**.

## 2. Macro-axis contracts

## 2.1 Semantic / Open-World Axis

### Characteristic work

Permit arbitrary referents, relations, compositions, maps, boundaries, and graded accounts to become representable without requiring a complete prior ontology.

Preserve:

* stable reference without claiming completeness;
* referent ≠ representation;
* focal unit ≠ mapper;
* boundary/individuation as explicit and revisable;
* constituent/participatory and other qualified relational structure;
* unknown/latent ≠ absent;
* grammar recurrence without forcing a universal content schema.

### Logical input

Encountered or inherited semantic material plus the current focal/boundary/frame conditions and structural grammar applicable to the inquiry.

### Logical output

A recoverable semantic field/account in which identities, relations, missingness, boundaries, and coverage standing are explicit enough for governing and temporal work.

### May govern

* referential identity/continuity conventions;
* relation and representation semantics;
* structural coverage obligations;
* boundary and grain expression;
* composability and graded resolution;
* how unknown/latent structure is represented.

### Must not govern

* what is currently important merely because it can represent it;
* action or binding authority;
* currentness merely from latest representation;
* truth merely from structural completeness;
* elimination merely from nonactivation.

### Dependencies

Requires a governing axis to determine consequential depth/activation and a temporal axis to control revisions/current standing.

### Failure signatures

* thick-schema requirement before novelty can be admitted;
* map = territory;
* stable ID = exhaustive identity;
* frame leakage;
* missing relation interpreted as absence;
* boundary change disguised as more detail;
* activation state encoded as existence.

### Requalification

Triggered by changed focal identity/grain, boundary/individuation, structural grammar that is materially different for the declared use, or evidence showing the current map misrepresents the referent.

## 2.2 Governing / Telic Axis

### Characteristic work

Establish the locally governing relation that determines what is consequential for the present engagement without claiming authority over existence or exhaustive truth.

A qualified governing orientation carries/resolves, as consequential:

* intended end state / definition of succeeding;
* purpose/telos / initiating contrast;
* relevance/admissibility criterion;
* governing obligations/constraints;
* declared use / intended Move;
* sufficiency / stop / fail conditions;
* conditions that would make the orientation insufficient and require reopening;
* links to evidence, authority, and unresolved questions where those bear on reliance.

### Logical input

A situated semantic field, focal engagement, declared or recoverable purpose, inherited obligations, evidence/access, and relevant authority/standing.

### Logical output

A locally governing orientation / discriminator against which active work can be prioritized, evaluated, stopped, exteriorized, or reopened.

### May govern

* consequentiality for present work;
* active depth and attention allocation;
* what counts as sufficient for the declared Move;
* which unresolved questions block, sensitize, or do not presently change the Move;
* local stopping and reentry conditions.

### Must not govern

* whether a referent exists;
* persistent identity merely through relevance;
* mapper/frame identity;
* evidence truth;
* authority merely through goal ownership;
* current binding merely because an orientation has been resolved;
* action permission.

### Dependencies

Requires semantic/focal/frame coordinates from the open-world axis and temporal/currentness machinery for installation/reliance/reentry.

### Failure signatures

* relevance-first deletion;
* done-as-truth;
* goal = authority;
* target absorbs mapper/frame;
* key change mutates referent existence;
* currentness inferred from resolution existence;
* self-sealing orientation that cannot admit challenges to itself.

### Requalification

Triggered by changed purpose/telos, initiating contrast, obligations, declared use, relevance criterion, stop/fail condition, material evidence that changes the decision surface, or a frame challenge capable of invalidating the orientation.

## 2.3 Temporal / Coordination Axis

### Characteristic work

Govern legitimate changes of semantic and operational standing through causal/coordination time.

Preserve:

* episode/process identity;
* phase/coordination state;
* parent/child inquiry and return;
* happened-before / dependency lineage where consequential;
* hard-stop/gate semantics;
* currentness and supersession transitions;
* suspension/reentry;
* applicability epochs/bases where needed;
* Metabolize/feed-forward.

### Logical input

A qualified present engagement, proposed transition, current coordination/binding state, predecessor identity, evidence/authority conditions, and transition-specific preconditions.

### Logical output

An explicit transition/receipt/state from which a cold reader can determine what changed, what is now entitled to proceed, what remains unresolved, and what must requalify.

### May govern

* phase legality;
* branch/return coordination;
* currentness transitions;
* suspension/reentry;
* predecessor/epoch checks;
* transition lineage;
* whether a prior qualification remains applicable after material change.

### Must not govern

* semantic truth merely because a phase advanced;
* action authority merely because Move is open;
* relevance merely because something is recent;
* referent identity merely because an episode points to it;
* total order where independent branches are causally unordered.

### Dependencies

Requires semantic identity/lineage and governing criteria for what counts as material or sufficient.

### Failure signatures

* proposal silently becomes current;
* latest-wins currentness;
* attempt = effect;
* stale orientation remains operative;
* child inquiry returns without lineage;
* phase-free authority promotion;
* branch independence collapsed into one global clock.

### Requalification

Triggered by material dependency change, invalid predecessor/current basis, phase-boundary failure, changed governing orientation, child return that alters the parent decision surface, or an explicit QF/sensor event requiring reopening.

### Fidelity invariants

The next Move must preserve these invariants:

**F1 — Referential fidelity.** Focal identity, grain, and boundary must be recoverable; changing one must not silently mutate the others.

**F2 — Frame fidelity.** Mapper/frame/access must remain attributable; the map’s selected contents do not become exhaustive referent contents.

**F3 — Warrant fidelity.** Evidence, evaluation, qualification, and confidence/standing must remain distinguishable from representation and authority.

**F4 — Authority/currentness fidelity.** Semantic fitness, current selection, authority to bind, and authority to act are separate.

**F5 — Possibility fidelity.** Nonactivation/nonselection must not imply nonexistence, falsity, or permanent irrelevance.

**F6 — Transition fidelity.** Material change must name what changed and route the appropriate requalification rather than invoking generic refresh.

**F7 — Reconstruction fidelity.** A handoff/reentry representation must permit recovery of the governing engagement without itself becoming the source of truth/currentness.

**F8 — Challenge fidelity.** Evidence capable of invalidating the current governing orientation must have a bounded route that is not filtered out solely by that orientation’s ordinary relevance rule.

## 4. Composition contract — present engagement without monolith

Shape selects a **composed engagement projection** as the logical interface for present work.

“Engagement projection” is descriptive language, not a new first-class proper name or required persisted object.

A valid projection resolves or points to, at the resolution warranted by the current work:

* focal referent / grain / boundary;
* mapper/frame/access conditions;
* applicable structural grammar and semantic coverage;
* governing orientation;
* evidence/warrant/qualification state;
* authority/custody/currentness where consequential;
* SSMM coordination state / episode / parent relation;
* active and dormant/exteriorized dispositions;
* Question Forward / sensors / reentry conditions;
* actor/worker relations where consequential.

### Composition rules

1. **Reference, do not absorb.** Constituents retain independent identity/lineage wherever they can change independently.
2. **Projection ≠ referent.** The projection is a map for the current engagement.
3. **Projection ≠ currentness.** Currentness comes from explicit coordination/binding semantics.
4. **Projection ≠ authority.** It may resolve authority but cannot create it.
5. **Projection ≠ completeness.** Omitted material must have a warranted disposition where consequential.
6. **Version independently.** A changed frame, GO, evidence basis, authority, or phase must not require pretending the focal referent changed.
7. **Permit partial resolution.** Unknowns can remain open when the governing Move permits them and a QF/reentry route exists.
8. **No giant declaration requirement.** The projection may be composed lazily from stable references; logical completeness does not require one serialized packet.

### Compatibility with current BUILD-7′ orientation kernel

The current qualified isolated orientation-lifecycle architecture is a valid narrower realization of this contract.

It already preserves:

* Orientation Scope;
* immutable Orientation Resolution representation;
* separate Qualification;
* explicit Binding Transition/current head;
* separate authority decision/capability;
* material-change fencing/requalification;
* QF/reentry;
* handoff representation not being source of currentness.

The new Shape **does not supersede** those separations. It places them within the broader tri-axial/fidelity architecture.

## 5. Disposition grammar — orthogonal, not one enum

Shape rejects one flat seven-value “status” field because it would conflate whether something was examined, whether it is computationally active, and what standing it has.

Freeze three orthogonal logical dimensions.

### 5.1 Coverage

* **UNEXAMINED** — not yet inspected under the relevant structural obligation.
* **EXAMINED** — inspected enough to assign a current disposition, including unresolved.

Coverage says nothing by itself about truth, activity, or relevance.

### 5.2 Activation

* **ACTIVE** — currently receiving computation/attention or participating in the present engagement.
* **DORMANT** — preserved/recoverable but not currently active.

Activation says nothing by itself about epistemic standing or future relevance.

### 5.3 Disposition / standing

At minimum preserve these distinct meanings:

* **RELIED_FOR_DECLARED_USE** — currently qualified/reliable enough for the stated use under the applicable basis; does not imply global truth.
* **UNRESOLVED** — live uncertainty remains.
* **NONCONSEQUENTIAL_NOW** — examined and presently does not change the governing Move under current G.
* **CONDITIONAL / SENSORED** — currently dormant or nonblocking, with explicit evidence/trigger that can change disposition.
* **REJECTED / CONTRADICTED WITH WARRANT** — excluded for the declared basis with retained reason/evidence.
* **PROHIBITED / IMPOSSIBLE UNDER QUALIFIED RULE** — a stronger exclusion whose rule and scope are explicit.

The logical grammar must permit combinations such as:

* ACTIVE + UNRESOLVED;
* DORMANT + CONDITIONAL/SENSORED;
* ACTIVE + REJECTED (e.g. adversarial comparator under test);
* EXAMINED + NONCONSEQUENTIAL_NOW;
* UNEXAMINED + DORMANT.

This prevents “not retrieved” from becoming a semantic verdict.

## 7. Derived-operation map

### Focalization / seating

**Classification:** operation over semantic coordinates.

Selects/declares the focal referent/grain/boundary for the current inquiry. Not equivalent to governing orientation.

### Frame qualification

**Classification:** cross-cutting qualification operation.

Makes mapper/frame/access consequentially inspectable. Not equivalent to focalization.

### Fractal Decoder Ring

**Classification:** historical token-portable derivation procedure.

Uses structural grammar + local conditions to derive/test a room-specific governing orientation / Master Key. It is not the governing result itself and not the whole finite-coherence architecture.

### Master Key / Governing Orientation resolution

**Classification:** governing-axis semantic function + possible materialized representation.

Determines locally governing telic/relevance/stop/reentry structure. Its materialization is not current merely by existing.

### Activation management

**Classification:** derived operation at Semantic × Governing × Temporal intersection.

Allocates present computation/attention according to governing consequentiality and coordination state while preserving dormant standing.

It does **not** eliminate possibilities.

### Binding / current reliance

**Classification:** temporal/authority-mediated transition.

Confers scoped current reliance on a qualified representation under the applicable basis. Does not establish global truth or external action authority.

### Exteriorization / Question Forward

**Classification:** cross-cutting fidelity operation/contract.

Moves material out of active computation while preserving what remains unknown, why it is nonactive, what signal could matter, and how reentry occurs.

### SSMM advance / suspend / return

**Classification:** temporal-governance operations.

Change coordination entitlement and preserve parent/child/currentness semantics.

### Requalification / reentry

**Classification:** typed transition family.

Triggered by material changes in coordinates/basis. Reentry is not one generic recursion operation.

### Handoff / transfer

**Classification:** representation + reconstruction operation.

Externalizes a recoverable engagement projection; receiving worker reconstructs and qualifies it. The packet/snapshot is not the source of authority/currentness.

### Metabolize / feed-forward

**Classification:** SSMM macro transition/integration operation.

Interprets consequences, updates what is known/standing, preserves unresolved residue, and seeds later Sense. Local phase gates may have metabolic behavior without making every gate a full persisted Metabolize object.
