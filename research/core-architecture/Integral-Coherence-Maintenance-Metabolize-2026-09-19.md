## 1. Why this checkpoint exists

The naming Move exposed a likely upstream defect in the referent being named.

ECO-160 successfully qualified **BRIMAR** as a proposal for the then-frozen successor-orientation referent. Subsequent Principal participation challenged that referent as too use-case-specific. The discussion recovered a deeper structural problem that appears across previously distinct ECOS instruments:

> How can a finite reasoner maintain a coherent, revisable engagement with an open-ended semantic reality without either carrying the whole field or silently destroying what is not presently active?

This checkpoint metabolizes that finding without prematurely converting it into doctrine.

The central correction is:

> **Prune computation, not possibility. Narrow commitment, not reality.**

The active engagement may be finite. The semantic possibility space must remain open, recoverable, and capable of reactivation.

## 3. The three-dimension hypothesis

The current strongest hypothesis is that faithful finite coherence maintenance requires three mutually irreducible architectural dimensions.

These are **candidate integral primitives/capacities**, not yet canonized peer objects.

### A. URG — open-world semantic grammar

Candidate constitutive job:

> Support ad hoc, unbounded referent combinatorics while preserving enough structural discipline that workers can understand and use the substrate reliably without a fixed domain ontology.

URG provides **openness without chaos**.

Required characteristics include, at minimum:

* universal/ad hoc Referent admission;
* compositional relation without category collapse;
* ability to preserve representation vs referent, evidence vs claim, map vs territory, and other governing distinctions;
* structural guidance sufficient that correct composition is the path of least resistance;
* support for novelty without requiring pre-enumeration of all possible domain entities or combinations;
* graded resolution rather than exhaustive materialization.

Candidate failure if removed:

> PGO + SSMM could govern goals and temporal progression, but only over a hard-coded or semantically brittle domain. Novel referents/compositions would require redesign or ad hoc escape hatches.

### B. PGO / Master-Key lineage — locally governing telic orientation

Candidate constitutive job:

> Bind a particular composite engagement to a specific observable ideal end state / governing orientation strongly enough that relevance can be discerned without collapsing the larger latent field.

PGO provides **relevance without reduction**.

This is not merely “goal,” “context,” or similarity ranking.

The current hypothesis is that a locally governing orientation establishes why some semantic material is consequential now, while allowing other encountered material to remain dormant, conditionally relevant, unresolved, contradictory, historically important, or explicitly exteriorized rather than semantically deleted.

Candidate functions include:

* focal engagement;
* purpose/telos / observable desired condition;
* governing obligations;
* relevance discrimination;
* evidence/access/authority conditions where consequential;
* sufficiency/stopping;
* explicit exteriorization;
* Question Forward and re-entry conditions.

Candidate failure if removed:

> URG + SSMM could represent almost anything and progress through disciplined phases, but relevance would be underdetermined. The system would fall back to generic retrieval heuristics, indiscriminate accumulation, or accidental pruning.

### C. SSMM — recursively gated temporal governance

Candidate constitutive job:

> Advance inquiry and action through legible, reviewable, recursively nestable changes of semantic/coordination standing without allowing exploration, commitment, action, evidence, effect, currentness, and requalification to collapse into one another.

SSMM provides **change without drift**.

The important property is not merely four named phases. It is controlled becoming through governed differentiation ↔ integration ↔ transcendence, with gates that expose what changed, what is now entitled to proceed, what remains unresolved, what returns to the parent, and what must be requalified.

Candidate failure if removed:

> URG + PGO could represent an open field and establish local relevance, but there would be no principled machinery for changing commitment through time. Exploration could silently become selection, proposal become canon, attempt become effect, stale orientation remain current, or child inquiry fail to return coherently.

## 4. Candidate integral relation

The provisional synthesis is:

> **URG gives openness without chaos. PGO gives relevance without reduction. SSMM gives change without drift.**

Together they may form the constitutive support for a more general ECOS capability:

> **Maintain a finite, coherent, dynamically revisable engagement with an open semantic field while preserving the standing, recoverability, and generative potential of what is not presently active.**

This is deliberately not framed as “smallest sufficient context.”

A finite engagement may be broad, redundant, exploratory, generative, or intentionally overcomplete when the governing Move warrants it.

The bounded quantity is the agent/system's present commitment and consequential exposure — not reality, novelty, learning, or latent possibility.

## 5. Derived-behavior hypothesis

Several mechanisms previously experienced as distinct may be interactions or projections of the three-dimensional mechanism rather than additional peer primitives.

This is a **hypothesis to test**, not a consolidation decision.

### Dynamic pruning

Candidate derivation:

> PGO-conditioned relevance operating over URG-expressible possibility during an SSMM coordination state.

Important distinction:

* **activation pruning:** what does not require active semantic computation now; cheap and reversible;
* **possibility pruning:** what has actually been ruled out; requires warrant.

Failure condition: absence from the active set must not silently become evidence of irrelevance or nonexistence.

### Binding

Candidate derivation:

> An SSMM-governed transition that gives current reliance/standing to a PGO-qualified semantic composition represented through URG.

Binding therefore applies to the finite engagement, not to infinity/reality itself.

### Question Forward / apertures / wired sensors

Candidate derivation:

> Preservation of a not-currently-active semantic possibility in URG form, whose PGO relevance conditions and SSMM re-entry trigger remain legible.

This is the mechanism by which non-reductivism can remain bounded without becoming exhaustive maximalism.

### Reentry

Candidate derivation:

> A coordination transition triggered when preserved conditions indicate that the prior PGO-governed engagement is no longer sufficient or applicable.

### Handoff / session reentry

Candidate derivation:

> Externalization of enough of the current URG-represented, PGO-governed, SSMM-current configuration for another worker/session to reconstruct an operationally equivalent engagement.

This explains why a mechanism originally understood as preventing a worker from getting lost during deep derivation also supports session handoff. The discontinuity changed; the coherence problem did not.

### Decoder Ring

Historical fact remains: Decoder Ring was a derivation procedure and Master Key its room-specific target + helps-rule.

New hypothesis:

> Decoder Ring may be the derivational/activation face of the deeper mechanism — how a worker discovers a locally governing finite engagement from an open field.

Do not rewrite historical artifacts as if this identity had been known originally.

### Master Key / PGO

New hypothesis:

> Master Key/PGO may be the local binding/governing face of the deeper mechanism — the presently relied-upon orientation that allows consequential work to proceed.

### FIBERR / Filament

Current lineage must be recovered before any consolidation claim.

New hypothesis only:

> Filament/FIBERR may express continuity/provenance/recoverability aspects of the same mechanism, especially the preservation of why a configuration has its present shape and how dormant alternatives remain traceably reopenable.

This must be tested against actual FIBERR/Filament source semantics; surface resemblance is insufficient.

## 6. Why this is not “everything is context management”

A true unification must generate the old distinctions rather than erase them.

The three-dimension hypothesis fails if its abstraction can only restate generic truths such as:

* upstream decisions matter;
* context should be preserved;
* work should be phased;
* relevant information should be selected.

Those are too generic to justify architecture.

A successful unification must explain, from the common structure, why:

* Decoder Ring behaves procedurally;
* Master Key behaves as a locally governing target/discriminator;
* FIBERR/Filament behaves as continuity/provenance/recoverability;
* Question Forward preserves dormant possibility without recursively opening it;
* SSMM distinguishes exploration from commitment and later requalification;
* session handoff and in-session deep-focus recovery are structurally homologous but operationally different.

If the common mechanism cannot derive these differences, the unification is over-compressed.

## 8. Strong falsifiers / ablation tests

The three-dimension hypothesis earns stronger standing only if it survives at least these tests.

### Ablation

1. Remove URG while retaining PGO + SSMM. Does open-world novelty/composition fail in a qualitatively distinct way?
2. Remove PGO while retaining URG + SSMM. Does relevance become underdetermined without substituting generic retrieval/optimization heuristics?
3. Remove SSMM while retaining URG + PGO. Does temporal/coordination category drift become unavoidable or require hidden replacement machinery?

A removal test is invalid if the replacement silently preserves the allegedly removed characteristic work.

### Cross-domain

Test at materially different engagements:

* architecture derivation;
* scientific/research inquiry;
* practical consequential action;
* session/worker handoff;
* unfamiliar external domain with no personal-memory substrate;
* reflective self-application of ECOS to its own machinery.

### Historical derivation

Recover Decoder Ring, Master Key/PGO, FIBERR/Filament, Question Forward and SSMM provenance independently.

Ask whether the three-dimension hypothesis predicts their observed functional differences better than treating them as unrelated instruments.

### Generative prediction

The hypothesis should expose useful new predictions or design obligations not inserted by hand.

If it merely redescribes known parts with a new umbrella phrase, it fails.

### Fertility preservation

Any architecture derived from the hypothesis must preserve the distinction between:

* not active now;
* examined and currently non-consequential;
* unresolved;
* conditionally relevant;
* contradicted/rejected with warrant;
* never examined;
* permanently impossible/forbidden where warranted.

If “pruning” collapses these into one inactive state, the hypothesis has been implemented reductively.
