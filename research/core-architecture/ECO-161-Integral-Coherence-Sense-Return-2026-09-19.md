## Executive result

The inquiry independently converges on a real higher-order problem:

> A finite reasoner operating in an open semantic world must be able to maintain a coherent, revisable, purpose-sensitive engagement without either attempting exhaustive active representation or silently converting what is inactive into what is absent, irrelevant, false, or nonexistent.

The provisional URG × PGO × SSMM synthesis survives **only after a material correction**.

The evidence does **not** support “URG, PGO, and SSMM are the three exhaustive primitives of the mechanism.”

The evidence **does** support treating them as three irreducible macro-capability dimensions / coordinate families:

1. **Open semantic expressibility and compositional situatedness** — current URG territory.
2. **Locally governing telic/relevance orientation** — Master-Key / Governing-Orientation territory.
3. **Legible transformation of standing through causal/coordination time** — SSMM territory.

Each fails differently under ablation. None can be replaced by the other two without silently reintroducing its characteristic work.

However, faithful finite coherence also requires independently variable **cross-cutting fidelity invariants** — especially referential/focal continuity, frame qualification, bootstrap warrant, preservation of live alternatives, evidence/warrant, authority/currentness separation, Question Forward/reentry, and transfer/reconstruction fidelity. Those are not automatically fourth-through-Nth “primitives”; Shape must determine their exact status. They cannot be swallowed into PGO or any other single axis merely to make the architecture tidier.

The correct Shape problem is therefore **tri-axial but non-exhaustive**.

A compact statement of the surviving synthesis is:

> **Open world, governed engagement, legitimate becoming — with fidelity invariants that prevent each local engagement from masquerading as the territory.**

The most important negative result is equally strong:

> **Finite activation is not semantic elimination. Relevance may govern active engagement; it may not silently govern existence.**

## 2. Independent derivation of the problem

The three-capacity hypothesis was not taken as the answer key. The problem was independently approached from seven origins.

### 2.1 Open-world semantic representation

A general system cannot require a closed domain ontology before an unfamiliar referent can become discussable. Historical P-03 evidence explicitly rejects the requirement that a previously unencoded problem be universally typed/classified before becoming focal. Current URG similarly treats stable identity as continuity, not completeness, and treats unknown/latent relations as unresolved rather than absent.

**Derived requirement:** the semantic substrate must admit novel referents and compositions while preserving structural distinctions and defeasible incompleteness.

This creates the first macro-capability family: open-world grammar / semantic situatedness.

### 2.2 Relevance under finite attention

Even with universal representability, a finite worker cannot actively compute every possible relation, implication, frame, evidence source, and recursive dependency.

A criterion is therefore needed for what becomes consequential **now**.

But a generic retrieval score is insufficient. ECO-92 makes the core pressure explicit: the Master Key exists so a complex system can decide what matters now while reality remains unbounded and recursively inspectable. Current lineage repeatedly separates target/done/relevance from existence and ontology.

**Derived requirement:** local engagement needs a governing telic/relevance relation that can prioritize active work without turning non-selection into ontological deletion.

This creates the second macro-capability family: governing orientation.

### 2.3 Temporal/currentness/coordination

A semantically rich, well-oriented snapshot is still insufficient if its standing cannot change legibly.

The system must distinguish exploration from qualification, proposal from acceptance, authorization from execution, attempt from effect, old orientation from current orientation, child inquiry from parent return, and historical validity from current applicability.

The temporal-fractal SSMM evidence independently establishes referential coordinates as distinct from coordination coordinates and identifies hard stops as integration/requalification boundaries.

**Derived requirement:** there must be explicit temporal/causal governance over changes in what is entitled to proceed and what remains current.

This creates the third macro-capability family: SSMM-like temporal governance.

### 2.4 Session/worker handoff

A handoff cannot be made faithful merely by copying more context. Another worker must be able to reconstruct the relevant referent, frame, governing orientation, standing, unresolved apertures, and current coordination state without silently inheriting obsolete or merely historical material as current.

This requires all three macro dimensions **plus** actor/worker and authority/currentness distinctions.

**Result:** handoff is not evidence for a separate fourth macro-axis by itself, but it exposes cross-cutting fidelity obligations the three-axis shorthand does not automatically specify.

### 2.5 Recursive inquiry / self-application

The system must be able to make its own maps, procedures, orientations, and coordination states referents without requiring infinite prior closure.

ECO-92 permits recursion only when deeper inspection can change the active decision; P-03 independently derives a bootstrap warrant that permits first Sense without a completed prior loop.

**Result:** bootstrap + bounded recursive opening are indispensable. They cannot be reduced to “PGO chooses relevance” because the bootstrap problem exists before a hardened PGO has been fully derived.

### 2.6 Consequential action

Once semantic work can cause action, additional distinctions become load-bearing: authority, custody, execution, verification, acknowledgement, effect, and requalification from consequence.

The compiler/coordination checkpoint explicitly distinguishes compiler-like, runtime-like, OS-like, and toolchain-like functions. SSMM governs temporal legitimacy, but action authority is not created by phase alone.

**Result:** action confirms the need for the temporal axis and exposes cross-cutting authority/warrant invariants.

### 2.7 Unfamiliar domain with no personal-memory substrate

A novel scientific paper, unfamiliar repository, unknown organization, mathematical object, or external evidence field still presents the same structural pressures: represent novel referents, establish a purpose-sensitive inquiry, preserve frames and warrant, proceed through controlled commitments, and keep excluded/unresolved possibilities reopenable.

**Result:** personal memory / second-brain retrieval is one substrate/use case. It is not the architectural category of the mechanism.

### Independent-derivation disposition

**CONVERGENCE: STRONG.**

The same higher-order problem appears from all seven origins. It does not depend on personal-memory continuity, naming work, or a specific Master-Key use case.

## 8. Fertility / latent-space preservation

The key distinction is not simply active versus inactive.

A faithful system must preserve at least:

1. **active/currently relied upon**;
2. **considered but currently nonconsequential**;
3. **unresolved / open**;
4. **conditionally relevant / sensored**;
5. **rejected or contradicted with warrant**;
6. **never examined**;
7. **impossible/forbidden under a qualified rule**, where warranted.

The first six must not collapse into a binary retrieved/not-retrieved or active/inactive score.

Therefore “dynamic pruning” is safer when Shape distinguishes:

* **activation management** — allocation of current computation/attention;
* **disposition** — epistemic/governing standing of what is not active;
* **elimination/rejection** — a separately warranted disposition;
* **reactivation** — a QF/sensor/currentness-triggered transition.

This yields a stronger formulation than “prune aggressively”:

> **Control activation aggressively; constrain elimination rigorously.**

## 9. Generative predictions

The synthesis earns more than umbrella status because it predicts several architectural obligations not supplied merely by the label “context management.”

### Prediction 1 — active-set absence must be typed

Any future implementation that cannot distinguish “inactive” from unresolved, rejected, never examined, or conditionally relevant will produce relevance-first deletion or dormancy-as-nonexistence failure.

### Prediction 2 — reorientation triggers must be typed by what changed

A change in focal grain, frame, governing orientation, evidence basis, authority/currentness, or coordination state should not all invoke the same generic “refresh context” operation. They require different requalification paths.

### Prediction 3 — handoff fidelity is reconstructive, not volumetric

A shorter handoff can be more faithful than a larger context dump if it preserves the independent coordinates and reentry apertures. Context volume should not be the fidelity metric.

### Prediction 4 — memory retrieval is insufficient without governing/currentness semantics

A perfect personal-memory MCP can still return stale, wrong-frame, non-governing, or currently irrelevant material. Retrieval is substrate capability; finite coherence requires qualified engagement.

### Prediction 5 — any “engagement object” must be a composed view, not an ontological monolith

Because focal seat, frame, Master Key, authority, and coordination basis can vary independently, a convenient current-engagement representation must preserve pointers/standing to independently inspectable components.

### Prediction 6 — frame challenges need a route outside ordinary relevance filtering

A governing orientation cannot be sole judge of evidence capable of invalidating that orientation. Existing prior work already anticipates this. A faithful architecture needs a bounded challenge/reorientation path.

These predictions are sufficiently discriminating to treat the synthesis as architectural, not merely rhetorical.
