## 1. Governing orientation and decisive contrast

**Containing referent:** SIGMA's ordinary claim-to-consumer qualification pathway, inside the larger ECOS obligation-to-use realization and its actual accounts, devices, workers and native sources. **Focal R:** the consequential **semantic-assessment acceptance seam** connecting an attributable domain evaluator's native relation/Level interpretation to the coordinator's `ADMIT` / `READY` outputs and consumer projection. Do not silently reseat R to the universal truth of Quadrant theory, identity of the source file, or the backend's entire authority machinery.

**Mapper / perspective / access:** present audit worker, able to read exact pinned repository editions, inspect TypeScript contracts/tests, and run locally isolated copies of the original coordinator + URG validator with explicitly constructed adapters. No actual production client or uninvolved consumer was observed. Principal is authoritative for normative acceptance; this worker is not.

**Initiating contrast:** an external reader reported the “what qualifies the qualifiers?” regress: exact hashes and qualified language might form a self-referential graph with no readily discoverable external ground. In a bounded native fact test, both the truthfully asserted and the falsely asserted text-span locations passed the same structural preflight and were exposed as `READY / ADMIT`. The defect is the **promotion of an evaluator's internally well-formed assertion into consumer reliance**, not the use of internal source records or authorship in itself.

**PGO / magic-wand outcome:** An ordinary interlocutor follows the lowest-friction path from a specific consequential question to an applicable native qualification criterion, independently attributable ground for that criterion, a bounded and defeasible decision, and a directly accessible challenge/requalification path. The ordinary path makes *ground contact more accessible than further internally consistent commentary*. The consumer must not have to know the entire SIGMA graph to distinguish a warranted claim from an unsupported one.

**Definition of done for this Shape:** (i) discriminate actual obligation and mechanism, (ii) choose smallest existing-carrier repair, (iii) specify pass/fail controls at the exact acceptance seam, (iv) preserve two very narrow exceptional routes and truthful dispositions, (v) return a self-contained successor Move decision without performing Move. **Stop** before implementation, contract ratification, database writes to operational tables, test fixture modification, Vercel promotion, or widening the FOT semantic study.

**HELPS — non-exhaustive, actionable signals:** immutable native source content actually recovered, exact tested predicate and source/occasion, a qualified native check or independently accountable judgment showing how evidence satisfies the criterion, paired contrary cases, and a consumer trace preserving both conclusion and its limits. **What to do:** follow actual source content and the predicate-specific discriminator, record outcome/standing and only then expose use-authorized reliance.

**HINDERS — non-exhaustive, actionable signals:** source digest cited as if it proves a semantic proposition; nonempty `warrant_ref` mistaken for examined warrant; same author supplying both conjecture and supposed independent proof without a separate ground route; coordinator `READY` when native predicate false; overall `HOLD` yet the same disproven candidate remains an apparently valid `ADMIT` in projection; automatic blocking of independently qualified unrelated claims; indefinite HOLD without an exact QF. **What to do:** withhold/requalify only the affected claim under its proper basis, trace the missing native ground, and return a concrete discriminatory Question Forward when needed.

## 4. Selected *proposed* repair — existing seam, not a new architecture

**Shape clause (candidate for Principal selection):**

> Before a consequential `ADMIT` can qualify a claimed native relation for the declared use, a bounded account must make recoverable (a) the exact proposition and native criterion at specified R/B/PGO/occasion, (b) its evidentiary source and the procedure/interpretation that connects that source to this predicate, (c) the result of that check at the appropriate empirical, formal, interpretive or authorized-decision standing, and (d) its defeaters and decision consequence. A record's content, provenance reference, digest, well-formedness or evaluator's own declaration cannot self-supply (c) when the claim requires independently qualifying contact. If predicate-specific qualification is absent or contradicted, the **same affected claim** must not be presented as `ADMIT`/reliance-qualified; emit the existing, actionably calibrated QF or grounded negative result. Source-level or session-level `READY` cannot substitute for claim-specific standing.

The clause is **purpose-relative**, not a global requirement for non-mapper or third-party humans. One agent may both examine and check when it genuinely uses distinguishable native data and a fitting criterion, provided each basis and limitation is recoverable. For empirical assertions the ground may be real observation/testimony/instrument records; for formal assertions declared premises and valid inference; for SIGMA normative choices an authorized actual Principal decision. An explicit decision qualifies the normative status of a rule, **not its universal applicability or real-world success**.

**Proposed realization order and responsibility separation:**
1. **Native evaluator responsibility, before `ADMIT`:** Evaluate **this exact predicate** against matching native-source content, conditions/occasion, and sufficiently realistic PGO. For the source-span case the operative test reads the **pinned UTF-8 text** and checks that the named section occupies the asserted interval, not merely that the path, hash, `assessment_ref` or `warrant_ref` looks correct. Existing `evaluateCandidate` can return `QUESTION_FORWARD` or `REJECT` instead of `ADMIT`; `assessment_ref`, `reason`, `NativeRelationClaim`, `evidence_refs`, and `current_use` carry the exact basis and standing. An independent existing native verifier path may be used where relevant. Do not require a new generic `verifyAnything` capability.
2. **Coordinator/reconciliation last line:** Treat a **failed applicable blocking requirement for a particular admitted claim** as that claim's failed reliance. Associate requirement to affected claim using existing relation/candidate identities and basis refs; downgrade/quarantine only affected `ADMIT` outcomes and remove them from actual `admitted`. Ensure the projection reflects the same downgraded disposition and no misleading reliance. If the existing reconciliation requirements cannot identify affected members, fail closed for the **implicated unknown set** until a precise link is recoverable rather than silently retaining a false admission; do not permanently erase separately qualified unrelated results. A separate unrelated QF may make the *whole inquiry* `HOLD` without negating an actually qualified *candidate*, but the consumer must not confuse local validity with global readiness.
3. **Default consumer ground path:** For every claim the consumer proposes to *rely on*, show its specific native criterion, checked source/ground and result (or explicit "not established"), scope and accessible falsifier/next action. Recover exact evidence on demand through existing source records; never require a worker to read an entire internal scripture-like chain to discover the original qualifying contact. The progressive-display choice must be tested with an actual client and is NOT proven by merely retaining pointer fields.

**Implementation-shape preference:** try evaluator-native predicate checking first, using present adapter/semantic protocol. Add the smallest coordinator consistency repair if/where post-evaluation falsification can arise (the bounded trial demonstrated this exact need). Only propose a new optional interface method, field or record type **after** proving current `evaluateCandidate` + `reconcile` + existing records cannot carry the required semantics and decision consequence. Avoid an automatic requirement to run one global semantic engine or to turn every semantic judgment into rigid deterministic logic.

## 5. Two and only two general exceptional routes

**E1 — explicit stipulation / hypothetical derivation.** A declared model premise, invented actor/world state or provisional design axiom may be used under a sufficiently specified local PGO for *conditional* inference. The consumer's claim must visibly remain conditional, and the stipulated status never becomes an observed native fact or universally binding semantic law merely because the derived reasoning is internally sound. Conditional inquiry may be “complete for stipulated purpose” but not `READY` as proof of the unstipulated actual phenomenon.

**E2 — actionable incompleteness.** When the pertinent native evidence or criterion cannot be acquired/qualified, return a concrete `QuestionForward` containing the exact unresolved native variable, what source/test/decision could settle it, its appropriate disclosure question, counterexample/reopening condition, who acts and where the result returns, and the consequence for current use. That may permit some **separately qualified** bounded actions, but never a false positive reliance on the unsupported predicate. A bare indefinite HOLD or elaborate internal recursion with no external/native discovery action fails.

**Not escape routes:** "the Principal authored the document, therefore all its empirical claims are true"; "source blob exists, therefore its metaphysics is correct"; "all structural fields are filled, therefore the predicate was witnessed"; "the same evaluator's second essay calls the first valid"; "overall HOLD hides a still-admitted false local claim"; invented third-party attestations.

**Pre-execution validation:** the controls discriminate structural validity, semantic/native verification, and consumer binding without assuming all empirical truth is algorithmically decidable. **Post-execution verification:** test actual emitted candidate dispositions, `admitted`, `READY/HOLD`, exact consumer projection, document edition, and source readback; do not use a lone global `PASS`. Failure must identify changed input, violated predicate, source, receiving owner, and precise repair.

## 7. Adversarial adequacy and anti-overbuild dispositions

- **"Require a longer citation graph." REJECT:** internally consistent provenance can still lack material grounding.
- **"Require different workers as universal independent evaluators." REJECT:** distinct agents can share the same unearned premise; one mapper can legitimately verify a byte relation against independent immutable source bytes.
- **"Trust an additional string `warrant_verified=true`." REJECT:** repeats self-certification. A method-specific result must be checked against the actual source and criterion by the appropriate native mechanism or sufficiently warranted adjudication.
- **"Block all unresolved work." REJECT:** conditional inquiry, local negatives and independently qualified partial results retain their own disposition under declared scope.
- **"Implement a universal theorem of truth." REJECT:** native empirical, normative, interpretive and formal qualification differ; the architecture governs nonpromotion and source-to-use contact, not omniscience.
- **"Make the coordinator fetch every domain fact." REJECT:** use existing native evaluation and adapter boundaries; preserve semantic domain descent and machine liability separation.
- **"Make a new `GroundPath` storage type before trying present carriers." REJECT:** no separately warranted semantic job shown for the bounded case.
- **"Overall `HOLD` solves N2." REJECT by actual Trial B:** the contradicted member remained `ADMIT` in `admitted` and projection. Repair member-level promotion.
