## 1. Purpose

Define the smallest positive procedure by which a worker can:

1. recover or provisionally establish a governing orientation and focal referent;
2. distinguish constitutive whole/part structure from consequential participation;
3. traverse the four Quadrant obligations without silently changing the referent or boundary;
4. route discoveries through already-defined typed transformations;
5. reconcile affected support and newly relevant obligations;
6. stop when the account is sufficient for the active use, or return a bounded unresolved state with exact reentry.

This profile composes existing contracts. It is not a replacement ontology, persistence schema, runtime workflow engine, or new Quadrant generator.

## 3. Situated inquiry basis

Use the installed inquiry index:

`kappa = (R, B, G, F)`

where:

- `R` = focal referent;
- `B` = declared constituent boundary / individuation criterion;
- `G` = governing orientation / PGO interface;
- `F` = mapper, frame and access conditions where consequential.

During bootstrap, `R` and `B` may be provisional. A stable handle does not imply complete individuation.

### PGO non-manufacture rule

PGO may select consequential distinctions, relevance, resolution, sufficiency and reentry.

PGO does not by itself manufacture:

- referent identity;
- constitutive membership;
- Level;
- evidence;
- truth/currentness;
- Type membership;
- authority or permission.

A relation can become essential to the current inquiry without becoming constitutive of the focal referent.

## 4. Positive orchestration procedure

### O1 — Bootstrap / recover orientation

Invoke the existing `Bootstrap B` semantics over encounter/recovery inputs, source provenance, access and remit.

Permitted result:

- provisionally recovered `R/B/G/F`;
- retained candidate set;
- discriminating Question Forward;
- `READY` for the declared bounded use;
- `HOLD` where orientation, identity, access or discrimination is insufficient.

No complete R or G is presumed before bootstrap.

### O2 — Seat and expose holonic structure

Seat the provisional focal referent.

Expose, without forcing closure:

- known or candidate constituents;
- known or candidate participatory/co-relational relations;
- unresolved relations;
- containing / larger-whole relations where consequential;
- the boundary criterion currently being used.

Constitution requires the installed Level witness discipline. Consequentiality, causal importance, enclosure, common purpose or dependency alone do not establish constituency.

### O3 — Traverse Quadrant at fixed R/B

For the same focal referent and boundary, expose all four installed obligations:

- UL = Constitutive x Governing
- UR = Constitutive x Determinate
- LL = Participatory x Governing
- LR = Participatory x Determinate

Unknown or nonconsequential answers remain visibly unknown/nonconsequential. A missing answer does not erase an obligation.

Ordinary `TraverseQ` preserves R and B.

### O4 — Route each material discovery through an existing typed transformation

Use the narrowest truthful operation:

- same R/B, more attributable material -> `Enrich`
- same R, boundary account changes -> `ReviseBoundary`
- focal referent changes / new composite is individuated -> `Reseat`
- governing orientation changes -> `Reorient`
- mapper/frame/access changes -> `ChangeFrame`
- evidence/standing applicability changes -> `Requalify`
- new typed relation -> `Relate`
- new organized composite candidate -> `Compose` before any `Reseat`

Do not use prose to hide a coordinate change.

### O5 — Reconcile after material change

Apply ECO-191's two-part change obligation:

`Reconcile = CheckAffected(Affected_old(delta)) AND CheckCoverage(Req(new_basis,use), Accounted(new_basis,use))`

Therefore every material change must check both:

1. whether old relied-on support was affected; and
2. whether the new seat/orientation exposes requirements the old account never covered.

An unchanged old dependency graph cannot prove new-use adequacy.

### O6 — Continue only for consequential unresolved differences

Continue traversal only when another available inquiry step could plausibly change:

- the legitimate interpretation;
- the focal/boundary choice;
- required evidence or standing;
- the lawful next transition;
- sufficiency for the declared PGO/use.

Do not recurse merely because more detail exists.

This composes the earlier Quadrant v0.2 stopping rule: stop adding questions when remaining uncertainty is nonconsequential under G, already discriminated, or would not change the current Move.

### O7 — Accountable exit

Return `READY` for the declared bounded use when:

1. focal identity and current boundary are recoverable at the required resolution;
2. consequential constitutive versus participatory relations are discriminated or explicitly unresolved;
3. all four Quadrant obligations have been considered, with unresolved/nonconsequential positions explicit;
4. relied-on claims preserve source, evidence/warrant and standing;
5. material coordinate changes have been reconciled;
6. no presently unresolved distinction can change the legitimate next transition without additional evidence/contact;
7. reentry, recomposition and historical recovery remain possible.

Otherwise return `HOLD` with a sufficient Question Forward and exact reentry destination.

The Principal's phrase “depreciated state with explicit pointers to finish” remains preserved as source language for an incomplete/reduced-reliance account. This profile does not silently redefine it as the installed HOLD disposition or introduce a new executable status.

## 5. Three Goedel-Signals as diagnostic escalation aliases

The Three Goedel-Signals are diagnostic patterns over existing contract failures, not new semantic primitives.

### G1 — circularity / infinite regression

Fire only when a justification, decomposition or recursive opening cannot earn an independent consequential distinction and instead depends on its own unsupported result or expands without a discriminating boundary.

Examples of mapped existing behavior:

- a cyclic justification supplies no independent warrant;
- `Open` may HOLD where mandatory recursion is unbounded;
- an unresolved boundary routes to Question Forward rather than forced constituency.

**Default repair:** inspect boundary, PGO/intention, source/evidence, or focal seat; then use the applicable typed transformation or HOLD.

Legitimate feedback loops, recurrent processes and cyclic real-world dependency structures are not automatically this signal.

### G2 — smuggled standing

Fire when a claim acquires actuality, warrant, necessity, currentness, authority or permission beyond its attributable basis.

**Default repair:** restore exact provenance/standing; `Requalify`, reject unsupported carryover, or route the missing authority/warrant decision.

Semantic plausibility and repeated reuse do not promote standing.

### G3 — performative contradiction

Fire when a proposed realization defeats a condition required by the governing commitment under which that realization is justified.

**Default repair:** reject/revise the realization, `Reorient`, `Reseat`, or expose the unresolved governing choice as appropriate.

Ordinary preference tension, tradeoff or possible downside is insufficient.

## 6. Fixed-R / coordinate-independence laws

This profile makes explicit the following composition laws already supported by installed sources:

1. **PGO change need not change R or B.** Reorientation may radically change relevance, active relations, sufficiency and required evidence while preserving referent and constitution.
2. **Boundary revision need not change G.** A mistaken or underspecified whole/part cut can be corrected under the same PGO.
3. **Quadrant traversal does not itself change R/B.**
4. **A new composite requires explicit individuation and continuity basis.**
5. **A relation becoming consequential does not make it constitutive.**
6. **A constitutive witness can itself become Quadrant subject matter without assigning Level permanently to one quadrant.**
7. **A changed seat/orientation does not silently transport old answer standing.**
