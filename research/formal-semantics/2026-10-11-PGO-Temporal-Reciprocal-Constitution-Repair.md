## 3. Refine vertical constituency and dependency: relations, not lists

Current URG Level witness: W_L=(C,O,d), C drawn from the basis admitted by B; O is the organization relevant to this whole existing as *this* whole; d is warranted asymmetric constitutive dependence under this comparison. L1–L6: constituency, organization, whole-dependence, comparison-direction/asymmetry, explicit boundary, and no rank/prestige/global-altitude leakage. This is a local *witnessed relation*, not a generic feature hierarchy, resource list, or utility ranking.

**Functional decomposition** differentiates what H must accomplish under a declared G and the transformations/outcomes that make H capable of this work. Functions and subfunctions may themselves be organized into higher/lower functional wholes if independently warranted. Being a functional *substep* does not alone witness referent Level.

**Constituent decomposition** differentiates what organized referents and relations actually realize H-as-H under its identity/boundary at a declared situational basis. It may include organized participatory relations of participants that independently exist as other wholes. Being externally addressable, replaceable, hosted as another service, or relational rather than material does not forbid being constitutive of a *particular* containing whole; nor does a dependency word alone warrant Level.

**The vertical integration:** a higher-level capability or process depends on a qualifying organization of realized capacities; these capacities can be recursively differentiated into subfunctions and sub-realizations. This entails at least three *noninterchangeable* claim kinds:
1. function decomposition / functional organization (what transformations jointly deliver the purpose);
2. realization/allocation (which organized mechanisms, participants and interactions satisfy which functions, at which occasion);
3. constitutive Level witness (which qualifying organized relations/parts are identity-relevant to which newly seated whole under which B).

Do not turn all three into the same “parent-child” edge. A subfunction can be logically necessary for a chosen intended outcome without requiring one fixed concrete implementing service as an irreplaceable token. A participant can be transient yet constitute an actual occurrence; a recurring durable capability can persist across successive realizing participants. Replacements require continuity and realization requalification, not an automatic loss or preservation of identity. Multiple kinds of vertical decomposition may coexist; disclose which one a claim asserts.

**Constitution–participation synthesis:** A participatory relation of one referent may be constitutive of a differently seated containing whole under a qualifying Level witness; no relation-word alone grants that witness.  At R₁ = one worker service, its interaction with an ECB service may be an outward participatory relation. At R₂ = an actual worker–ECB–human reorientation episode, that organized interaction may be *inside the qualifying constitutive basis* of R₂. At R₃ = durable ambient interaction capability, the specific episode may be a realization/instance rather than an indispensable constituent token. These are lawful changed seats, not an exclusive binary kind of relation. Each asserted Level relation still needs its own witness; no participation label proves constitution.

**From function downward:** “Resume correctly” can be functionally differentiated into: recover the governing orientation; discover relevant prior evidence; distinguish changes and current standing; reconcile old support and newly applicable obligations; then route an authorized action or a bounded HOLD. These are *outcome-relevant functions*. They may be further decomposed into subfunctions, but that does not identify the specific parts or services of the eventual whole.

**From constitution upward:** suppose one occurrence actually has a user, worker and ECB connection cooperating under a specific recovery-and-requalification protocol. Their organized contributions and interaction might constitute that *connected reentry episode*, with a Level witness required for a claim that a particular participant or relation is part of the episode under its declared boundary. A different episode uses a portable, disconnected working packet. Both can fulfill the higher-level functional demand for truthful recovery yet have different *actually realized* relations. The durable capability's continued identity is not a mechanical inference from either one's success.

**Where both directions constrain the other:** top-down outcome specifies that the recovered material must be current and reliance-worthy; bottom-up observation of a stale but exact document forces a new qualification requirement or a bounded degraded result. It does not authorize the observer to redefine the Principal's goal downward, nor allow the goal to assert that a connection or constituent exists. A replacement service is not automatically identical to the old service, yet a changed performer may preserve the qualified higher-level capability when its identity and continuity conditions are met.

**Two independent switches:** changing G can alter which capacities and prior evidence are relevant without itself changing the referent's State; changing a State at occasion o can alter which relations are *actually participating* without automatically changing G or B. If a new whole is discovered, do not project its constituents backward onto the old seat—use Compose/Reseat and two-sided reconciliation.

**This example can fail:** if a cold-reader cannot identify the focal event/capability/containing whole, demonstrate required Level witnesses or distinguish an intended participant from an observed one, its apparent functional completeness does not qualify it.
