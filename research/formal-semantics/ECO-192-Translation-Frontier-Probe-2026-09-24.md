# ECO-192 — Translation Frontier correspondence probe

24 September 2026 · Register B · controlling commission: ECO-192

## Result and practical consequence

**A genuine indexed/fibred transport fragment survives. The whole reseating operation is not established as a Cartesian lift.**

For explicitly matched evidence slots, established reindexing machinery determines what is carried and constrains identity, composition and factorization. An unmatched destination requirement cannot receive evidence from this transport. Destination disclosure, semantic bridge qualification and current-use reconciliation remain distinct operations.

This earns a concrete Shape target: separate a source-preserving transport receipt from a destination disclosure/coverage receipt, then require both at the relevant reliance boundary. It does not establish that SIGMA is category theory, that FIBERR is a fibration, or that the complete semantic field is a predetermined mathematical database.

**Principal Fulcrum answer:** yes for a precisely delimited class of unchanged, exactly attributed records under an explicit bridge; no for a claim that the bare seat-change determines the complete newly qualified semantic account. Whether a broader, useful class of claim transformations has canonical transport remains a Shape question.

**Classification:** DIRECT FORMALIZATION for the local record-family construction and its proved laws; STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE for the complete reseating architecture; HYPOTHESIS for a general seat category with canonical semantic transport. The aggregate verdict is MIXED because the restricted correspondence is established while the general semantic correspondence is not.

### Terms whose mathematical meanings matter

| Term | Meaning used in this probe |

| Fiber | The category of accounts over one fixed index. One account is an object in the fiber; it is not the whole fiber. A fiber is not a physical filament. |

| Vertical arrow | An account map that leaves the base index fixed. “Vertical” here does not mean SIGMA's holarchic dependency axis. |

## 3. Candidate mapping: the lightest serious construction

### 3.1 Keep indexing, evidence and enactment distinct

The construction models a **record-preserving comparison layer**. It does not model the entirety of reality or semantic judgment.

For a fixed local comparison, choose a record universe containing the immutable records being compared. New records may extend that universe explicitly. Unknown facts are not false facts. Records may describe uncertainty, contradiction, historical assessments and revoked authority; presenting such a record does not make it current support.

The base carries explicit interface and engagement annotations. Two engagements with the same focal referent but different questions, histories or PGO editions are not silently identified. A comparison can forget these distinctions in a display projection only if that projection is not subsequently used as the authority for qualification.

**FORMAL**

C_I = (P(U))^{O_I};

X ≤ Y ⇔ ∀o ∈ O_I, X(o) ⊆ Y(o).

**READ**

An account at index I assigns a set of immutable records to each declared slot. There is an inclusion from one account to another exactly when every slot in the first contains only records also present in that slot in the second.

**OPERATIONALLY**

Compare structured presentations while preserving their record identities and attribution. An inclusion says that one presentation contains another's records. It says nothing about improved truth, currentness, permission, or the legality of an ECOS state transition. Retraction is represented by a new attributable record and changed eligibility, not by assuming inclusion reverses time.

**Standing: DIRECT FORMALIZATION of the retained-record fragment.** Typed relational records remain records with endpoints and provenance. The construction does not independently enforce all their semantic consistency conditions.

### 3.2 Exact reindexing

**FORMAL**

f: O_J → O_I;

f*: C_I → C_J;

(f*X)(o) = X(f(o)).

**READ**

The comparison f assigns each destination slot a source slot. The corresponding reindexing operation sends a source account to a destination account by placing at each destination slot the records from its assigned source slot.

**OPERATIONALLY**

Retrieve the old records for an explicitly matched destination request. The record remains about its original subject, question and basis. Reusing it as evidence for a different proposition or current use requires an additional qualified bridge or assessment. A service-to-database refocus does not provide that bridge just because both records concern “the system.”

**Standing: DIRECT FORMALIZATION for exact retrieval; HYPOTHESIS for a wider class of qualified semantic bridges.**

The function points from destination slots to source slots because it answers “where does this requested material come from?” The real reseating event proceeds from old engagement to new engagement. These are different directions and different objects.

A bare legal refocus does not determine f. The receipt must supply exact correspondences or identify unmatched slots. Multiple candidate correspondences remain alternatives.

### 3.3 Why this is genuinely fibred, within its declared scope

For the family construction, the total category consists of pairs of an interface and an account. Over a comparison f, an arrow exists precisely when the proposed destination presentation includes only records supplied by reindexing the source.

**FORMAL**

(f, ι): (J, Y) → (I, X) exists ⇔ Y ≤ f*X.

It factors as

(J, Y) → (J, f*X) → (I, X).

**READ**

A comparison from a destination account to a source account over f exists exactly when the destination account is included in the retrieved source account. Every such comparison factors into an inclusion at the destination index followed by the canonical retrieval comparison to the source.

**OPERATIONALLY**

The inherited part of a destination account must have a recoverable source explanation. In this model there is only one inclusion map when inclusion holds, so the factorization is unique. That is the Cartesian property here. It formalizes provenance-preserving retrieval; it does not formalize an arbitrary act of investigation.

The construction over sets and functions is a genuine split indexed category and its total construction is a Grothendieck fibration. Restricting its use to semantically certified comparisons does not manufacture those certifications. Stacks gives the general indexed-to-fibred construction; the explicit inclusion model supplies the present proof.

**Standing: DIRECT FORMALIZATION of this model, not a theorem that all SIGMA transitions form this fibration.**

### 3.4 Nontrivial composition constraint

**FORMAL**

For f: O_J → O_I and g: O_L → O_J,

((f ∘ g)*X)(o) = X(f(g(o))) = (g*(f*X))(o);

id*X = X.

**READ**

Following two slot comparisons and then retrieving records gives exactly the same records as retrieving in two steps. Retrieving along an identity comparison changes nothing.

**OPERATIONALLY**

Within a frozen comparison scope, batching or splitting a purely representational transport must not alter the presented record tokens. If the two routes differ, either the implementation is wrong or some evidence, filtering, bridge, history or authority change occurred and the operation was not pure reindexing.

This is a strict equality for this explicitly chosen family representation. General chosen Cartesian lifts yield coherent isomorphisms instead; they do not require identical storage layouts. This distinction is established in Stacks Lemma 4.33.7.

**Standing: DIRECT FORMALIZATION.** The law concerns the same composite comparison, not arbitrary paths with the same endpoint labels.

### 3.5 Full change-of-base: conditional, not smuggled in

**FORMAL**

Given a functor Q: B → Set with Q(I) = O_I, define F(I) = C_I and F(f) = Q(f)*.

**READ**

If a category of certified comparisons consistently assigns each index its slot set and each comparison its slot function, the family construction can be reindexed over that category.

**OPERATIONALLY**

This would let a seat- and history-annotated comparison registry reuse the same transport mechanism. It requires identity/composition consistency for the registry's interface maps. An arbitrary disclosure routine has not been proved to supply such a functor.

**Standing: STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE; the general certified-comparison registry is a HYPOTHESIS.** A legal seat event is not automatically a base morphism with universal semantic transport.

## 4. Both Fulcrum branches

### YES — a definable transport class exists

The class consists of exactly attributed retained records presented under declared slot correspondences. Their payload, original subject, question, support and lineage are unchanged. This includes historical assessments and unchanged process-parent links. It excludes automatic retargeting of assertions, automatic current qualification and automatic creation of a Level witness.

For this class, the source account and comparison determine the transported account. The mathematics constrains composition, identity, source factorization and empty unmatched support. A destination-sensitive disclosure and reconciliation operation can be attached without forcing it to masquerade as transport.

The stronger phrase “determined by the seat-change itself” holds only if “seat-change” includes the certified comparison data and frozen applicability assumptions. It does not hold for the old/new referent names alone.

### NO — ordinary transport does not determine the full destination

There are two exact failure points.

**First mismatch: missing source correspondence.** A destination slot may ask something the old active structure never represented. A total destination-to-source map would have to invent an association, point to an explicit “no source” value, or import an expanded structure. Each is additional information or machinery. None follows merely from calling the seat-change a reindexing.

**Second mismatch: cartesianity versus real history.** A bare seat diagram may make “there and back” look invertible. A real inquiry can acquire evidence, revoke authority or change its causal predecessor while returning to the same visible seat.

**FORMAL**

If f is an isomorphism and χ is a Cartesian lift over f, then χ is an isomorphism.

**READ**

If the base comparison has a two-sided inverse, any Cartesian lift over it also has a two-sided inverse in the total category.

**OPERATIONALLY**

A model that makes seat-return inverse transport of the entire engagement would have to recover its full currentness and authority state. ECO-191 explicitly allows this to fail. Therefore either history and effects remain explicit in a richer base/account, or the whole reseating event must cease to be identified with the pure Cartesian lift. This is an actual incompatibility of that overstrong mapping, not evidence against all fibred representations.

**Standing: REJECTED MAPPING for whole-event invertible Cartesian reseating over bare seats.** The general mathematical implication is Stacks Lemma 4.33.2.

### Destination exposure and the forward direction

**FORMAL**

Disc(S′) = (O_J, A_J, Z_J);

D ⊆ O_J; f: D → O_I; N = O_J ∖ D.

**READ**

Destination disclosure returns declared slots, active requirements and unresolved discovery questions. Some destination slots have an explicit source comparison. The remaining slots are unmatched.

**OPERATIONALLY**

The destination may expose a previously dormant requirement, formulate a new request, or report a material discovery gap. Unmatched does not mean new in reality: the source presentation may simply have omitted it.

**Standing: DIRECT FORMALIZATION of the interface obligation; HYPOTHESIS for any complete or canonical disclosure algorithm.**

A forward transport is relevant when the known comparison embeds old slots into a larger destination interface. For a map from source slots to destination slots, use the following construction.

**FORMAL**

For i: O_I → O_J,

(i!X)(o) = ∪_{a ∈ O_I, i(a)=o} X(a).

**READ**

At a destination slot, forward transport collects the records of every source slot assigned to it. If no source slot is assigned there, the result is empty.

**OPERATIONALLY**

Carry the old records forward without fabricating records for new requirements. Record identity survives even when the same record has several uses; repeated presentation is not independent corroboration.

**Standing: DIRECT FORMALIZATION of forward record transport.** This is the opfibred direction for the chosen family model, not a semantic inference rule.

The relevant universal property can be stated without hiding its meaning.

**FORMAL**

i!X ≤ Y ⇔ X ≤ i*Y.

**READ**

The forwarded source account is included in a destination account exactly when the original source account is included in that destination account retrieved back along the slot comparison.

**OPERATIONALLY**

Forward transport is the least destination record presentation compatible with carrying those source records. Any extra destination evidence must be supplied separately. This universal property does not say the least presentation is sufficient for the destination's use.

**Standing: DIRECT FORMALIZATION.** Proof: either side says that each source record belongs to the destination slot assigned to its source slot.

### Which account of newly relevant obligations survives?

The proposed alternatives are not mutually exclusive.

1. **Absent from the old active interface:** an obligation was never posed there. This is compatible with its independent existence and with relevant evidence elsewhere.
2. **Latent but inactive in retained material:** the system may already possess a permission record or governing criterion that becomes active after reorientation. Activation must retain its original provenance and check currentness.
3. **Generated by destination-sensitive disclosure:** inquiry can formulate a new obligation record from new relevance, observation or reasoning. That creates a representation of a requirement; it does not manufacture the underlying reality or authority.
4. **Still undiscovered:** retain the bounded discovery question and its consequence for reliance.

A fibration's “total category” does not mean an exhaustive store of all real obligations. It collects the mathematical objects specified by the model. Equating it with SIGMA's full latent field is a **REJECTED MAPPING**.

## 7. Transport, compositionality and order

**Pure retained-record transport is functorial** for the explicit comparison maps just defined.

**Current claim reuse is partial.** A historical record always remains recoverable in its source envelope. Positive reliance may be unavailable after a changed question, use, basis, evidence or authority. A separate assessment may establish that reuse remains valid.

**General reseating is conservatively relation-valued.** Several coherent boundaries, candidate bridges or disclosures may remain admissible. ECO-191's guarded bootstrap already permits alternatives and HOLD. No universal canonical choice has been earned.

**Lax structure remains a hypothesis.** One would need specified comparison maps between composed and direct outcomes, a direction for those comparisons, and coherence laws. “New information appeared” does not by itself establish any of this.

The three continuity cases must remain separate:

| Case | Preserved identity | Additional burden |
|---|---|---|
| Same referent, revised boundary | Source referent identity and old boundary-indexed claims | Explicit boundary revision; reclassification and claim applicability |
| Different existing referent | Both original and destination identities | Explicit relation/bridge; the old assertion remains about the old subject |
| Newly individuated composite | Constituent identities and formation lineage; composite identity established at the needed semantic resolution | Coherent organizing criterion, lawful individuation and then seating; no automatic physical row or Level |

SelfFocus is the same grammar once the structure has a legitimate referential designation. It neither needs a special meta-category nor proves that all mathematical categories or all truths are objects inside the runtime.

### When order matters

Refocus and reorientation can select different target boundaries depending on order. Discovery before reentry may expose a missing permission that reentry-before-discovery had not considered. Authority revocation before a requested use differs from a historical authorized use before revocation. The full histories remain different even if final displays agree.

**FORMAL**

If two comparison paths induce the same slot function h, then their pure record retrievals both equal h*X.

**READ**

When two paths give exactly the same slot assignment, retrieving the same source account along either path gives the same record presentation.

**OPERATIONALLY**

This permits a narrow commute test after freezing the actual comparison and records. It does not permit changing the order of PGO enrichment, bridge qualification, disclosure or authority transitions without checking their guards and effects.

**Standing: DIRECT FORMALIZATION for frozen retrieval.**

A proposed operational commute requires independent read/write effects, stable guards, stable discovered requirements, compatible authority and an explicitly chosen observation scope. Even then equal projected content does not make the historical event sequences identical.

### An exact limit on composition beyond the frozen fragment

Suppose an intermediate presentation drops an obligation and later disclosure restores it. A sequential process can lose a candidate record from the active view, while a direct lookup from the retained ledger can find it. That does not falsify the reindexing theorem: the two routes did not carry the same full account through the same slot maps. It falsifies an implementation that treats a lossy active projection as the retained evidence store.

A stronger claim-transport calculus must declare whether it preserves all candidate material or only currently eligible support, and how later requalification revisits previously excluded material.

## 9. Exact gains, mismatches and Shape leverage

The mathematical gains are specific:

**First, transport gains a testable universal property.** “Preserves structure” becomes a source-factorization requirement for an explicitly delimited record layer. A transported presentation cannot conceal where its inherited records came from.

**Second, composition becomes a falsifier.** Under frozen comparisons, direct and two-step retrieval must agree, strictly in this model or through specified coherent isomorphisms in a more general representation.

**Third, forward extension explains the empty destination burden.** The least record-preserving forward account carries no evidence into an unmatched slot. Supplementing it is an additional operation, and the two-extension countermodel shows why source coverage cannot decide destination coverage.

**Fourth, invertibility exposes a false history collapse.** If a model makes a round-trip seat change invertible and treats every whole-event transfer as Cartesian, it cannot preserve ECO-191's irreversible currentness distinctions.

The exact remaining mismatches are:

1. **Seat event versus comparison map:** legal refocus does not uniquely identify a semantic bridge.
2. **Presentation versus warrant:** correct record transport is not current semantic qualification.
3. **Disclosed interface versus actual field:** a finite known interface does not exhaust destination reality.
4. **Cartesian comparison versus discovery:** acquisition, retraction and requalification need not have universal factorization.
5. **Visible endpoint versus history:** equal displayed seats or payloads do not establish equal authority/currentness.
6. **General composition:** stable lawful transport for richer claim structures has not been shown.
