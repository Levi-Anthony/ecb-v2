## 3. Agreed direction and prototype lineage

### What is preserved from the Principal's intervention

1. Audit how well each term is defined before making coordinates look definitionally symmetric.
2. Locate the PGO, intended world effect, participants and inquiry before deriving from an example. The primary upstream Integral disposition makes isolated object reasoning rapidly underspecified.
3. Maintain contact with folded referents and alternatives, why they are folded and what would unfold them. Radical non-reductivism permits different human, agent and persistence representations; it does not require selecting one representation as universally best.
4. Investigate why “referent” has no obvious “signifier” counterpart. The absence is an open explanatory/design question, not an instruction to add that primitive.
5. Make stable reference nimble: rapidly compose, decompose and redraw boundaries as the purpose requires while the underlying referent remains recoverable.

### Repaired target and consequence

**Earlier formulation:** a focal seat written as (R, grain, boundary), three change intentions plus receipts, illustrated by a car and then its fuel pump.

**Demonstrated mismatch:** that presentation implied comparable definition maturity for all three coordinates and reasoned about an object change without a sufficiently located governing purpose. It also used one “boundary” where constitutive individuation, consequential composition and view coverage could do different work. Those shortcomings affect the architectural model, not only the prose.

**Consequence:** use the situated porch inquiry and the accepted URG index as the active basis. Preserve independent addressability and explicit coupling, while treating grain and the boundary discriminators as live questions. The old specimen and tuple remain prototype history; their unsituated results do not establish qualification.

**Accepted situated index:** `κ=(R,B,G,F)` — read “kappa equals focal referent, boundary, governing orientation, and mapper/frame/access.” Here **G means governing orientation, not grain**. This is an inquiry index, not a database schema or exhaustive engagement record. Grain must remain distinguishable wherever expressed.

Independent changeability means a coordinate can be addressed and a change proposed independently. It does not mean changes are semantically uncoupled. Warranted covariance is allowed when the dependency and before/after relations are explicit.

### Constructive-dual strategy

Retain one contract with two complementary surfaces:

- **Positive guarantee:** the capacity or relation faithful operation enables.
- **Inherited clause / failure condition:** the established loss of fidelity by which the guarantee can be challenged.
- **Proposed witness:** a situated operation capable of exposing correspondence or violation.

A grammatical rewrite alone would leave the design question unchanged. A logical negation would generally assert a different proposition. The selected direction is a **constructive dual**; mathematical equivalence has not been proved and is not claimed.

### Boundary alternatives — retained together

- **Constitutive/individuation boundary:** what is admitted as constitutive of this porch under the declared criterion.
- **Consequential composition:** what must enter consideration to prepare it for painting; furniture may matter without becoming constitutive in the sense of a load-bearing post.
- **View coverage:** what a checklist, agent query, photograph or display selects.

The candidate decomposition is **B + PGO-governed disclosure/composition + projection coverage**. Keep it folded as the reuse hypothesis because the existing contracts may already suffice. Unfold a new architecture question only when a situated case cannot express a consequential distinction without overloading an existing coordinate. Keeping the alternatives live does not require one persisted object for each.

### Grain alternatives — retained together

- Descriptive resolution: “furniture” versus identified chairs, table and planters.
- Action-discrimination resolution: which differences may alter the required treatment.
- Compositional resolution: porch, railing, baluster or fastening point as the current unit of consideration.

Candidate positive discriminator:

> Grain is the resolution at which differences are presently permitted to affect the relevant inference, composition or action.

This is a workshop candidate. A compositional case may defeat its sufficiency. Focalizing a baluster is not automatically merely a finer description of the porch.

### Signifying-role hypothesis

The architecture needs a universal subject anchor across heterogeneous representations. It may not need one universal Signifier object type: a phrase refers, a photograph depicts, a foreign key points, and a description asserts different native relations. Each representing artifact can itself later become a referent.

That is an architectural hypothesis explaining the current asymmetry, not proof that “Signifier” can never be needed. Unfold it when a consumer requires a distinction that existing artifact/relation/role semantics cannot preserve.

### Source account

### Inherited versus stipulated detail

**Inherited:** shared porch handle; painting purpose; moving/protecting furniture/items; encounter-based composition; preparation-to-paint-target projection change; continuity/succession question.

**Not supplied by the source:** a particular painting operator, their permission, material/finish, precise paint targets, time/spend limits, measured safety conditions, completion criteria or full constituent inventory.

These are folded distinctions. Capture does not require inventing them. Consequential derivation requires unfolding whichever distinction changes the answer, governing obligation or permission for the requested use.

**Assistant-invented variations:** a bolted planter, adjacent-wall overspray, a rotten board, named preparation workers and comparison with other porch purposes. They are proposed discriminators below, not new facts about the source porch.

### Folding contract under investigation

A folded account retains contact with its subject and known possibilities without requiring permanent exhaustive expansion. Where a distinction bears on the current result, retain or recover its subject/basis, standing, why folded, dependencies, unfolding condition and return route.

Keep a distinction folded while the active PGO licenses the same inference/composition/action across the unresolved alternatives. Unfold it when purpose, encountered condition, dependency, disagreement, downstream consumer or materially disconfirming evidence changes the required distinction.

This is local sufficiency under the named inquiry, not deletion of what is presently inactive. F5 and F8's inherited possibility/challenge obligations continue to apply. Unknown possibilities are not represented as an already enumerated complete catalogue.

### Revised positive F1 — current workshop formulation

> A referent remains stably recoverable across situated recomposition. Purpose-relative boundaries, constituent sets, resolutions, relations, and projections may be composed, decomposed, folded, unfolded, and redrawn without silently replacing the referent or making any current projection exhaustive. Where a transformation genuinely constitutes a different referent, the succession or relation is made explicit and the prior referent remains recoverable.

**Core design direction:** stable identity supports nimble, recoverable situated recomposition. No current view has monopoly over what the referent is.

“Reversible” in the earlier explanatory shorthand means recovery of prior identity, basis and lineage and the ability to construct another view. It does not assert physical reversibility, uniquely invertible decomposition, or lossless reconstruction of undisclosed facts.

### Three representation doors

| Door | Candidate surface | Fidelity relationship |

Representation-first convenience is not a reason to erase differences. Different encodings are valid when their relevant common reference and distinct jobs remain recoverable.

The working hybrid is relation-preserving structure, coordinate-seat views and typed qualification. This is a compositional hypothesis, not a mandatory topology or selection of one universally correct representation.
