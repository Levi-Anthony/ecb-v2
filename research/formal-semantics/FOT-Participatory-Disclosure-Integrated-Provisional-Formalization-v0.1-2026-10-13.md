## 2. Provisional positive formalization — the smallest adequate difference

**Seating index (existing grammar):** `κ=(R,B,G,F)` where R is **one focal referent**, B its boundary/identity, G the active PGO and F the mapper/frame/access. Add independently attributable occasion `t` and relevant conditions through existing situated claims, not a new mandatory tuple field.

**Native relation carrier (existing grammar):** `RelClaim(ρ,A,S,β,standing)` where ρ names the domain relation/predicate, A has ordered role-bearing referents, S is a native source/edition, β is a situated basis including applicable conditions/time, standing is warrant/authority/currentness. More than one connected claim may be needed. **No source field is self-validating, and the same record carrier does not merge distinct disclosures.**

### 2.1 Two independently attributable candidate propositions (meta-notation only)

For a fixed `κ,t`, interpret these shorthand propositions using existing URG native claims, not a new API, Boolean, ontology or installed validator:

- `PL(κ,t; D,ρ,A,S,β,W)`: **participatory-field articulation** *locally warranted* for R when (i) the implicated determination D of **this R** is correctly seated, (ii) a **domain-native field relation** ρ with roles A and source S actually applies to R at β/t, (iii) ρ yields a recoverable, discriminating distinction such as meaning, permission, incompatibility, implication, obligation, expectation, consequence or opposition **for this R**, (iv) the distinction has attributable W (standing, source, currentness, and relevant alternative), and (v) the qualification is not merely the mapper's proposed meaning. This is the **Participatory Left (LL)** inquiry/witness condition *in a particular domain*, not a definition claiming every domain is norm-governed or conscious.
- `PR(κ,t; O,C,W')`: **participatory enacted/sustained organization** *locally warranted* for **the same R** when actual organization O of participation (including stable operative arrangements, roles, interactions, effects, practices, couplings, ongoing constraints or transformations) obtains under conditions C/t with attributable evidence W'. This is the **Participatory Right (LR)** inquiry/witness condition; mere metadata/documentation, promised behavior, conceptual possibility or future intention do not independently witness actual enactment.

**These shorthands are deliberately not iff-ontologies or a complete mechanical decision procedure.** A single native relation may give evidence relevant to both; the **claimed positive function and inferential basis** must still be separately recoverable. For R which is a nonhuman program/service, D means the service's pertinent determination under the native account, **not assumed subjective experience**. “Field” is a source-qualified relational account, not a second secretly focal entity or necessarily a human community. Its **specific distinctions and consequences** must be named: “related in some way” is insufficient.

**Core working clause (provisionally selected for FOT compilation):**

> **Participatory disclosure** concerns the same situated referent R through two irreducible correlated positive inquiries. **Participatory Left (LL)** discloses domain-native articulated relations of distinction, meaning, compatibility, implication or obligation through which R's determinations acquire consequential significance in a field. **Participatory Right (LR)** discloses the actual organized participation through which such determinations are produced, coordinated, carried, sustained or transformed. Qualify each through its own positively specified native criterion, source/standing and conditions; preserve their mutual relevance but do not infer either from the other's report. An intention, communicated agreement, conflict, source protocol or implementation can contribute differently depending on its actual warranted role; **none is a universal necessity or automatic sufficient condition for both**. The PGO governs purpose, significance, method and stopping, without constituting a claimed relation merely by naming it. Preserve the other two irreducible Individual disclosures over the **same R/B**. If a decision-bearing field or enacted relation is unresolved, return a specific disclosure-oriented Question Forward with a hypothetical event warranting reopening its exact variable, plus an owner and stopping condition.

### 2.2 Consequential separations that compilation must never lose

1. **Intention as a possible determination versus its field relation.** “A privately intends X” can be a qualified Individual Left proposition without proving A/B share X; an independently warranted native rule/expectation/conflict can make X relevant to Participatory Left. **Mutual endorsement is not always required** (e.g. an acknowledged refusal can supply a relevant opposition); nor can A's private desire manufacture B's acceptance.
2. **The field source versus its actual local applicability.** The *existence* of RFC 9110 is evidence of normative HTTP semantics but not that every deployed S has adopted the specific local conditional-update policy. A program's source documentation, advertised agreement, installed configuration and actually governing rule are distinct attribution/standing questions.
3. **Articulated compatibility versus actual coupling.** The meaning of an adopted `If-Match` constraint differs from actually performing its comparison; an acknowledged dance pause rule differs from present B responsiveness. Failure to perform required behavior can be meaningful as *violation* without voiding the stipulated field rule. Mere absence of one request event or dance step is **not** proof that all Participatory Right organization is absent.
4. **Private intention versus joint enacted coordination.** Two stipulated worlds can contain the same A intention and same accessible t0 cues but different actual B responsiveness. The correct statement of the scene mapper's knowledge may be **unresolved** even when the experimenter knows the invented world condition. Evidence produced at t1 by A's intervention cannot retroactively prove t0 pre-intervention enactment.
5. **Quad coordinate versus independent Level, Direction and State.** At R=A or R=S, LL/LR speak about that *focal referent*; a co-relatum B/C or containing whole H/X is not a quadrant by type or source. Constitution of H requires its own positive Level criterion after explicit Reseat. Vertical UP/DOWN attention and actual formed/ceased event are distinct. State/occasion changes and new conditions may requalify the two Participatory accounts without erasing the previous one.
6. **Semantics versus observation versus standing.** A logical constructed counterexample establishes bounded independence under its stipulations, not a measured real-world result or host-consumer proof. A structural `QUADRANT_POSITION` with the right coordinates alone does not qualify the source meaning; a `QUADRANT_UNKNOWN` only remains decision-complete when `qf_ref` actually dereferences to a well-formed calibrated question.

**UNKNOWN / DEFERRED / HOLD on a consequential variable:** Status **alone is MALFORMED as closure**. State exact question and whether it concerns field applicability, native meaning, current operation, consumer projection or contract authority; identify primary positive disclosure, possible discriminating evidence, an explicit hypothetical reopening scenario for **that very variable**, alternatives, owner, return and stop. **Reader action:** follow the QF/PGO repair and revise the same source/claim path. **INAPPLICABLE / DORMANT:** Justify the native criterion/PGO by which it does not currently apply or lacks consequence, identify change conditions that would reactivate relevance and owner. **Reader action:** reevaluate on that condition; never treat lack of active request as elimination of the universal LL/LR disclosure obligations.
