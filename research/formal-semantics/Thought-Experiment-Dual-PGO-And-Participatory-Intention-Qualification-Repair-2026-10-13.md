## 1. Positive protocol for constructed inquiry: two PGO roles

**PGO-I — inquiry / experiment orientation.** Governs *our act of investigating*. Recover: focal semantic variable, why its discrimination is consequential to the intended consumer, point of view and mapper F, compared cases and controls, applicable source contracts, what would support/undermine interpretations, useful output, sufficiency/stopping and return. The intended result is a **well-qualified distinction**; it is not a preselected answer. Its success conditions must not be smuggled into the world being imagined as facts.

**PGO-S — scenario / situated situation orientation.** Governs *the particular hypothetical world examined*. Recover: focal referent(s), containing whole, purpose operating **within the scene**, native participant tasks/goals, identity and persistence conditions, scope/boundary, relevant occasion/State, success/failure criteria, credible realizing mechanisms, and the conditions under which a participant's contribution actually advances or undermines that scene's end. It supplies a domain-native qualification basis for hypothetical results, **not proof** that a stipulated actor has the internal intention, current readiness, enacted relation or actual observed evidence.

**Relation:** PGO-S is situated/nested under PGO-I for design and comparison, not a second sovereign authority whose output ratifies the inquiry. Both are *genuinely* PGOs in their respective referent/role and can themselves be reseated/refined under the PGO-throughout law. The scenario's outcome-validity condition is different from the experiment's epistemic success criterion. Neither prescribes ontology by labeling. If several scenes are compared, hold PGO-I fixed and specify whether each PGO-S is the same controlled situated end with a changed condition or a genuinely different scenario; do not silently treat these as equivalent.

**Critical third notion, without automatically adding a third compulsory PGO:** An **individual participant's intention** may be an actual domain claim within PGO-S. It need not coincide with PGO-S's externally stipulated design purpose. One could intend to continue dancing while not actually coordinating, or participate through an established responsive practice without explicitly formulating that intention verbally. Do not deduce participant intention from experimenter purposes or from an outcome label; investigate any consequential intention claim with appropriate UL/UR/Participatory disclosures and source standing.

**Positive qualification procedure:** state PGO-I and PGO-S separately; seat R/B/occasion and scenario participants; declare native success criteria and which variables may change; independently qualify current intention, organization, response and event continuity where relevant; use paired counterfactuals/evidence to test the particular proposed relation; return either a bounded positive discriminator, reasoned counterexample, or exact disclosure-oriented QF plus hypothetical reopening trigger. **HELPS/UNDERMINES below are non-exhaustive examples**, not criterion lists.

## 2. Repaired dance inquiry — two PGOs specified before conclusion

### PGO-I: our experiment

**Focal question / purpose:** Qualify whether sustained **operative participation** by A during a partnered dance pause is explained by individual intention alone, actual maintained relational organization, their conjunction, or a broader PGO-governed concept. The target is a reusable *positively specified and source-adequate* discriminator for Participatory disclosure, distinguishing scene-level purpose, individual intention, actual coordination and retrospective record.

**Definition of done:** A formulation that discriminates (i) intention with continuing interaction, (ii) intention without operative coupling, (iii) enacted/sustained participation without explicitly articulated intention, and (iv) a historical record whose relation is no longer operative; it must not mis-seat R, substitute H/P identity, promote hypothetical events to observations or fix intention as the answer by definition. Return its source/warrant and precise unresolved QF if underdetermined.

**Mapper and conditions:** current assistant reasoning under accepted Principal revisions, drawing on [Quadrant v3](../urg-kernel/Quadrant-Disclosure-Contract-v3.0.md), [PGO first profile](PGO-Directed-Situated-Referent-Orchestration-Profile-v0.1.md), and existing source-qualified [cross-axis return](First-Operational-Theory-Level-Lower-Direction-Cross-Axis-Reconciliation-2026-10-13.md). These are thought experiments, not sensor observations. All four Quadrant obligations remain for A.

### PGO-S: the specific hypothetical dance occasion

**Focal scenario:** Individuated dancer A, co-relatum B, one partnered dance episode H during a musical phrase, at a bounded pause of approximately three seconds. **Containing purpose within the scene:** successfully complete a jointly coordinated phrase, retaining safe, mutually usable timing/readiness for continuing movement together when the musical cue resumes. The native *success criterion* is coordinated continuation without a new invitation or a unilateral disruptive movement. **Identity/continuity:** the episode H may persist across a short pause under its stipulated agreed dance-event continuity conditions; a durable partnership P, if there is one, is a separate referent. **Material variable intentionally left open:** whether A and B in fact maintain reciprocal responsiveness/operative coupling at the pause. The scenario PGO does **not** assert that such coupling exists merely by specifying it as useful or required for success.

**Qualified positive inquiry at R=A:** In the scene's actual stipulated condition, what is A's own lived/intended orientation (potentially relevant UL)? What is A's differentiated posture, response, timing or availability (UR)? What articulated field of musical/conventional obligations/expectations shapes A's relation to B (Participatory Left, LL)? What actual responsive organization, including maintained coupling through stillness if present, carries A's participation (Participatory Right, LR)? Each is independently interrogated; no coordinate is a type of actor and none is suppressed when its content is unknown.

### Discriminating condition variations — same inquiry PGO and scene goal

| Constructed variation | A's intention | Actual operative coordination under the stipulated scene | What it tests / bounded inference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **V1 — reciprocal pause** | Both partners privately intend to continue (stipulated) | Mutual responsive readiness/coupling remains, and they coordinate resumption (stipulated) | Positive candidate for sustained participation; *whether* private intent was necessary is not proven |
| **V2 — intention without coupling** | A intends to resume (stipulated) | The supposed responsive relationship has been interrupted; B is not actually coordinating with A at that moment (stipulated) | Shows intention alone does not establish current **reciprocal operative** organization, even if A's own intention genuinely obtains |
| **V3 — unarticulated organized response** | No explicit introspectively reportable continuation plan stipulated | A/B remain bodily responsive through a learned joint practice and demonstrably resume in coordination (stipulated) | Challenges any test requiring *consciously stated* intention; do not assert absence of all intentionality |
| **V4 — archival relation** | Either dancer may remember or desire future contact | Only a record of past dancing is established; no source-backed presently effective relation has been stipulated | Shows a historical record does not **by itself** witness currently enacted participation; absence of evidence is not automatically evidence of absent effects |

**Controlled validity caution:** V2 changes actual reciprocity and could change the scene's eventual success and even H's continuity. Those changes are **outcomes to qualify** using PGO-S and native H/B conditions, not assumptions that H persists unchanged. V4 may be a different occasion/state; compare it explicitly, not as a supposedly identical pause. If the case were restated as two different purposes (e.g. complete current H versus someday dance again), declare a **different PGO-S** rather than hiding the difference.

**HELPS — non-exhaustive examples:** actual interdependent response, shared timing constraint, a binding convention with operative present consequences, state-dependent mutual responsiveness, source-qualified event-persistence rule, explicit awareness/intention where positively warranted. **Reader action:** determine exactly which native intended/actual proposition each clue supports at the scene occasion.

**UNDERMINES — non-exhaustive examples:** private intent with no reciprocal coordination; goal description mistaken for occurrence; archived partnership treated as current enacted relation; posture/readiness asserted without attribution; a different PGO-S silently substituted between cases; assuming an explicitly unreported intention is completely absent. **Reader action:** repair the exact world assumption or PGO-S control, then requalify under PGO-I instead of asserting a convenient answer.

## 3. Provisional distinction and calibrated Question Forward

**Working inference:** A PGO may carry and resolve the *functional significance* of a participating act, including the intention or end to which coordination is organized. But **PGO-governance of significance** and **the actuality of an operative relationship** are independently qualified. The scenario's goal does not cause coordination to exist, and a participant's personal intention need not be the single universally sufficient observable of participation. Some continuing operative organization may be meaningful despite stillness or without an explicitly stated conscious plan.

**Candidate—NOT yet a qualified universal definition:** “Present operative participation is a currently obtaining, source-warranted, situated relationship through which R's determinations are effectively coordinated, constrained, enabled or propagated with co-relata in relation to the active PGO, even when overt action is temporarily absent.” Treat “effectively” and “currently obtaining” as prompts for *positive native test and consequences*, not naked evaluative gates. This candidate may still fail if a legitimate participatory field without effective operative coupling is improperly erased (Participatory Left) or a relevant but not yet active affordance is flattened into nonexistence.

**QF-PART-01b — intention, PGO mediation and operative participation.**
- **Variable and occasion:** With R=A in fixed dance PGO-S, across the three-second pause, is actual present mutual responsiveness **necessary** for claiming enacted/sustained Participatory Right participation, and what independent role does A's intention play? Keep Participatory Left's articulated field as a separate positive obligation.
- **Calibrated LR question:** *At the pause, which native interaction or sustained responsive constraint still actually shapes A's available and realized coordination with B, as distinct from A privately intending to dance again?* **Correlated LL:** *Which source-qualified expectations and meaning-relations actually bind A's participation at that moment, even if no visible motion occurs?* **Correlated UL/UR:** What is warranted of A's own lived intention and actual response profile, without treating one as a proxy for the other?
- **Discriminators/evidence:** Compare V1/V2/V3 with fixed scenario goal; inspect attributed reciprocal responsiveness, continuity rule, time-specific organization, articulation/standing; withhold claims about intention not actually supplied by the hypothetical or a qualified observation.
- **Explicit hypothetical reopening trigger:** Suppose a tightly specified real or constructed human/nonhuman case demonstrates successful *current* organized participatory effects across a pause despite absence of measurable reciprocal responsiveness, or demonstrates strong declared mutual intention with no operative coupling but warranted current LR participation under a valid alternative native criterion. If warranted, reopen exactly the proposed necessity of responsive coupling for **enacted** participation; repair the native criterion without automatically negating the LL field or reclassifying Level.
- **Return/stop:** First Operational Theory Shape / Participatory disclosure qualification, with the [Quadrant v3 source](../urg-kernel/Quadrant-Disclosure-Contract-v3.0.md) as control. Stop once the proposed necessary/sufficient claim has a source-qualified positive discriminator or a narrower, explicitly located QF. No automatic mechanical rename or hosted qualification claim.
