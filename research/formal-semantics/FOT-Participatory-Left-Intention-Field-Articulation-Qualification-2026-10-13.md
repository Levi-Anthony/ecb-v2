## 1. Positive PGO-I and PGO-S before discriminating the field

**PGO-I — external semantic qualification.** **Mapper:** current Principal and assisting research worker; access to the governing contract and to invented case facts, not to actual people's minds. **Focal:** the positive *relation* between A's intention and A's Participatory Left articulated significance, compared with A's Individual Left intention and Participatory Right operative coordination. **Initiating defect:** a prior assistant-inferred “intention” answer would collapse private aim, situation-purpose and social meaning, or could require actual dyadic movement before field meaning exists. **Purpose / magic-wand outcome:** a reader can discriminate which real or stipulated situated field relations give an intention determinate significance, without making the PGO, an intention or a statement *manufacture* that relation. **Done:** controlled comparisons show how a particular intention can have different field status with identical private intention and/or identical operative coupling; distinct LL/LR statuses, evidence and a remaining falsifying case are explicit. **Stop:** a qualified local positive inference plus actionable QF, not a universal psychological theory.

**PGO-S — approved situated dance inquiry, with one controlled case enrichment.** **Mapper:** dancer A. **Object:** A's current participatory field significance and enacted responsiveness vis-à-vis B during a two-to-three-second musical pause in the same constructed event H. **Initiating contrast:** B's expected response is not reliably interpretable; A may force action by mistaking stillness for consent or unnecessarily abandon a jointly responsive pause. **Locally governing purpose:** choose the next *safe, mutually responsive or respectful* action in the current phrase; successful end = accurately use available evidence to invite, clarify, maintain the pause or conclude without overriding B's agency. **Access:** A's own private intention and situated bodily/music/contact cues, the memory of any actual prior agreement or field convention, and communicative feedback; A cannot automatically access B's private intention. **Native rules for this experiment:** both dancers remain individually individuated; H identity/persistence is a separate native question; stable readiness can persist without overt locomotion; unobserved world facts are stipulated only for the external experimenter's thought experiment. **Control:** distinguish t0 before any probe from t1 after it; a probe can produce coupling that did not obtain at t0.

**Discernment HELPS — non-exhaustive examples and actions:** an actually established shared meaning/rule; credible partner acknowledgment; a currently governing, attributable dance convention or role; differentiated consequences for whether A may initiate movement; retained mutual feedback/response where present. **Action:** identify *which proposition* the cue supports (personal intention, A's relational obligation, mutual commitment, or actual operative coupling), its source, timing and what contrary cue would require revision.

**Discernment HINDERS — non-exhaustive examples and actions:** A's private preference being promoted into “B agreed”; an imaginary field norm being treated as observed; describing identical outward posture as identical field meaning; confusing prospective agreement with past commitment; making A's tentative invitation prove earlier dyadic coordination; declaring Participatory Left absent because Participatory Right mutual coupling is inactive. **Action:** restore field source and standing, isolate the changing predicate/occasion, qualify positive difference or complete a calibrated QF. A list item is a diagnostic invitation, not a sufficient/necessary gate.

## 2. Exact candidate of positive function

> **Working positive formulation (bounded and open to challenge):** At fixed R/B, Participatory Left discloses the *actually articulated relational field* in which R's determinations—including possible intention—acquire consequential distinctions, compatibility, obligations, meanings, or implications under the active PGO. An intention is significant **in that disclosure** insofar as an independently qualified relation links it to those domain-native distinctions and what follows for R's participation. The relation may be an acknowledged commitment, established shared practice, role-bearing obligation, meaningful disagreement or asymmetrically held expectation; no single form is presumed universal. The local mapper's orientation and scenario's purpose govern which distinctions are consequential to investigate but do **not** themselves create the field or establish B's acceptance. Participatory Right separately asks what organized coupling, practice, arrangement or propagation actually occurs or is sustained at the same occasion.

**Three needed non-identities:**
1. **A intends X (Individual Left)** is not by itself **X is acknowledged/required/possible/meaningful in A's field (Participatory Left)**. With source-qualified context, the *same* intention can enter an actually articulated field, including as a violation of another's expectation. There is no categorical requirement that it be mutually endorsed to be *relationally significant*.
2. **An articulated field relation obtains (Participatory Left)** is not by itself **a particular actual mutual coupling is enacted (Participatory Right)**. A valid rule or obligation can constrain what ought to happen even while the prescribed behavior is absent; other LR arrangements may nonetheless still obtain.
3. **Both disclosures concern A at fixed R/B.** When H (the dance event) is intentionally centered, Reseat and requalify H's four disclosures. Neither LL nor LR is a Level witness, global classifier or property of “the other dancer B.”

**Standing note:** The formulation's frame is source-supported by v3; its use to classify the imagined cases below is a *stipulated native* demonstration, not independent empirical validation or ratified universality. A and B can have richer experiential or normative relations than the small controlled cases model.

## 3. Controlled positive discrimination — retain PGO-I, PGO-S, A and t0

Each row is an explicit **fictional stipulation** for the experimenter. The exact native time t0, A/B individuation, A-as-mapper, dance purpose, music phrase and safety rule remain unchanged unless specified. A's knowledge differs from the experimenter's stipulated world truth; any unstipulated cue stays unknown. These tests alter **field articulation**, **private intention** and **current dyadic enactment** independently, as far as stipulated conditions allow.

### Comparison F1/F2: same intention and t0 coupling, different articulated significance

**Shared stipulated world:** A privately intends to resume at the next phrase. A and B maintain equivalent t0 contact and actual responsive readiness; gross movement is paused. The experiment *deliberately holds the relevant LR dyadic coupling constant* for this comparison and independently specifies a field condition.

- **F1 — acknowledged convention:** Earlier in H, both A and B explicitly agreed that a particular stillness/hold cue means “wait until either partner positively invites continuation”; the agreement continues to govern t0. **Qualified Participatory Left difference:** A's current intention encounters a reciprocal *permission/choice distinction* and obligation not to treat quiet as assent. The local mapper A can invoke this rule if the agreement is reliably remembered. The rule's existence and current applicability are independently stipulated, not inferred from posture.
- **F2 — no such particular convention:** A has the *same* personal intention and A/B have the *same* stipulated current responsive organization, but they did **not** agree to that particular cue meaning; only the general safety/respect terms remain. **Qualified Participatory Left difference:** the *particular negotiated meaning* and its corresponding permission/choice obligation from F1 do not obtain here. Other musical, consent and interpretive field distinctions still exist. The absence of this exact agreement does **not** mean LL disappears or that A can move without B's actual responsiveness.

**Finding F:** Intention and present overt/operative dyadic responsiveness do not by themselves settle which *articulated normative meaning* obtains. An independently qualified field relation discriminates F1/F2. This is not the trivial claim that language replaces physical evidence: the experiment stipulates the native rule independently and keeps the two PGO purposes fixed. If a future consequential action diverges because of the rule, requalify subsequent LR at t1 rather than claiming t0 had been different.

### Comparison F1/F3: same articulated rule, different actual coordination

- **F3 — acknowledged rule but B disengaged:** Preserve exactly F1's shared, presently applicable agreed cue meaning, A's intention and the current occasion. Stipulate that **B is no longer reciprocally ready to continue** at t0, and initial available cues to A remain equivocal. The rule still articulates what a respectful invitation/withdrawal would mean for A; the specific *mutually operative dyadic coupling* described in F1 does not obtain. A's warranted judgment remains uncertain at t0.
- **Finding G:** A field rule can remain positive and decision-bearing in Participatory Left while a **particular** dyadic responsive organization is absent in Participatory Right. This does not mean no LR participation whatsoever exists—A may still participate in other operative arrangements such as the music event or floor etiquette. The accepted source promise and actual enactment are different propositions; their apparent conflict is intelligible.

### Comparison F1/F4: same enacted coupling and shared field rule, different private conscious report

- **F4 — unarticulated individual plan:** Preserve F1's maintained responsiveness and acknowledged field rule; do not stipulate a consciously reportable “I plan to resume” from A at t0. A may respond fluently under embodied habits, or be uncertain about future movement; **the absence of an explicit plan report does not establish absence of all intention or orientation**.
- **Finding H:** A's articulate report of private intention is **not a necessary carrier** for qualifying the stipulated shared LL rule or operative LR responsive coupling. This neither proves those disclosures independent of all forms of intentionality nor settles the nature of pre-reflective intention.

### Comparison F2/F5: unilateral or conflicting intention without mutual endorsement

- **F5 — A intends to resume; B explicitly wishes to stop:** Fix R=A, PGO-S and current occasion; posit a directly communicated B refusal that A receives, and A's still-private intention to resume. There is **no present shared commitment to resume**. Nevertheless the *articulated disagreement*, legitimate choice boundary and consequence for A's next action are positive *Participatory Left* relations if the underlying scene norms/source support them. A's intention is **relationally consequential through its opposition to B's communicated position**, not a collective intention and not evidence of mutual LR coupling.
- **Finding I:** Reciprocal acknowledgment or harmony is not a universal gate for intention's relational significance. It can enter LL as conflict or noncompliance under a warranted field. The private orientation by itself is not the LL witness; the *qualified relation to a field distinction* is.

**Control warning:** F5 changes A's access and the world's B state relative to F2; it is an **adjacent contrast**, not an identical-conditions causal isolation. The experimenter must identify which result depends on B's newly available refusal before claiming a general necessity/sufficiency law.

## 5. Decision, QF and real return path

**Selected at bounded source-qualified Shape scope:** In the accepted dance scenario, **intention can be a consequential contribution to Participatory Left when an independently qualified situated field relation gives it articulated meaning, permission/obligation, compatibility, or conflict**. Intention *alone* does not certify mutual shared commitment, and Participatory Left is **not reducible to intention**. Participatory Right must separately qualify enacted/stable organization. Positive F1/F2 and F1/F3 prove the *logical separability within a well-specified hypothetical model*, not empirical facts about dancers or universal ontology.

**QF-PL-01 — further reach of positive field articulation.**
- **Variable:** Whether the above *field linkage* account captures nonconscious, conceptual, nonhuman and delayed-commitment instances of Participatory Left without importing human intention or mistakenly asserting current LR reciprocal enactment.
- **PGO-I / mapper:** For a chosen new native domain, qualify LL's positive articulated relations independently of the dance-specific intent/consent assumptions. **Calibrated Participatory Left question:** *What independently warranted distinctions, compatibility relations or consequences actually position the same R within its relevant field, with what source and occasion, regardless of whether R has a personally reportable intention?*
- **PGO-S requirement:** Choose and specify an actual in-scene mapping function/actor or the honest absence of one, referent/boundary, initiating contrast, magic-wand end, valid native qualification instructions, HELPS/HINDERS non-exhaustive example cues, access and stop. Do not use a merely thematic analogy.
- **Hypothetical discriminating reopening trigger:** Suppose a domain-native, editioned protocol supplies relationally binding semantic compatibility between two nonconscious program components while no source warrants either's mental intention, or a long-delayed commitment continues to constrain a participant's legitimate acts even without an operative reciprocal exchange at this occasion. If it distinguishes warranted positive LL but the candidate rule cannot express that without hallucinating an intentional mind or a live LR coupling, reopen precisely the *field linkage clause* and revise/requalify through Quadrant v3.
- **Owner/route/stop:** FOT Shape Participatory semantic owner, with Quadrant source authority preserved; stop on independently qualified positive case/countercase or a smaller QF. Neither scene is asserted real or a commissioned deployment experiment.

**QF-PL-02 — standing of a unilateral intention in a field.**
- **Variable:** Whether a privately unexpressed A intention has *present* relational significance at t0 absent reciprocal acknowledgment, overt effects and any specified normative rule about private intentions.
- **Calibrated Participatory Left question:** *Which qualified field distinction, if any, gives A's unexpressed aim consequential significance now, and whose evidence can actually establish that distinction?* **Correlated Individual Left:** What does A actually intend, with what accessible standing? **Correlated Participatory Right:** What current organization, if any, is actually affected, without projecting future consequences backwards?
- **Hypothetical reopening trigger:** An independently stipulated, source-qualified native practice in which unexpressed private commitments actually establish binding duties **without public communication**, with a discriminating comparison showing that the obligation changes when the private commitment changes. Alternatively, source-grounded absence of any applicable field rule may restrict the LL claim to A's own orientation. Such a case warrants reopening *exactly that unexpressed-intention linkage*, not rewriting the entire Participatory ontology.
- **Owner/route/stop:** FOT Shape; stop on qualified source standing and discriminator, or retain a narrower QF with an explicit inspection action.
