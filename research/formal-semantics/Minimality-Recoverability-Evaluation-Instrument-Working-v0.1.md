## 0. Core purpose and limits

**Magic-wand end state:** A worker can investigate an arbitrary nominated referent or proposed distinction under a suitably realistic PGO; retain all decision-relevant semantic differences; express and recover them with the smallest *adequate* already-available structures; identify when distinct interpretation, representation, persistence, or enforcement is genuinely required; and pass a trustworthy, source-qualified result to the next consumer without representing all possible facts.

**Positive formulation:** **Preserve consequential differentiation through minimum sufficient independently justified structure, with recoverable meaning, traceable warrant, and appropriately protected obligations.**

The instrument is a **review practice**, not a new universal tuple or gate. Apparent compression is acceptable when the individual meanings remain accessible. Separately labeling two ideas is not evidence that they need separate persistent objects. Conversely, compact notation does not authorize a loss of required semantic or enforcement capability.

### Four independently assessed questions

1. **Semantic need:** Which distinctions have genuinely different meanings, truth/validity conditions, obligations, source standing, or consequential effects?
2. **Representation:** Can existing identities, typed native relations, contextual bases, evidence/standing, versions and projections express and recover those differences?
3. **Consumer recoverability:** Does an actual or bounded simulated consumer obtain and correctly use each relevant difference at the moment and resolution of the decision?
4. **Enforcement:** Which stable, mechanically decidable, decision-bearing obligations require independent deterministic protection, and which open semantic judgments must remain adaptable?

A positive result on (2) is an **expressive capacity** result. It is not proof of semantic correctness in (1), demonstrated consumer behavior in (3), or satisfied enforcement in (4).

## 1. Entry and scope — PGO before consequential comparison

Provide sufficient situational orientation for the *specific* decision, without imposing a permanent global PGO form:

- **Mapper / standpoint / access:** Who is assessing; what evidence, contracts, and permissions are actually available?
- **Subject / seat:** Exact focal referent or proposed differentiation, its boundary and the containing whole; which existing alternative is being compared?
- **Purpose and definition of done:** What useful work, downstream use or magic-wand outcome will the distinction support? What exactly counts as this review being complete?
- **Support and undermining:** What evidence or consequence would support or undermine the target outcome, meaningfulness of the distinction, and its representation?
- **Controlled variables:** Which identities, native predicates, PGO conditions, relevant occasions/boundaries and current claims are held fixed? Which dimensions may intentionally vary?
- **Standing and stopping:** What sources govern, how is validity distinguished from verification, who can accept a semantic/architecture change, and which unresolved result warrants a QF?

PGO controls selection, depth, evidence sufficiency, and what to do next. It does not manufacture referents or the truth of proposed relationships. An index such as existing `κ=(R,B,G,F)` locates inquiry without exhaustive world representation. Existing URG `RelClaim(ρ,A,S,β,standing)` carries native typed participants, semantics, basis and standing; **A already means ordered participants in this contract**. No additional universal attention variable is proposed.

**Stop / repair the seat** when a missing PGO dimension would change the outcome or structural interpretation. A change in the active focal referent is an explicit navigation operation, not evidence that the old referent's real relationships changed.

## 2. Source fidelity and top-down/bottom-up reconciliation

### Source-first extraction

For every load-bearing distinction:
- Quote or precisely locate the *actual* governing proposition and edition; record its authority/scope/currentness.
- Identify the particular assertion being assessed; assess that assertion rather than a reconstructed weaker/stronger variant.
- Identify existing positive purpose and consumer obligation. Historical category names and clause counts do not, by themselves, establish independent structural jobs.
- Retain any authoritative invariant or previously established positive distinction even if the evaluator currently lacks a confirming empirical specimen.

### Top-down pass

Start from the intended use: what must a successful consumer **understand, distinguish, retrieve, infer, decide, or enforce** for the PGO to succeed? Trace each responsibility down to necessary information, relation semantics, evidence access and accountable controls.

### Bottom-up pass

Start from actual native relationships, source evidence, existing carrier capabilities and observed consumer behavior. Recover which distinctions are already present, which are qualified, which are only representable or hypothesized, and what constraints actual implementations impose. Propagate discovered feasibility/limitations upward to the intended whole.

### Reconciliation

Each material proposition must receive a two-sided return:
- **Intended demand:** exact decision consequence / original obligation.
- **Actual supply:** located source, representation, behavior and warrant.
- **Delta:** missing, already jointly satisfied, differently scoped, or still unqualified.
- **Action:** preserve, enrich, repair, introduce independently warranted structure, or hold with an exact QF.

The two directions constrain each other; neither grants standing to the other unilaterally.

## 3. Audit questions — apply to each proposed differentiation

Evaluate six questions in this order. A single source-correct answer can satisfy several; do not force duplicate tests or artifacts.

**Q1 — Positive distinction.** What exact semantic or operational difference does the candidate preserve? Name native predicate, referents, conditions, attribution and decision consequence. Existing ratified invariants count as positive architectural standing; do not demand an arbitrary new empirical failure as permission to retain them.

**Q2 — Controlled discrimination.** Under a suitably fixed PGO and explicitly located variables, what would be different if this distinction were absent, conflated or misinterpreted? Compare the actual original proposition and supported alternatives. Use counterfactuals or ablation where informative, not as universal proof gates. If the domain difference is constitutive or interpretive rather than measurable behavior, use its applicable criteria.

**Q3 — Existing carrier.** Where can this difference currently reside? Prefer stable referent identity, native-domain typed relations, situated bases, source-qualified claims, existing semantic contracts, and recoverable views. Check whether the distinction can share a carrier without losing independent interpretation. One relationship may require multiple connected claims; a shared carrier is not necessarily one record.

**Q4 — Recovery at use.** Can the intended consumer reconstruct who/what the claim refers to, its exact predicate, boundaries, source edition, basis, status/standing, and which information is missing? Where a claim is consequential, demonstrate the read or label the consumer behavior UNVERIFIED. Source custody, a structural fixture PASS, and authored examples do not prove actual consumer uptake.

**Q5 — Independent mechanism and liability.** What separate primitive, persistent record, evaluator, semantic condition, or deterministic gate would do *additional required work*? Check stable/ratified mechanically decidable obligations, current enforcement coverage and failure consequences, the costs of discretion, and the loss of useful flexibility if fixed structure is imposed. **The governing structural-leverage rule permits physicalization on architectural warrant alone when the job is settled; observed repeated failures are not a mandatory prerequisite.**

**Q6 — Net sufficiency and reentry.** What information or discrimination would be lost by combining, or artificially duplicated by splitting? Is the target outcome decision-complete at the chosen resolution? Record the smallest *adequate* arrangement and any exact unresolved proposition, change signal, decision route, owner and return surface.

## 4. Evidence and decision thresholds

A reviewer must label the **grade of result**. Do not compress these into an undifferentiated PASS.

| Result | Minimum affirmative basis | Appropriate statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Semantic distinction established** | Exact applicable governing source, independently qualified native semantics, or valid situated differentiation under stated limits | “This difference must remain meaningful/recoverable.” |
| **Expressibility demonstrated** | Specific existing carrier(s) and a faithful example of how they encode the relevant propositions | “Existing machinery can represent this bounded distinction.” |
| **Recoverability demonstrated** | Actual read/reconstruction by the named consumer, with exact referents, conditions, standing and alternatives preserved; or explicitly scoped offline simulation | “This consumer recovered it under these conditions.” |
| **Semantic qualification demonstrated** | Domain-applicable evidence that the specific positive claim is warranted, beyond preassigned labels or mere field completeness | “This particular claim has the stated warrant.” |
| **Independent structure justified** | Precise semantic, consumer or enforcement job left unfulfilled by available mechanisms, plus acceptable informational/operational cost; or ratified deterministic control requiring physical protection | “This independently enforced/represented element has a distinct job.” |
| **Decision complete at scope** | All consequential required differences available, validated for intended purpose, any execution evidence verified as claimed, residual QFs located | “GO within stated decision scope; other truths remain open.” |

Use **HOLD / UNKNOWN** where evidence is insufficient, **NOT ESTABLISHED** where the tested basis fails to justify the claim, and **INAPPLICABLE / DORMANT** when supported by the proper semantics and PGO. These inherit the URG's absence/noncollapse grammar; this table does not introduce a universal enum.

**Admission thresholds:**
- Keep an already governing semantic or enforcement obligation unless specifically superseded through authorized architecture decision.
- A proposed separation into an additional *independent* object/field/layer/gate needs an identifiable new job, not the mere existence of distinct words.
- A proposed consolidation needs a recoverable crosswalk for every load-bearing difference, including scope, basis, warrant, and consumer behavior where necessary.
- Proposed generalization from a constructed case is provisional until matched to its stated evidence horizon.
- Explicit source authority and independent semantic interpretation take priority over a favorable count or score.

There is **no additive numerical score**: a single fatal loss of a governing distinction can defeat an otherwise economical design.

## 5. Output disposition vocabulary (review labels only)

**PRESERVE IN CURRENT CARRIER:** A consequential difference remains recoverable in existing semantics/representation; no separate mechanism established.  
**ENRICH OR REQUALIFY:** The carrier exists but requires more native detail, evidence, source standing, contextual resolution or consumer qualification.  
**INDEPENDENT STRUCTURE WARRANTED:** A specific added structure/enforcement job is supported by authoritative obligation or demonstrated missing discrimination; capture its scope, owner and enforcement mode.  
**DEFER WITH QF:** The positive distinction or actual recoverability remains materially uncertain; preserve named alternatives and discriminating evidence/reentry.  
**OUT OF SCOPE / DORMANT:** Unneeded at this decision's resolution with explicit reactivation criteria; does not mean false/nonexistent.  
**REPAIR ORIGINAL EVALUATION:** A past conclusion relied on a proposition different from the source, changed variables without control, inferred semantic truth from expressibility, or otherwise exceeded evidence. Preserve the failure and amended inference.

These are ordinary human review labels, not proposed new schema types. Several can describe different dimensions of one review. Publication or custody is not acceptance.

## 8. Generalized anti-drift and stopping safeguards

- Revisit **the exact assertion**, its source authority and applicable comparison before declaring a defect or simplifying it. No straw-man contrasts or assumed weak forms.
- Explicitly name what is being *differentiated*: truth condition, semantic job, referent, record, access route, enforcement, or display view. These are different separations with possibly shared implementation.
- Keep fixed parameters stable across a comparison, and mark any legitimate reseating/reorientation; controlled flattening must not erase scope or provenance.
- Treat current architecture as a first-class candidate for sufficiency, not as an automatic failure or automatic success.
- A new object or mechanism must earn its distinct job; an existing constitutional noncollapse or enforcement obligation need not re-earn authority through an invented experiment.
- Recoverability is consumer- and use-specific; representation correctness and consumer uptake are separate claims.
- Let specific consequential incompleteness generate the QF. Do not force an endless question or require complete ontology closure.
- End once the PGO's proximal decision can be responsibly made, retaining dormant alternatives, scope exclusions and reentry conditions.
- When a correction follows an earlier overreach, retain both the repaired conclusion and the mechanism of error for future evaluators.

## 9. One-paragraph worker commission

**Evaluate [candidate change or proposed distinction] for minimality and recoverability under [specified PGO]. First identify the exact referent, containing whole, intended result, controlled variables, actual source wording and authority. Derive the consumer's needed distinctions top-down and the native evidence/representational capabilities bottom-up. For each consequential difference, assess semantic necessity, existing-carrier expressibility, downstream recovery at decision time, and separate enforcement liability. Preserve existing accepted meaning, prefer the smallest adequate carrier, identify what a new independent element would actually do, and qualify results by evidence class. Return an explicit disposition, reconstruction trace, any prior inferential correction and precise QF. Do not infer universal truth or replacement-contract authorization from one bounded specimen.**
