## 1. Executive decision

The **whole SSMM loop should be dedicated to excavation**, not only Sense/Shape.

Reason: the present defect is not lack of ideas. It is **distributed earned structure with mixed altitude, standing, supersession and continuity**. Sense/Shape alone could produce one more excellent analysis artifact while leaving the next worker to repeat source archaeology and leaving downstream surfaces with stale or overlapping formulations.

The excavation itself has legitimate phase-specific work:

* **Sense:** discover and discriminate the actual invariant/definition landscape.
* **Shape:** freeze the recovered lattice, source-of-truth/standing rules, derivation graph, closure matrix and exact gaps.
* **Move:** make that frozen excavation product real as a durable, navigable, source-linked continuity instrument and qualify its reconstruction/contradiction behavior.
* **Metabolize:** propagate reconciled standing/supersession into <issue id="99e859ce-9911-456e-b2d4-542115f017e7" href="https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-136/shape-universal-referent-grammar-specify-and-qualify-a-portable">ECO-136</issue> and downstream consumers and commission the next axis-formalization loop from the repaired basis.

Stopping after Shape would leave the highest-leverage result — **installed continuity and downstream repair** — undone.

This does not authorize the successor axis formalization inside <issue id="5b4b31af-016d-474f-b68f-75a70e79b602" href="https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-220/ssmm-excavation-urg-invariant-lattice-recover-reconcile-qualify">ECO-220</issue>. Excavation remains the focal object of this loop.

## 2. Grand orientation retained

SIGMA/ECOS is a **meta-architecture**, not presumptively a replacement for mature domain architectures, semantic systems, standards, languages or tools.

Its universal substrate must therefore be sharp enough to:

* preserve native domain semantics without absorbing them;
* seat heterogeneous referents consistently;
* distinguish referent, representation, mapper, evidence, standing, authority and currentness;
* compose/transform across contexts without silent promotion;
* expose lawful reseating and requalification;
* support multiple projections/compilations while preserving lineage and warranted distinctions.

The immediate architectural pressure is therefore **formal clarity of the universal coordinate/invariant system**, not ontology expansion.

## 6. Preliminary axis-formalization maturity assessment

### A. Holarchic altitude / Level — **highest readiness**

Current earned semantic core:

> **Level / altitude is interrogated through asymmetric existence dependency together with constitutive organization: what must exist, and in what organization, for the designated whole to exist as that whole?**

Strong anti-definitions already earned:

* generic containment is insufficient;
* physical size is insufficient;
* rank/prestige is insufficient;
* complexity is insufficient;
* developmental attainment is insufficient;
* numerical altitude is not intrinsic by default.

Operational discipline already exists:

* seat R;
* freeze non-tested variables;
* type the change;
* preserve/enrich;
* dependency/container test;
* projection test;
* explicit reseat;
* physicalization quarantine;
* counterexample gate;
* PGO adequacy/enrichment gate where Stage/Level adjudication depends on purpose.

Current program explicitly records Level as **sufficient for present Register-B descent**.

Remaining formalization burden appears mostly to be:

1. consolidate the definition and checker into one exact versioned contract;
2. formalize seat/reseat and boundary interaction;
3. state transitivity/partial-order expectations only where actually warranted;
4. define identity/whole survival and changed constitutive organization carefully;
5. expose machine-checkable versus semantic-judgment portions;
6. attach hostile cases and falsifiers;
7. reconcile with <issue id="3b306b18-df3f-4818-808e-9aa59c6bb47a" href="https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-191/sense-formalization-sigma-recursive-referent-system-derive-minimal">ECO-191</issue> transition semantics.

This is **not** blank-slate Level discovery.

### B. Quadrants — **very high readiness, but bounded current qualification**

Current compact generator:

1. **Relational seat**
   * constitutive seat: inquiry into R under its declared constituent boundary;
   * participatory seat: inquiry into the same R in relations crossing/exceeding that boundary.
2. **Answer burden**
   * governing possibility/dependency: what permits, requires or excludes what may/must/cannot be the case;
   * determinate case: what condition/configuration/relation/event/result is the case here.

Crossing yields current UL/UR/LL/LR obligations.

Strong existing constraints:

* fixed focal referent R across traversal;
* changing constituency is boundary revision / re-individuation, not ordinary quadrant traversal;
* content/evidence type does not determine quadrant;
* all four obligations remain structurally available even when answers are unknown;
* coverage != epistemic completeness;
* access method, phenomenology, spatial enclosure, scale, individual/collective, sentence vocabulary do not generate quadrants.

Remaining formalization burden:

1. lift v0.2 from bounded functional contract into exact whole-axis formal definition;
2. reconcile later <issue id="a6ad4441-722d-4a6b-8a84-afbac417ceed" href="https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-179/sense-ul-invariant-focal-local-possibilitydependency-structure-across">ECO-179</issue> analysis with qualified v0.2 without accidental supersession;
3. formalize composition with Level/altitude and explicit reseating;
4. distinguish boundary enrichment from boundary revision;
5. normalize question-burden semantics enough for deterministic assistance without pretending all classification is syntactic;
6. freeze axis invariants, transformations and reentry;
7. connect C1–C6 architecture and tests without making implementation the definition.

Quadrant is therefore an excellent co-target with Level.

### C. Four fundamental directional pressures — **high semantic maturity, lower formal closure**

<issue id="49d8c50b-fcc1-4ef7-ac48-51a558062f45" href="https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-184/metabolize-architecture-directions-capacities-capabilities">ECO-184</issue> gives Principal-accepted Register-B working architecture:

  * agency / self-preservation ↔ communion / self-adaptation;
  * transcendence ↔ dissolution;
* “directional” means **relational transformation**;
* tendency / capacity / drive are modal views of the same underlying coordinate structure;
* capacity = transformation available/supported for R under conditions;
* drive = that directional possibility actively mobilized / exerting transformation pressure;
* tendency = neutral structural description;
* object-specific capacity = referent-indexed availability of directional transformation;
* direction != capability != Line != SSMM phase != PGO != Level.

This is already much stronger than a metaphorical AQAL import.

However, <issue id="49d8c50b-fcc1-4ef7-ac48-51a558062f45" href="https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-184/metabolize-architecture-directions-capacities-capabilities">ECO-184</issue> explicitly leaves open:

* whether Drive ever needs semantics beyond mobilized capacity.

**Current recommendation:** include directional pressures in the successor formalization loop only behind a readiness gate. Level + Quadrant should not be delayed if Direction formalization expands into a broader theory-of-transformation problem.

* **Line** is significantly sharpened by <issue id="49d8c50b-fcc1-4ef7-ac48-51a558062f45" href="https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-184/metabolize-architecture-directions-capacities-capabilities">ECO-184</issue>: persistent domain-indexed longitudinal continuity over changing capacity/capability organization.

These should benefit materially from Level + Quadrant formalization rather than precede it.

## 7. Why Level + Quadrant are the right next kernel

The Principal's target survives the first excavation pressure.

They jointly answer two extremely upstream questions:

* **Level:** what whole is constituted from what, at what dependency/organizational altitude, and what boundary/reseat is being asserted?
* **Quadrant:** for that same seated referent and boundary, what kind of question burden are we asking — constitutive or participatory; governing or determinate?

That combination creates a powerful address for subsequent semantics:

> **Which referent? Which boundary/altitude? Which relational seat? Which answer burden?**

If stabilized, this should make later State/Stage/Line/Type and domain-specific descent substantially easier because many current ambiguities are really **wrong seat, wrong altitude, wrong boundary, or wrong burden** errors.

Directional pressures then potentially add:

> **What primitive relational transformation is available/active for this referent?**

That looks like a plausible third foundational coordinate family, but its formal closure should not be allowed to hold Level/Quadrant hostage.
