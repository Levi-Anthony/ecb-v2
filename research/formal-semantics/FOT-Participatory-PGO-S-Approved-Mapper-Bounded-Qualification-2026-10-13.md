## 1. Modelability principle: positive scope, explicit limits

**Working principle:** Any referent that can be sufficiently individuated and seated within a governing situation can be **modeled for a declared purpose and discriminating resolution**, including incomplete/uncertain referents, phenomena and hypotheses. Successful modeling does **not** require exhausting every property or proving every claim. It requires preservation of the referent/map/mapper distinction; exact PGO/boundary/occasion; consequential predicates and relations; sources/standing; relevant unresolved variables; permissible inferences; and a route for challenge, requalification, actualization or stop.

**Not automatically entailed:** that every fact can be observed, every property determined by finite tests, every model is truthful, every domain's Level is positively witnessed, every conjecture is computable, or existing URG carriers have *already been independently shown* to express all future cases without loss. When a variable is unresolvable, the model's truthful output can be a **specific calibrated Question Forward** rather than an asserted conclusion. This makes “model anything” a useful **design aspiration subject to counterexample and minimality/recoverability tests**, not a self-certifying universal theorem.
