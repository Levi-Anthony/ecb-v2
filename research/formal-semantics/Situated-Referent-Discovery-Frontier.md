## 3. Reciprocal bootstrap and meaningful reference

After raw ingest to the thoughts table, stable enrichment/seating is the present referent-contact bootstrap problem. Raw capture retains its independent intake contract. The referent-contact process can start from a goal, noun, observation, contradiction, question, relation, artifact or already-known node. It is not directionally restricted and can be repurposed under another explicit PGO.

A sufficient **situational basis** is upstream of consequential derivation. At minimum it includes a PGO; it also carries the current situation, scope, relevant actors/relations, frame/access, state/time and standing where they affect the inquiry. That sufficiency is not a demand to finish all context before doing any bootstrap work.

Situation, provisional identity, whole/part relations, quadrant disclosures, evidence and questions reciprocally stabilize each other. Identity, Level and Quadrant therefore help answer **“what are we referring to?”** together. A stable initial handle can preserve contact while those distinctions remain provisional.

Reuse the existing situated inquiry index `κ=(R,B,G,F)` — read **kappa: focal referent, boundary/individuation, governing orientation, mapper/frame/access**. G denotes governing orientation, not “grain.” This index is not an exhaustive persistence schema. “Grain” remains inherited language under investigation, not a newly introduced first-class term.

### First-person intentional traversal

Once the situation supports a non-circular ideal end state, temporarily focalize that state and take its first-person intentional seat:

- **Upward:** What am I part of? What depends on me to exist?
- **Downward:** What makes me distinctly me? What must exist, in what organization, for me to remain this whole?
- **Laterally:** Which neighbors interact with me, constrain me, support me or distinguish me at the declared level?

These questions initiate discovery; they do not certify Level. “Depends on me” can describe external support, function, value delivery or constitutive existence. A witnessed holarchic Level relation still requires the installed **constitution + organization + asymmetric existence-dependence** discriminator. A containing product, desired outcome and stakeholder are not automatically the same kind of whole.

The first-person seat is a focal-indexing operator. Whether a referent has felt experience, intention or a registered internal state remains a separately warranted domain claim. In a magic-wand exercise, the focal object is explicitly prospective; desired role descriptions do not become observations of an installed object.

Candidate exit records carry the attained basis, unresolved consequential distinctions, standing, sources, next discriminating contact and exact return destination. ## 4. Question Forward and Overshoot

**Question Forward (QF)** is simultaneously a standing invariant disposition, semantic primitive, compositional element, wired-sensor interface and disciplined reasoning practice. Its interrogative form encodes an unresolved difference, directs attention, cues derivation, preserves openness, and reinforces the receiving worker's situational agency and evidence basis.

A candidate operational record associates a question with its focal basis, unresolved distinction, evidence route, consequential alternatives, adequacy/return condition and applicable change triggers. These are interface obligations to reconcile with existing machinery, not a newly installed record type.

| Question's job | Legitimate behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Repair an inadequate situational basis | Recover the governing intention or consequential border before expanding downstream inquiry. |
| Generate or discriminate structure | Ask which answer would change the current account, what evidence bears on it and how much resolution is enough. |
| Remain wired as a sensor | Later evidence can satisfy, challenge, refine or regenerate the question; an answer does not remove the parent disclosure obligation. |
| Support handoff | Preserve why the distinction matters and where derivation resumes without making the compiler's guess governing truth. |

**Overshoot Methodology** deliberately presses traversal beyond a merely neat account until a warranted border or incoherence signal becomes visible. The Principal's “Gödel signals” name three operational alerts here: circularity/infinite regress, smuggled standing and performative contradiction. The name does not add a mathematical theorem to this procedure.

| Signal | Exact target required before claiming it fired | Repair/return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circularity / infinite regress | A justification or decomposition cannot produce a consequential distinction under the active basis | Establish a border, recover intention or look to another source. Legitimate recurrence is not itself incoherence. |
| Smuggled standing | A claim has acquired authority, actuality, necessity or other standing beyond its attributable basis | Restore the claim's actual provenance/standing and route the missing warrant or decision. |
| Performative contradiction | The proposed enactment defeats a condition on which its governing commitment depends | Revise realization, reseat or expose the unresolved governing choice. Ordinary preference tension alone is insufficient. |

In the switch story, a persistent control **might** harm valued minimalism; its mere existence does not demonstrate that effect. The earlier assistant's assertion that this already established performative contradiction exceeded the invented scenario's stipulations. The target is that asserted diagnosis, not the Principal's three-signal method. Consequence: retain a conditional sensor and test the actual effect before declaring the signal.

## 7. Dynamic composition and deterministic situated valenced recall

“Situated” must not crystallize into a permanently exhaustive object. Stable identity and reliable custody should support rapid re-and-decomposition, relational discovery and new focal compositions. A sufficiently situated account is an attributable, use-indexed stabilization that can be revisited.

**Valenced** means that information carries an explicit evaluative relation under a concern/criterion: for whom and for what it supports, obstructs or otherwise matters, with its basis, conditions and standing. It does not mean a universal good/bad score, embedding similarity or retrieval rank. The valence may be conditional, plural or disputed.

The Principal's exact phrase is **“clear separation of concerns between deterministic and later space operations.”** This pass provisionally reads “later space” as **latent-space semantic/generative operations**. That gloss remains unresolved terminology; the independently specified lookup/derivation/discovery distinction does not depend on silently ratifying it.

| Concern | Candidate contract | Semantics retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact record lookup | Given an explicit address and edition/as-of selection, recover stored source, claim, composition, state or valence faithfully; unresolved selection is visible | Lookup neither generates omitted meaning nor establishes current-world truth. |
| Declared deterministic projection | Given fixed qualified inputs and a versioned rule, reproduce the declared projection with omissions/dependencies visible | A reconstruction can be deterministic even when its original classification required semantic judgment. |
| Semantic discovery and composition | Propose new relations, decompositions, focal wholes, interpretations or applicability; retain source anchors, assumptions, uncertainty and derivation | Discovery can change the inquiry's topology; it does not acquire evidence or authority merely by being plausible. |
| Requalification/current use | Check exact basis, changed premises, affected-old support and destination-only disclosure before relying on transported information | Currentness, semantic fitness, selection and action authority remain independent. |

This is a separation of responsibilities, not a claim that every model operation is stochastic or that every rule is semantically adequate. Determinism is an execution property; warrant and authority are different properties. Natural-language referent resolution can remain ambiguous while exact addressed retrieval is reliable.

### Minimum recoverable material — design obligations, not a selected schema

1. **Identity and source:** independently addressable referent/record identities, exact source or declared normalization, co-reference/continuity basis and edition.
2. **Situation and composition:** R/B/G/F; typed relations; selected membership/organization; composition edition and formation/continuity basis; folded material, reason and unfolding trigger.
3. **State and valence:** state basis, occasion and attributable claim; evaluator/concern/criterion, contextual meaning, conditions and standing of each valence claim; known conflicts and unknowns.
4. **Change and recall:** material-change receipt, transport/crosswalk, dependency-local requalification, source/destination obligations and precise as-of/current-selection semantics.
5. **Open discovery:** coverage limits, QFs/sensors and a challenge route that does not discard evidence solely because the current PGO deemed it irrelevant.

Reuse independently standing identity, claims, relations, evidence, native domain records and ECO-191 receipts. A persisted composition or cached projection is useful if its inputs and invalidation conditions are recoverable. It must not become the owner of identity, PGO, state, evidence and authority as one inseparable truth. Rapid composition need not entail rederiving all known information from scratch.
