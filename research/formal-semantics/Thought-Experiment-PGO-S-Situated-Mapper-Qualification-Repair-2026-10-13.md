## 1. Exact defect and repaired positive qualification sequence

**Previously insufficient:** PGO-I specified the analyst's question, and PGO-S stipulated that A/B should successfully complete a musical phrase. That PGO-S was a **scene-level wish/goal**, but it did not nominate or qualify who in the scene must *make an inquiry*, why their discernment arose, what the mapper has access to, which exact distinction controls their immediate decision, or which evidence and updates would move them from uncertainty to warranted action. Asking the Principal if A “is still participating” therefore outsourced the missing scene-local governing work to the Principal instead of deriving a decision from a qualified situated PGO.

**Positive sequence for a thought experiment with an internal inquiry:**
1. **PGO-I** — experimenter's focal semantic distinction, use/consumer, test design, discriminating outcomes, falsifiers and stopping; the analyst's sought result cannot stipulate world facts.
2. **PGO-S initiation** — specific initiating contrast / difficulty inside the imagined world, exact actor who becomes mapper, focal object/relation being mapped, mapper position/access limits, situation/occasion, surrounding whole and legitimate authority to act.
3. **PGO-S native positive end** — successful/magic-wand outcome the local mapper is trying to realize, operational definition of done including warranted alternatives such as successful resumption or respectful pause/termination, real normative constraints and opportunity costs.
4. **PGO-S discriminating method** — concrete inquiry/actions available to that mapper and the native signals/results that HELP or HINDER the local outcome, with *non-exhaustive examples* tied to qualification and update decisions. Include stop/fallback and what would count as being mistaken.
5. **World-versus-evidence control** — keep the scenario's stipulated actual world conditions distinct from what the situated mapper can see and infer, from what the external experimenter can assume, and from the desired outcome. Distinguish actual events from maps, and when an action alters what is being mapped.
6. **Return** — only then use the scene to qualify the PGO-I semantic distinction; record supported inference, specific alternate, or calibrated disclosure-oriented QF with a hypothetical evidence event and owner. If the scene's own mapper, criterion or initiating contrast remains missing, status **PGO-S NOT SUFFICIENT FOR THIS INFERENCE — REPAIR SCENE**, with the particular repair location, not a generic warning.

**Important scope:** Not every imagined situation necessarily contains a sentient deliberate in-world investigator. When a test has no genuine scene-local inquiry, disclose that and specify the lawful *situated observation/mapper* rather than silently pretending an imagined participant is deliberately evaluating; if this test specifically requires an internal mapper, either construct and qualify that actor or choose a different test. This is a functional role-separation rule, **not** a mandatory new tuple, third PGO or extra universal ontology.

## 2. Repaired hypothetical dance — candidate sufficiently situated PGO-S

**Scenario authorial convention:** All facts in this section are explicitly hypothetical stipulations. No real dancer, studio encounter or psychological condition has been observed. The experiment is not evidence of the genuine anatomy of intention, consciousness or participation.

**PGO-I, external:** Investigate which positive conditions of *A's presently operative participation with B* are distinguishable from A's privately intended future interaction, from a merely historical relationship, and from projected scene goals. The intended use is the FOT Participatory Left (LL) / Participatory Right (LR) semantic discriminator QF-PART-01; no label alone qualifies current enactment. **Definition of done:** a properly controlled case can show where subject intention, enacted coupling, reciprocal readiness, and historically stored connection diverge, and what evidence warrants a particular conclusion. **Mapper-I:** assistant/Principal as current theory investigators; **referent-I:** the distinction/qualification, not the dancer's immediate decision.

**PGO-S, situation:** A and B have already been coordinating in a particular tango encounter H for approximately 30 seconds. During an ongoing musical phrase, B becomes still for roughly two to three seconds; a movement response A expected is **not yet reliably legible** to A. The music offers a possible resumption, but B's actual responsiveness has deliberately **not** been stipulated. The ambiguity could be a mutually maintained pause, an unintelligible signal, temporary distraction, or disengagement. H's continued identity cannot be proved by naming this a “pause.”

**Initiating contrast / negative risk within S:** A is about to propose further movement but lacks a reliable distinction between shared responsive stillness and cessation of coordination. An unqualified next step could override B's timing or choice, disturb balance or treat nonresponse as tacit consent; prolonged purposeless guessing could also prevent coherent continuation. **This contrast initiates A's local inquiry**, not our external argument about LL/LR.

**Mapper-S:** **A**, a competent situated participant who can interpret cues and choose whether/how to propose action. **Position/access:** A's own intention and axis, music/time, contact and movement feedback, observable or conveyed responses from B and available verbal clarification. A cannot directly read B's private intention. The experimenter may stipulate a B-world-state for comparisons, but A does not gain access to those stipulations.

**Focal referent-S:** A **as a currently participating dancer** (stable identity/boundary) in the ongoing H context. **What A is mapping:** the time-indexed state and implications of A's *jointly organized responsiveness with B*—whether A's next movement invitation is warranted, whether reciprocal coupling still obtains and what current conditions demand. **Co-relatum B** and **event H** remain distinct referents; attending to this relation does not quietly reseat H or prove that H persists.

**PGO-S positive magic-wand end:** Within the current phrase, A accurately interprets the actionable coordination and chooses a minimally intrusive, mutually respectful course: (a) resume actual responsive dancing where warranted, **or** (b) continue a supported deliberate pause / seek clarification / conclude the dance where warranted. A does not force an ungrounded movement, and their own continuity/axis and B's agency remain intact. The best outcome is not “A gets B to resume”; it is **warranted coordination or coherent restraint**. This makes success possible when the actually appropriate response is to stop.

**PGO-S definition of done and stopping:** The immediate inquiry closes when A has enough attributable, current support to select a low-risk action and observes compatible response **at the relevant occasion**, or deliberately maintains safety while seeking clarity because evidence remains unresolved. If B clearly withdraws or communicates noncontinuation, refrain from treating H as ongoing without separately qualifying its identity. If responsive movement resumes, its positive observed consequence can help **verify the action**, but cannot retroactively prove that every prior intention/readiness attribution was valid.

**PGO-S local discernment instructions:** Begin from own axis and shared music. Distinguish *known* body-contact/temporal feedback from A's assumptions about B. Check whether quiet contact and responsiveness remain actual at this occasion; where uncertain, prefer small reversible invitations or explicit clarification rather than unilaterally escalating. Observe consequences and separately qualify (i) A's intention, (ii) B's accessible response, (iii) dyadic organization, (iv) H identity/persistence if decision-bearing. Interpret any change after an invitation as an **intervention outcome**, not necessarily proof of the pre-intervention relation.

**HELPS — non-exhaustive examples for local end:** attributable still-but-responsive connection; mutually intelligible time/weight availability; B's voluntary response or clear request for pause; an invitation that preserves B's option to decline; coherent shared resumption or respectful ending; signals that discriminate current coupling from a remembered habit. **Instruction/action:** identify what native proposition each signal supports *now*, and revise the chosen course if new contradictory evidence arrives.

**HINDERS — non-exhaustive examples for local end:** imposing motion on B without response; assuming that silence/stillness constitutes assent; treating A's plan as B's intention; interpreting archival partnership status as current joint organization; falsely “confirming” the earlier state from feedback generated by A's own later intervention; using an outcome label to suppress an actual contrary cue. **Instruction/action:** compare with the positive end, reduce/revise the proposed action, repair the underlying cue's warrant, clarify or respectfully pause instead of forcing the desired outcome.

**Unfixed discriminators:** B's actual present intention and readiness, whether the sustained responsive arrangement was already operative *before* A's subsequent probe, and the conditions under which particular H continues are not established by PGO-S. Those conditions remain **variables**, not tacit assumptions. Fix or vary them deliberately for the external experiment PGO-I.
