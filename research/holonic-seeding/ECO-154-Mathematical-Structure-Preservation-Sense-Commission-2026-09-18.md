## Principal impetus

The current ECOS use of **conformal mapping** has primarily been metaphorical: a domain transition declares what is expected to be kept, changed, or lost so fidelity can be assessed rather than assumed.

A stronger possibility is now under investigation:

> use the formal mathematics itself to generate a principled semantic/research neighborhood, then test whether any of those problem-shapes yield mechanisms for discovering candidate holons, organizing multi-scale structure, or constraining lossy transformations.

One motivating example is whether holonic seeding has a formal relation to compression- or clustering-based discovery: repeated/shared structure may make a composite representation cheaper or more predictive than independent representation of its constituents.

## Idealized end state

Sense should make a later worker capable of designing a **small, interpretable, falsifiable Register-B probe** in which:

1. candidate higher-order structure is generated from observed data/relations rather than merely declared;
2. compression/coarse-graining can be measured;
3. what must be preserved is explicit and decision-relative rather than assumed;
4. distortion/loss can be characterized;
5. a candidate holon is treated as an evidence-bearing proposal, not an ontological fact;
6. disagreement between candidate sensors is visible rather than averaged away.

Sense does **not** need to prove that one universal algorithm governs all holonic formation.

## Starting decomposition

Keep these problems distinct unless evidence supports unification:

1. **Discovery / seeding** — what recurring/shared structure warrants proposing a higher-order unit?
2. **Compression / coarse-graining** — what detail can be removed once a useful composite representation exists?
3. **Fidelity / distortion control** — what relational or task-relevant structure must survive the transition?
4. **Multi-scale organization** — how do proposed units participate in nested or overlapping organization without forcing a flat taxonomy?
5. **Governed relevance** — how does the present governing purpose/use determine which losses are consequential?

Do not assume that conformality itself solves all five.

## Required mathematical neighborhood

Begin from the exact formal problem solved by each family, not from terminology resemblance. At minimum investigate:

* conformal maps;
* quasiconformal maps / bounded distortion;
* metric embeddings and distortion;
* algorithmic-information/compression similarity, including normalized compression distance where relevant;
* Minimum Description Length / model-cost tradeoffs;
* rate-distortion theory;
* Information Bottleneck / relevance-preserving compression;
* hierarchical clustering and ultrametric representations;
* partial-order-constrained hierarchy where relevant;
* statistical-physics / renormalization / coarse-graining as multi-scale effective description.

The worker may expand beyond this list **only when a formal dependency, relaxation, dual, neighboring theorem/problem class, or observed mismatch points there**. Do not perform analogy sprawl.

Potential expansions worth testing rather than presuming include topology/manifold learning, graph coarsening/community structure, persistent structure, sufficient statistics, predictive-state representations, and category/functor language.

## Import discipline

For every mathematical concept distinguish:

**FACT** — what the formal theory actually says.

**STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE** — an explicit mapping between formal objects/operations/invariants and ECOS objects/operations.

**HYPOTHESIS** — an unverified ECOS mechanism suggested by that correspondence.

**METAPHOR ONLY** — language that is useful but does not survive formal mapping.

No mathematical prestige transfer. No imported term becomes ECOS architecture merely because the analogy is evocative.

For each proposed correspondence answer:

1. What formal problem was the mathematics designed to solve?
2. What are its objects and operators?
3. What invariants does it preserve?
4. What may change?
5. What counts as distortion/loss/failure?
6. What assumptions make the result valid?
7. Which assumptions fail or become undefined in semantic/holonic data?
8. What neighboring theory appears if those assumptions are relaxed?
9. What ECOS observation would falsify the proposed correspondence?

## Key working hypotheses to test, not protect

### H1 — compression-derived holon candidate

A candidate holon may become worth proposing when encoding a recurring collection as:

**model/composite H + residuals**

is materially better than repeatedly representing its members independently.

Test whether MDL / algorithmic-information ideas make this precise enough to generate candidate composites without silently equating compression with ontology.

### H2 — governed bottleneck

A useful higher-order representation may be modeled as compression of detailed information while preserving information consequential to the present governing inquiry/use.

Test whether Information Bottleneck, rate-distortion, sufficient-statistic, or related formulations provide a better formal neighborhood.

### H3 — bounded-fidelity transform

Conformal/quasiconformal/metric-embedding ideas may be better suited to testing transformation fidelity than to generating holons.

Test whether a useful semantic analogue exists for local structure, neighborhood, relative direction, or bounded distortion. Do not manufacture one if the semantic space lacks the required geometry.

### H4 — governed coarse-graining

Holonic formation may sometimes be better modeled as governed coarse-graining / emergence of effective variables across scale than as taxonomic nesting.

Test the limits of this analogy, especially where physics assumes ensembles, locality, symmetries, or scale behavior unavailable in semantic systems.

### H5 — multiple sensors, no forced winner

Compression similarity, embedding/metric similarity, graph-relational structure, task relevance, predictive utility, and human-derived grouping may legitimately disagree. Those residuals may be decision-bearing evidence about the kind of structure being detected.

## Required source posture

Use primary or high-quality scholarly sources where possible. Recover definitions and assumptions tightly enough that a cold reader can distinguish the real mathematics from the ECOS import.

Prefer original papers/textbook-grade references for:

* normalized compression distance / information distance;
* MDL;
* Information Bottleneck;
* rate-distortion;
* metric embeddings;
* conformal/quasiconformal mappings;
* renormalization/coarse-graining.

Secondary sources may orient but should not carry decisive formal claims where primary material is accessible.

## Required Sense work

### A. Formal-neighborhood map

Produce a compact map of the investigated theories showing which transformations/relaxations connect them conceptually.

Examples of the desired relation type:

* exact/local preservation → bounded distortion;
* distance preservation → approximate metric embedding;
* lossless representation → lossy compression under distortion constraint;
* generic compression → relevance-preserving compression;
* fine-scale variables → effective/coarse-grained variables.

The map should expose why each neighbor was investigated.

### B. Preservation/loss matrix

For each serious family record:

* native mathematical object;
* transformation/operator;
* preserved quantity/structure;
* permitted change;
* loss/distortion measure;
* preconditions;
* candidate ECOS analogue;
* confidence in structural correspondence;
* likely role: discovery / compression / fidelity / multi-scale organization / none.

### C. Holonic-seeding candidate mechanisms

Derive at least three meaningfully distinct mechanism families if evidence supports them. Do not merely rename algorithms.

For each:

* input required;
* output candidate;
* why it could count as a holonic seed;
* what evidence would support the seed;
* what it cannot establish;
* interpretability;
* dependency on a governing relevance criterion;
* failure modes.

### D. Toy formalizations

Construct small mathematical specimens, not production architecture. At minimum attempt:

1. **compression specimen** — a repeated relational pattern where composite + residuals becomes cheaper than independent description;
2. **governed-loss specimen** — two compressions with similar size but different preservation of task-relevant information;
3. **distortion specimen** — a mapping that looks semantically plausible but breaks a declared relational invariant.

If the required geometry/information measures cannot be defined without arbitrary choices, that is a finding.

### E. Negative controls

Include examples where:

* strong compression should **not** imply a holon;
* a semantically meaningful holon is poorly recoverable by compression;
* embedding proximity misleads;
* hierarchical clustering imposes a false tree on overlapping structure;
* coarse-graining destroys a consequential minority/residual;
* conformal language fails because no justified angle/metric/manifold exists.

### F. Probe recommendation

End Sense with the smallest high-information Register-B experiment that could discriminate among the strongest candidate mechanisms.

The recommended probe must specify:

* corpus/specimen;
* candidate sensors;
* observable outputs;
* human/reference comparison if used;
* disagreement analysis;
* falsifiers;
* what result would justify entering Shape;
* what result would send the work upstream.

## Explicit non-goals / hard boundaries

This commission does **not**:

* canonize “conformal” as a universal ECOS property;
* define the holarchy ontology;
* modify URG grammar;
* install a holonic-seeding algorithm;
* create schema/API/runtime changes;
* infer ontological reality from compression efficiency;
* equate embedding distance with semantic distance without qualification;
* require a total order or tree where the structure may be partial, overlapping, graph-like, or scale-relative;

## Stop rule

Stop and return the upstream gap rather than naming a mechanism through it if any of the following is outcome-determinative:

* “holonic seed” cannot be operationalized enough to compare candidate mechanisms;
* the proposed semantic space requires an arbitrary metric/geometry whose choice determines the result;
* the governing relevance variable needed for rate-distortion/bottleneck-style reasoning is undefined;
* the work reveals that “discovery,” “compression,” and “fidelity” are separate referents requiring separate commissions;
* formal assumptions are violated so strongly that the correspondence is metaphor-only.

Otherwise complete Sense and stop **before Shape**.
