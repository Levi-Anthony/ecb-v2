## Multi-angle re-examination of the candidate Shape

*What assumption is hidden here?* — That SIGMA→ECOS is one relation. It is at least three
(projection, execution, return), and the strongest single finding of this operation came
from refusing to formalize them as one (see NR-02 / TC-006).

*What would make this research useless?* — Producing a reading list. Mitigated by requiring
every retained record to name a **residual delta against a live mechanism** and a
**falsifier**. 31 candidate records were dropped at screening for having neither.

*What could cause literature-induced architecture drift?* — Adopting an elegant formalism
whose prerequisites v2 lacks, then quietly inventing the prerequisite. Mitigated by the
§12 analogy admission rule and the Negative Results Registry, which is a first-class
output, not a footnote.

*What build-critical seam are we missing?* — Two, found during Shape and added:
**monitorability** (what is even decidable from a finite receipt trace) and
**convergence under concurrent surfaces**. Both were absent from the initial neighborhood
list and both earned reallocated quota.

*What would falsify the current candidate mechanism?* — Named per transfer contract. A
transfer with no falsifier was demoted to `METAPHOR_ONLY` by rule.

*What can remain unresolved?* — Everything on the freeze line, and every question whose
answer does not change BUILD 1–5. Registered as deferred apertures, not silently dropped.

*Are we opening a tiny SSMM the Master Key does not require?* — Checked at three points.
Geometry coverage was cut on exactly this ground after H5 resolved early.

*Is the requested depth sufficient, excessive, or inadequate?* — Sufficient for contract
sharpening and test generation. **Inadequate** for anything requiring machine-checked
proof; four probes are scoped but none is executed here, and that limit is stated rather
than papered over.
