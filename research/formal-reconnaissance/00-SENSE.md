## Known contradictions and tensions carried into Shape

- **T-1.** The architecture demands "runtime receipts demonstrate the invariant governed
  execution" *and* forbids introducing structure before it is earned. Receipts that carry
  proof obligations are structure. Which comes first was unresolved at Sense.
- **T-2.** `current ≠ newest` is an epistemic commitment that most of the mature revision
  literature violates by axiom. This was expected to produce either a strong hit or a
  strong negative; it produced both.
- **T-3.** The one place v2 currently has a real metric (384-d `gte-small` cosine
  similarity) is the place where treating it as a *meaning* metric would violate
  `map ≠ referent` and `confidence ≠ standing`. Geometry findings had to be screened
  against this.

## External research need, expressed as a delta

The architecture is strong on **distinctions** and weak on **discharge**. Every protected
item above says what must not collapse; almost none says *what observation would show that
it did*. The delta sought externally was therefore not new concepts but:

1. failure detectors for distinctions v2 already holds;
2. the boundary between what can be mechanically enforced and what can only be observed;
3. implementation machinery for receipts that carry their own check;
4. a non-"newest-wins" formal treatment of evidence return;
5. falsification of any analogy v2 is relying on without prerequisites.
