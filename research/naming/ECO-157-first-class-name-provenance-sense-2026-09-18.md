## 2. Executive Sense synthesis

**FACT — SIGMA is the strongest recovered first-class naming precedent.** Its canonical expansion is **Stratified Integral Governance Meta-Architecture**. The March 8, 2026 BRAIN capture explicitly records the naming doctrine: **function-first; every letter earns its place from a real discipline; art emerges from rigor.** The same source says SIGMA's mathematical and cultural resonances were discovered after precise functional naming rather than used as candidate seeds.

**FACT — BRAIN independently reproduced the same pattern.** **Brain Retrieval Associative Integration Node** maps every letter to a real disciplinary term while the resulting word is immediately meaningful, pronounceable, memorable, and operationally invocable.

**FACT — ECOS followed a different path.** “Effortless Connection Operating System” is explicitly recorded as a **brand-layer expansion**, not a SIGMA-compliant constitutional expansion. The Operating System framing was separately defended as legitimate, while an internally rigorous ECOS expansion remained unresolved at that point. ECOS nevertheless persisted successfully because the token itself is compact, pronounceable, strongly tied to Effortless Connection, naturally evokes ecosystem, and names the overall operating-system identity without needing its expansion every time.

**FACT — the later SIGMA Naming Protocol did not create SIGMA.** It was derived from SIGMA's success. Its own provenance says SIGMA was the original name and inherited its name before the protocol existed. The protocol formalized the observed pattern and later added retrieval distinctiveness, embedding-neighborhood testing, form-function recursive isomorphism, and a constrained saturation/inversion route.

**FACT — the historical Decoder Ring and Master Key were distinct referents.** The Fractal Decoder Ring artifact states:

* **Decoder Ring** = the written derivation procedure for a room;
* **Master Key** = the room-specific target + helps-rule that the procedure derives;
* room / board / scope = derivation context, not the Master Key itself.

The same artifact explicitly narrows the Master Key definition to avoid **output-vs-derivation blur**.

**INFERENCE — later ECOS usage progressively blurred that distinction.** By July, “Master Key / Decoder Ring” was sometimes spoken of as one scoped-meaning primitive family while still retaining the explicit rule that child-room keys are derived via the Decoder Ring procedure. Later Master-Key architecture accumulated focal object, frame, scope, sensors, authority/currentness questions, and other orientation work.

**CONSEQUENCE — the current ECO-153 function is best understood as a successor synthesis from that broader orientation lineage, not simply the historical Master Key under a new label.** This does not destabilize the current semantic contract. It explains why the old name became increasingly misleading.

## 4. Why SIGMA works as the model first-class name

### Semantic compression without filler

Every expansion term does architectural work. Removing or replacing a term materially changes the model. The expansion is compressed structure, not decorative lore.

### Proper-name behavior

SIGMA is short, pronounceable, visually distinctive, and natural in compounds: SIGMA layer, SIGMA invariant, SIGMA naming, SIGMA/ECOS.

### Progressive disclosure

A cold reader can use SIGMA as an opaque proper noun. A warmer reader can expand it. A deep reader can unpack the disciplinary semantics. The name works at several resolutions.

### Emergent surplus meaning

The Greek/mathematical and cultural associations add meaning without repairing a deficient definition. This is multiplicative surplus rather than semantic compensation.

### Form-function isomorphism

Σ/summation reinforces SIGMA's integrative/synthetic work. The form reflects the function without being required to define it.

### Retrieval distinctiveness

The token is compact and unusually discriminable inside the ECOS corpus. It functions as an address, not merely a description.

### Semantic resilience

SIGMA survived semantic corrections and architectural maturation because the name attaches to a stable structural center of gravity rather than incidental implementation claims.

### Human closure after rigorous scaffolding

The BRAIN naming lineage shows that disciplined derivation can narrow the field without mechanically producing the final name. Human recognition/taste can legitimately close the last gap once rigor has made the viable structure legible.

**Synthesis:** SIGMA fulfills “more than its fair share” because one compact token simultaneously provides addressability, structural description, disciplinary pedagogy, retrieval uniqueness, symbolic surplus, compositional use, and brand identity. Those benefits reinforce one another instead of trading off.

## 5. What ECOS teaches that SIGMA does not

ECOS is a successful counterexample to the claim that every first-class name must be a SIGMA-style acronym. The evidence explicitly distinguishes its brand-layer expansion from a possible deeper expansion, yet the token remained useful and durable.

ECOS succeeds through a different bundle: direct attachment to Effortless Connection, immediate OS affordance, ecosystem resonance, compact pronounceability, and enough semantic breadth to survive architectural change.

**Naming lesson:** a first-class identity can be carried by a strong token whose canonical semantic contract lives elsewhere. Exact acronym semantics are one high-value mechanism, not the only mechanism.

## 6. Naming-layer architecture exposed by the provenance

The historical Decoder Ring/Master Key blur and current BUILD-7′ separations make it unsafe to ask one name to carry every naming job. Sense therefore distinguishes five surfaces:

1. **First-class proper name** — compact identity humans invoke, remember, retrieve, and recognize as part of ECOS.
2. **Semantic descriptor / definition** — cold-readable phrase saying what the function is. “Orienting Composition” currently has standing only as a qualified proposal in this role.
3. **Procedure / operation name** — any explicit process used to derive, resolve, recompute, or inspect the function.
4. **Materialization name** — stored/transmitted representation. Current architecture already keeps Orientation Resolution separate.
5. **Machine identifier** — schema/API/implementation handle, qualified only when implementation warrants it.

**HYPOTHESIS:** the likely target architecture is **proper name + semantic descriptor**, rather than one descriptive phrase forced to perform both jobs. Shape must freeze or reject this; Sense does not silently adopt it.

## 7. Updated first-class naming requirements

### Semantic safety floor

A candidate must not:

* reify the function as one packet, key, document, or artifact;
* imply it creates or owns authority;
* collapse into current binding, qualification, scope, resolution, Move, or Question Forward;
* imply exhaustive/global closure;
* denote merely one constituent such as goal, frame, scope, or stopping condition;
* confuse the function with the procedure that derives or updates it.

### First-class identity requirements

The name should:

* be short enough for routine invocation;
* be pronounceable or otherwise effortless to say;
* support natural compositional use in ECOS prose;
* be memorable after low exposure;
* be distinctive in string search and semantic retrieval;
* remain viable as architecture deepens;
* feel like a peer of SIGMA, ECOS, and BRAIN without cosmetically imitating them;
* support progressive disclosure from token → descriptor → full definition;
* avoid requiring repeated explanation merely to function.

### Synergy / multiplicative-value target

Unlike the prior ECO-153 run, the next Shape should actively value surplus performance when it **emerges without semantic repair**:

* real disciplinary learning;
* multiple honest layers of meaning;
* etymological or symbolic resonance;
* form-function isomorphism;
* productive visual/typographic identity;
* useful acronym expansion;
* memorable bounded metaphor;
* strong verbal morphology and natural compounds;
* cross-scale applicability.

These are multipliers, not excuses. A candidate with a material semantic failure does not recover by accumulating aesthetic points.

### Process requirement

Retain the historical doctrine: **function-first → rigorous field shaping → human recognition/selection.** Do not reduce the process to acronym fishing, vibes-first brainstorming, thesaurus substitution, backronym repair, or mechanical averaging across material failures.

## 8. Eligible naming-method families for Shape

These are method families, **not candidate names**.

**A. Forward-derived real-word acronym** — SIGMA/BRAIN route. Decompose the stable function; use real terms; search for a compact pronounceable word only after the function map exists.

**B. Layered acronym / brand expansion** — ECOS route. A strong token may carry a brand-layer expansion while the deeper semantic contract remains separately explicit. Multiple honest expansions may be acceptable if they do not disagree about the referent.

**C. Real-word appropriation / bounded metaphor** — Crucible-like route. Use an existing word whose affordances are useful and whose literalization does not impose false structure.

**D. Earned coinage / transformed word** — FIBERR-like route. Coining, spelling transformation, anagram, or distinctive morphology becomes eligible when it solves a real retrieval/identity problem and still carries functional weight.

**E. Opaque proper noun** — eligible in principle under the Principal's newly explicit requirement, especially if semantic precision is cleanly delegated to the descriptor. It has a higher teaching cost and must repay that cost through exceptional addressability, memorability, retrieval, or brand fit.

**F. Symbolic / scientific / linguistic borrowing** — a culturally or technically grounded token may be strong if inherited meaning aligns without hijacking the referent. SIGMA shows the upside; collision testing protects against accidental term-of-art capture.

No family is privileged before Shape.
