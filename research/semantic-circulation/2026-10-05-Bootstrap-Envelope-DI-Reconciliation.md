## Operational reading rule

When ECOS uses a provisional scaffold, simplification, temporary schema, candidate realization, or replaceable mechanism, do **not** invent a parallel "scaffold convergence" primitive merely to preserve its provisional status.

Recover the applicable PGO and Bootstrap Envelope and make the decision-relevant distinctions explicit:

- target / magical observable;
- present scaffold or provisional realization;
- what freedoms are actually available now;
- authority and effect limits;
- preserved invariants;
- provenance and recoverability;
- what the scaffold does **not** establish;
- the replacement, restructuring, requalification or reentry conditions that matter for the current use.

The Bootstrap Envelope is distinct from a runtime **effect envelope** such as ECO-214's bounded authorization/effect window. One concerns the bounded realization/bootstrapping situation under PGO; the other constrains actual permitted effects. They may interact but must not be collapsed.
