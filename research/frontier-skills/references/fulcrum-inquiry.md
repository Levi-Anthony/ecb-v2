# Fulcrum Inquiry — Question Forward profile

Use when a proposed correspondence or uncertainty has a hinge whose alternative outcomes change the next decision. This is a specialized result within `ecos-question-forward` (historical draft PR #40), not another mandatory step or a substitute for its WHAT, WHY OPEN, CURRENT EFFECT, TRIGGER, ROUTE and provenance fields. Use ordinary Question Forward for a missing date, source, deferred action or consequential unknown without a genuine branch discriminator.

Record: **candidate correspondence → exact hinge condition → discriminating question → YES consequence → NO consequence → fixture/test → scoped standing update**. Include mixed/underdetermined outcomes when the alternatives overlap. Keep the user's exact proposition and quantifiers. A fixture must yield different decision-relevant interpretations across branches; if it cannot, say no current discriminator and preserve the open question and reentry condition. Designing a test neither runs it nor authorizes its side effects.

Example: candidate: source records determine a destination account. Hinge: whether two destination accounts can agree on source slots but differ on a newly required slot. YES (two such accounts exist): transport alone cannot determine destination coverage. NO under a specified complete, qualified bridge: examine whether that bridge's premises actually hold; no authority follows automatically. Fixture: identical load receipt with permission slot absent/present. Standing: transport-only sufficiency rejected in this fixture; exact record transport remains separately assessable.
