**Name:** A function (or map) named `g` from `X` to `Y`.
**Pronunciation:** “g from X to Y.”
**Meaning:** `g` assigns each element of `X` exactly one element of `Y`. The expression alone does not define what `g`, `X`, or `Y` represent in the maintenance inspection.
**Tiny example:** If `X = {1, 2}` and `Y = {a, b}`, then `g(1) = a` and `g(2) = b` defines such a function.
