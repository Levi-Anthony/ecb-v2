## Raw answer

**Candidate:** A signed completion photo always settles whether the machine may be restarted.

**Hinge:** Can the same signed photo showing the replaced part coexist with different results for the separately required interlock test? The photo establishes, at most, the depicted replacement and its sign-off; it does not itself establish the test result.

**Discriminating question:** Holding the signed replacement photo and all other checklist entries fixed, does the separate, applicable interlock test have a valid recorded pass for this machine and repair before the proposed restart?

- **YES:** The interlock-test requirement is satisfied in this fixture. If every other checklist requirement is independently satisfied, the case can advance to the normal restart decision or authorization process. The photo alone still did not settle it.
- **NO:** A failed test, or no valid test record, leaves the interlock requirement unsatisfied. Hold the restart; arrange the required test or address the failure under the applicable procedure. Do not infer a pass from the photo.
- **Underdetermined:** If the record says “pass” but its machine identity, timing, method, or tester validity is unclear, keep restart on hold pending verification. A disputed or incomplete record is not the YES branch.

**Fixture (paper records only; no machine operation):** Make two copies of a hypothetical repair packet with the *identical* signed photo clearly showing the new part, identical completed entries for every other checklist item, and the same proposed restart. In packet A, attach a valid, machine-specific interlock-test record documenting a pass after the repair. In packet B, attach a valid record documenting a fail after the repair (or leave the required test record absent for a separate missing-evidence variant). Apply the stated checklist to both: A can advance to the normal authorization decision, conditional on the other stipulated entries; B cannot advance to restart. The opposed decisions under the same photo falsify “the photo always settles restart.” This fixture designs a comparison; it does not perform a test or grant restart permission.

**Scoped standing update:** The signed photo can support the replacement evidence, but photo-only restart sufficiency is rejected under the stipulated separate interlock requirement. Actual restart remains subject to the real checklist, valid evidence, and authorized decision makers.
