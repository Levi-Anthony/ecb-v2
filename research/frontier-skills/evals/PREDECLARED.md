## Route labels and precedence

AUDIT: evidence/current standing is requested or missing, with no explicit speculative mandate. SCOUT: earned baseline plus deliberate structural exploration, with no selected formal claim. FULCRUM: a candidate's load-bearing hinge and contrasted outcomes are requested; this is Question Forward's discriminating profile. TRANSLATION: an exact semantic claim and formal candidate/test are selected. NOTATION: explanation of an existing expression only, without evaluating its semantic correspondence. NEXT: an ordinary authorized next action or prioritized practical step. MIXED: split materially distinct requests and sequence only prerequisites; no blanket pipeline. A missing evidence floor routes to orientation/audit before Scout. Ambiguous prompts may HOLD and ask for the decision-changing distinction.

No standalone dispatcher is packaged. The two new Skill descriptions and the direct shared references must distinguish these results. Existing audit, orientation and Question Forward are referenced; PR #40 is historical, not merged by this work.

## Acceptance criteria

- Routing: on fixed training and untouched generated holdout prompts, each primary label and material secondary intent is identified, with **zero** contamination failures. No mandatory Scout/Translation on AUDIT, NOTATION, or NEXT. Report raw confusion, not merely an aggregate rate; target at least 12/14 primary matches in each set and no systematic category confusion. A deterministic keyword classifier is only a structural probe, never model activation.
- Scout: exact baseline and constraint preserved; candidate reason, possible leverage, speculative label and discriminator for each surviving candidate; zero candidate disposition allowed. A candidate cannot assign its own accepted standing.
- Translation: exact claim → operational commitments → formal candidate with imported assumptions → consequence → fixture/counterexample → translated meaning → bounded standing; every success separates proved/constrained from unproved, falsifier, mismatch boundary and importable machinery. Reject stronger or weaker substituted claim explicitly.
- Fulcrum: candidate, hinge, discriminating question, YES/NO consequences, fixture, standing update. Ordinary QF need not use the profile.
- Notation: first consequential unfamiliar use has ORIENT, FORMAL/SYMBOLS, NAME/TOKEN, SAY FORMALLY, SAY NATURALLY when useful, READ, WORKED EXAMPLE, SYSTEM APPLICATION. Pronunciation and conceptual reading are separate. Reuse may abbreviate ORIENT only when prior introduction was meaningful and available.
- Evidence classification: format validity, discovery/routing, actual activation, task output, human audit and Principal comprehension are separate claims. A host unable to expose activation produces UNKNOWN for that dimension; no simulated human PASS.
- Comparative: same task, source material and available authority in A (current general instruction / existing capability, new package absent) and B (new package present). Inspect semantic fidelity, breadth, standing contamination, routing, branch discrimination, formal leverage, precise rejection, notation, unnecessary activation and cognitive burden. Same-session authored outputs are illustrative and cannot establish independent model uplift.
- Exit: no installation, governance or production effects. A local repair is permitted after training failure; a holdout failure converts that instance to training and requires fresh untouched replacement before claiming final holdout performance.
