## T07 — selected formal claim

**Exact target:** “Old load evidence determines all destination permission obligations.” “Determines” means any two destination accounts with the same source-visible records must have the same permission coverage. This is stronger than the inherited ECO-191/192 claim; the task expressly selects this target. The operational commitments are an old load receipt, a newly required permission slot, and distinct current-use authorization. A false positive would allow deployment with only old load evidence.

**ORIENT:** Restriction/reindexing comes from the mathematical study of indexed collections and categories. It normally retrieves records along a declared map of slots. Here it tests whether source-visible records uniquely determine destination coverage. Do not infer that the map is a valid ECOS semantic bridge, an authority grant, or a complete destination-disclosure algorithm.

**FORMAL / SYMBOLS:** Let source slots be `I={load}`, destination slots `J={load, permission}`; `i:I→J` sends `load` to `load`. Let `Y₀(load)=Y₁(load)={L}`, `Y₀(permission)=∅`, `Y₁(permission)={P}`. Then `i*Y₀=i*Y₁`, yet `Y₀(permission)≠Y₁(permission)`.

**NAME / TOKEN:** `i*` is “i star” (restriction along `i`); `→` is “to” for the map type; `∅` is “the empty set”; `≠` is “is not equal to.” Subscripts zero and one identify two accounts, not times or stages.

**SAY FORMALLY:** “i maps I to J; Y sub zero at load equals Y sub one at load equals the singleton containing L; Y sub zero at permission is the empty set, whereas Y sub one at permission is the singleton containing P. i star of Y sub zero equals i star of Y sub one, yet their permission components are not equal.”

**SAY NATURALLY:** “Both accounts look identical through the old load-only interface, but one has a permission record and the other does not.”

**READ:** Restricting each destination account to the source slots loses the permission difference. The source view is not enough to reconstruct all destination slots.

**WORKED EXAMPLE:** A hypothetical load receipt `L=load-test-7` appears in both accounts. `Y₀` has no permission receipt; `Y₁` has `P=grant-9`. The old checker reads only the load slot and returns the same observed input for both. The result says nothing about whether `grant-9` is valid.

**SYSTEM APPLICATION:** ECO-191's old-support check can remain green in both cases. ECO-192's exact record restriction transports/retrieves `L` lawfully under a qualified slot comparison, while destination coverage must ask for permission independently. **Formal consequence:** a two-account countermodel refutes transport-only determination of destination coverage. **Does not prove:** that any real deployment is permitted, that every relevant destination obligation has been discovered, or that whole reseating is a Cartesian lift. **Falsifier of an intuitive total YES:** the pair above. **Break boundary:** exact attributed record transport versus destination-sensitive obligation disclosure and authorization. **Importable machinery:** scoped reindexing laws for explicit record families, not semantic or authority promotion. **Standing:** DIRECT FORMALIZATION of this countermodel and the scoped local record operation; the broader reseating analogy remains STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE.

## T09 — notation only

**ORIENT:** A reindexing operator is used in indexed-set/category mathematics to move a family of records along a declared map between indices. Its normal job is controlled retrieval under a new indexing interface. Here it explains an already supplied expression; the star does not by itself tell us the direction or qualify a real-world mapping.

**FORMAL / SYMBOLS:** `f*: C_I → C_J`. For this toy convention, define `f:J→I` and `f*(X)(j)=X(f(j))`, where `C_I` and `C_J` are families of sets of records on the respective slot sets.

**NAME / TOKEN:** `f*` is “f star”; `C_I` is “C sub I”; `C_J` is “C sub J”; `→` is “to” in the operator's type. Another source may define star differently, so its exact definition must be recovered before use.

**SAY FORMALLY:** “f star, from C sub I to C sub J; f star of X, evaluated at j, equals X evaluated at f of j.”

**SAY NATURALLY:** “Use f to look up which old slot supplies each new slot's records.”

**READ:** This chosen rule transfers a family by following the mapping backward from each destination slot. It does not create records absent from the old interface.

**WORKED EXAMPLE:** Let `I={load, origin}`, `J={load}`, `f(load)=load`, and `X(load)={receipt-7}`, `X(origin)={note-3}`. Then `f*(X)(load)={receipt-7}`; the origin note is not present in this restricted view.

**SYSTEM APPLICATION:** If the given expression is ECO-192's local record retrieval under an explicitly qualified slot comparison, it can preserve a load receipt in the presented destination view. It does not establish destination permission coverage, actual current reliance, or whole-reseat validity. This is a teaching projection of the stated expression, not a new correspondence test or change in its standing.
