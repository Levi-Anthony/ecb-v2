# Translation Frontier

This is a bounded correspondence test, not a source of governing semantics or mathematical authority over ECOS. Current governing sources and the exact user claim outrank an attractive model.

## Trigger and intake

Require an exact semantic claim, referent, definitions, purpose, prior standing, source edition, selected formal target or question and bounded intended use. If no claim is selected, use Scout or the [Fulcrum Inquiry profile](../../references/fulcrum-inquiry.md). If only the expression needs explanation, use the [notation helper](../../references/formal-notation-human-audit.md) directly. For mixed needs, consult [routing and composition](../../references/routing-and-composition.md). Do not run a ceremonial fixed pipeline.

## Round trip

1. **Semantic checksum:** quote or accurately restate the exact claim, quantifiers, dependency, standing and intended use. Mark every simplifying or strengthening assumption as your own. If the model cannot address the user's claim, disclose the changed target and test it only as a separately scoped proposition.
2. **Operational commitments:** name observable consequences, source/currentness constraints, failure behavior and an ordinary fixture that would refute a claimed match. Recover source requirements rather than inventing them.
3. **Formal candidate:** define typed objects, maps, domains, direction and imported assumptions. Give the construct's normal mathematical job and why it is relevant; do not identify metaphor with mathematical structure. Use the [notation helper](../../references/formal-notation-human-audit.md) on first consequential unfamiliar expression.
4. **Formal consequence:** derive what follows from stated premises. A finite check can test a declared finite model but does not establish semantic equivalence or source truth. Record the exact scope of a proof, countermodel or inconclusive result.
5. **Fixture/counterexample:** compare the consequence to a concrete case, including a mismatch that could falsify an intuitive YES. Test both preservation of prior support and newly exposed destination obligations where the claim requires them. Never let a formal vocabulary hide an untested bridge or current-use premise.
6. **Translate back:** state what the mathematics proves or constrains, what it does not prove, the exact mismatch/break boundary, what machinery becomes legitimately importable and which use is still unsupported. Distinguish a local transport law from whole-event qualification.
7. **Standing update:** classify DIRECT FORMALIZATION, STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE, HYPOTHESIS, METAPHOR ONLY or REJECTED MAPPING, as scoped; retain rejected versions and reasons. Existing audit/acceptance owns any promotion. A precise rejection may be a useful result. If an upstream contradiction changes the outcome, stop and route it, without silently rewriting the claim.

Result trace: `source claim → operational commitments → model and assumptions → derivation → fixture → translated meaning → bounded standing and next discriminator`. Require a source-linked nonproof boundary for each claimed success. Stop after the selected claim and its decision-bearing implications; do not import a generic formal ontology or install anything.
