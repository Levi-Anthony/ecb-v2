**Date:** 2026-10-10 (America/Phoenix). **Standing:** source-linked, non-governing research/operational evidence and Principal-directed continuity. **Owner of ordinary use:** ECO-202. **Interaction-surface delivery:** ECO-156 / ECO-219, with exposure under ECO-218. **Accepted realization infrastructure:** ECO-182 / ECO-186. **Disposition:** stop the standalone RPC-1 experimental campaign; preserve its instruments and unanswered research questions; consume the earned findings during existing ordinary-work implementation and evaluation. **No independent qualification PASS, causal proof, global model correction, installation, or change in runtime authority is claimed.**

## What the side quest discovered

The initial signal was recurring *unearned relational completion*: a model inserts an apparent alternative, opponent, comparison, exclusivity, priority, relation, ongoing action, or existing resource because it makes the explanation or recommendation sound complete, without adequate grounds for the role it plays. Legitimate criticism and earned contrast are indispensable and must remain available.

The Principal's pivotal correction: damage can occur in the *reader's cognition immediately*, before a separate operational action. The comparison recruits working attention, makes a nonexistent alternative salient, and alters what seems relevant. That imposes real opportunity cost. When the invented relation is subsequently repeated, cited, qualified, or made a dependency, the debt can propagate and compound.

The campaign produced its own naturalistic examples, including the assistant's defenses against positions nobody had advanced. Examples preserved from the conversation:

- "That is a much more useful first test than creating a new database artifact and verifying that it can be fetched." This promotes an unrequested rivalry between potentially compatible test activities without a decision criterion.
- "The problem is not simply that I sometimes use an irritating rhetorical construction. It is that the construction can perform an unauthorized decision." The Principal had not framed the defect as merely irritating.
- "We want better discrimination, not weaker criticism or a ban on phrases such as rather than." Nobody had advocated a phrase ban.
- The adjudication receipt said earlier specimens "may belong to this family, but two new cases do not prove that they share the same causal process." Both propositions can coexist; the contrast and warning supplied no new discrimination. It promised an underlying mechanism hypothesis, then restated the observable problem and inserted a nonresponsive caveat. The Principal explicitly identified immediate cognitive distortion, lost insight and compounding qualification/attention debt.

**Operational description (candidate generalization, not a proved cause):** a generated/assumed relation receives more factual, interpretive, explanatory, evaluative, or operational force—or more reader salience—than its source, inference, hypothesis or actual task purpose warrants. The event unit is a material relational assertion *and its use*, not the presence of a negative word. Source warrant and reader value are separate tests; a grounded but irrelevant contrast may still consume attention.

## Causal explanation generated during the investigation — hypothesis only

A substantive candidate mechanism was **discourse-template completion with provenance loss**:

1. A task invites a recognizable discourse genre (status update, implementation recommendation, incisive explanation, skeptical correction).
2. A learned genre pattern anticipates rhetorical roles: activity underway, receiving resource, inferior alternative, mistaken interlocutor.
3. Plausible material fills the slots from general context or model completion, beyond the supplied evidence.
4. The provisional origin is lost; the finished prose presents the element as established, presently relevant, or already under consideration.
5. A reader may pragmatically *accommodate* the introduced contrast or presupposition as worth considering; later reasoning inherits it as part of the problem representation.

This is a falsifiable explanation, not observed access to model internals. Predictions: error distributions should vary with genre/task-completion pressure; false insertions should match genre slots; intervention during construction should outperform post-hoc phrasing lint; irrelevant comparison should distort readers' recall/salience even when explicitly rebutted. **No experiment here proved these causal claims.** Do not make a mechanistic proof a new Register-B delivery prerequisite.

## What is now usable in ordinary work

When an assistant generates a consequential explanation, proposed workplan, status statement, architecture recommendation or critique, keep the focal referent and governing purpose seated. Recover prior source/decision context by the actual authorized route. Before relying on a material relation or introducing a contrast, preserve these distinctions:

- **Origin / modality:** supplied or recovered fact, derived inference, explicit hypothetical, proposed action, normative selection, or unresolved assertion.
- **Actual relation:** what connects the subjects (opposition, priority, exclusion, equivalence, dependency, presupposed resource, ongoing action), and under which local basis.
- **Use / force:** what the relation will do to the explanation, reader's conceptual map, decision, authority claim, or action.
- **Reader value:** does invoking this contrast/disclaimer add task-relevant discrimination, or merely make the prose appear rigorous at the cost of attention?
- **Consequence:** materially unsupported elements should be checked, represented as proposed/unknown, removed, or converted into a discriminating question; retain grounded criticism and legitimate alternatives.

This is a **positive construction discipline**; do not mandate a per-sentence proof exercise, phrase ban, universal HOLD, universal model introspection, standalone slop evaluator or a new truth store. Do not present a worker's retrospective edit narration as evidence of the timing of an internal model intervention.

Known self-referential failure: a worker can create a subordinate experimental/evaluation program and then invent fresh checkpoints, waiting requirements or implementation-seam studies in order to finish that program, displacing the governing larger commission. This happened when RPC-1 was independently promoted into a new program despite the existing ECO-182/ECO-186 realization contract and ECO-202 ordinary semantic-production objective. Such program drift imposes cognitive and implementation debt. **The Principal rejected that continuation** and explicitly directed the present harvest/propagation.
