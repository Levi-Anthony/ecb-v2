## 3. Core architectural form

The strongest integration is **not** a seven-column record.

The axes are different semantic jobs:

* Level — constitutive dependency relation;
* Quadrant — inquiry-position generator;
* Direction — relational-transformation signature;
* State — actualized configuration;
* Line — longitudinal continuity/comparison structure;
* Stage — PGO-relevant Line-indexed projection of Level;
* Type — schema-indexed classification.

Flattening them into uniform “attributes of R” would reintroduce exactly the category collapse the individual contracts eliminated.

Therefore the integrated core is:

> **stable referents + attributable typed relations/claims + explicit situated bases + independently standing URG semantic contracts + domain-schema descent + typed change/requalification + composable projections.**

## 4. Referent contract

A **Referent R** is an addressable first-class subject of inspection or relation with stable identity sufficient to preserve continuity across descriptions, classifications, observations and representations.

Inherited constitutional rules:

* identity may precede description/classification/native binding;
* R != its representation;
* R != a Type classifier merely because it is classified;
* R may become mapper, map, schema, classifier, PGO, contract, event or other focal object in another inquiry;
* stable identity does not imply semantic completeness, truth, currentness or authority.

### Boundary and grain

Referent identity, referential grain and boundary/individuation remain distinguishable.

Changing B may:

* enrich the account;
* revise the boundary;
* require re-individuation;
* require Reseat/new referent;

depending on the declared continuity basis.

No one field silently decides this.

## 5. Relation contract

The universal core requires relations but must not impose one closed domain ontology.

A generic relation claim is:

`RelClaim(ρ, A, S, β, standing)`

where:

* **ρ** — relation-kind identity;
* **A** — ordered/role-bearing participating referents; arity is domain-declared;
* **S** — native schema/semantic source and edition;
* **β** — applicable situated basis/context;
* **standing** — evidence/warrant/currentness/authority as consequential.

Universal guarantees:

* participant identities are recoverable;
* relation kind is attributable;
* native semantics remain recoverable;
* basis/scope is not hidden;
* standing is not inferred from persistence;
* relation representation != relation truth.

URG does **not** require all relations to be binary graph edges.

## 6. Situated semantic index

`κ=(R,B,G,F)`

* R — focal referent;

## 8. Domain-semantic descent

The universal substrate must support:

> Universal Substrate → situated descent into domain-specific schema discovered/composed/ad hoc'd on encounter → Integral Models/Maps/Views → addressable referent systems.

Therefore:

* native domain semantics remain native;
* URG supplies identity, situated relation, axes, standing, change and projection grammar;
* domain schema supplies concrete engineering/physics/biology/software/etc. constructs;
* a domain schema may itself become a referent;
* schema/type/State basis changes are explicit and crosswalked.

No “universal core” success permits ontology capture.

## 9. Map / mapper / projection contract

A Map/View/Model is a representation/projection over referents and claims.

Preserve:

* mapped referent;
* mapper;
* map;
* mapping relation;
* purpose/use;
* frame/access;
* scope/resolution;
* evidence basis;
* omissions/currentness/standing.

Projection != referent.

Projection != currentness.

Projection != authority.

Projection != completeness.

The same underlying referents may lawfully support multiple views.

## 10. PGO interface

PGO / Governing Orientation is adjacent, not swallowed by URG.

Within URG it:

* selects consequential questions/distinctions;
* controls relevance for the bounded inquiry;
* helps determine adequacy/sufficiency/reentry.

It does not:

* manufacture referents;
* manufacture Level;
* manufacture Type membership;
* erase dormant possibilities;
* confer authority by preference.

The endorsed purpose rule remains:

> when assessing a mapping, make the assessment's own purpose and consequential frame assumptions inspectable so mapping defect can be distinguished from frame mismatch.

## 13. Unknown / absence / uncovered grammar

Preserve at least:

* UNKNOWN — evidence/semantics insufficient;
* NOT_ESTABLISHED — tested basis does not warrant the claim;
* INAPPLICABLE — contract does not apply on a declared basis;
* UNCOVERED — the current schema/grammar cannot represent the material case without distortion;
* CONTRADICTED/REJECTED — negative standing with warrant;
* DORMANT/NONCONSEQUENTIAL — not currently active without ontological erasure;
* UNEXAMINED — no assessment yet.

These are not one enum in all domains.

They are a noncollapse grammar for keeping absence, ignorance, irrelevance and falsity distinct.

## 14. Question Forward

A Question Forward is the structural aperture attached to unresolved decision-bearing material.

A sufficient QF names:

* the unresolved proposition/choice;
* the exact basis/current standing;
* the discriminating observation/evidence/signal that could change it;
* how alternative signals would route the next decision or reentry;
* where the result returns.

“Need more information” alone is insufficient.

QF preserves open possibility without keeping everything computationally active.
