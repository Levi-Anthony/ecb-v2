## 3. Inquiry steps and protected result grammar

1. **Seat** R, whole identity/persistence, B and grain, containing whole, active PGO (purpose, outcome, alternatives, support/undermining, stop), mapper/frame, native predicates, relevant occasion. If an omitted PGO dimension changes the determination, repair the seat before asserting Level.
2. **Nominate** neutral candidate referents/relations C. Do not create an already-constitutive membership set as evidence.
3. **Recover** source-qualified actual or stipulated organization O: role/arity, order, topology, coupling, norms, coordination, time and constraints, distinguishing representations from obtaining arrangements.
4. **Qualify** the domain-native positive realization/individuation criterion and its own source/standing; then determine whether the observed basis and organization satisfy it. Alternative sufficient bases, interdependent elements and replaceable tokens remain distinguishable from collective-basis dependence.
5. **Examine direction** under L4's original matched relata/constitutive predicate, not generic survivability or reciprocal influence. Distinguish local sibling peers under a declared native organization/grain. Do not assume global transitivity, numerical altitude or permanent hierarchy.
6. **Return** the supported relation, reasons, source/warrant/currentness and fourfold Quadrant v3 disclosure obligations; unresolved consequential variables each carry a **well-formed QF** rather than a bare status.

**WITNESSED:** locally justified positive constitutive relation, with its named basis and scope; not universal proof or automatic acceptance of all neighboring claims.

**NOT ESTABLISHED:** specified tested grounds do not justify the claimed relation; explain which criterion fails or what warrant is missing. A concluded negative result under fixed conditions is not artificially converted into UNKNOWN; if future conditions might make it consequential again, define the exact changed variable and corresponding reentry QF.

**UNKNOWN:** necessary evidence, criterion, comparison or participation is unresolved. This label is *only* metadata on an inspectable QF. No bare status, generic “need more research,” or empty `qf_ref` satisfies closure.

**HOLD / DEFER / UNCOVERED:** preserve different actual meanings (authorization, schedule, expressive gap, etc.). If decision-bearing and unresolved, attach the same calibrated QF obligation; do not use a different label to evade it.

**INAPPLICABLE / DORMANT:** valid only under disclosed relevant grounds and reactivation boundary, not as a convenient way to avoid a question. Ordinary settled positive and negative claims are not forced back into endless inquiries.

## 4. Question Forward completeness — direct Principal repair

The accepted Principal rule: “Bare UNKNOWN tags, and the like, become an escape hatch and are considered malformed under the Question Forward invariant. They need to be paired with a specific disclosure oriented calibrated question and an hypothetical example scenario that would trigger a warranted reopening inquiry of the specific variable.” Exact source remains at `brain:577e42cd-709b-4221-b19d-b0a8c42aa501`; this specification adds interpretation, not a rewritten Principal quote.

A **well-formed QF** for consequential unresolved matter identifies:

- **Variable:** particular claim, referent R/B, PGO, native predicate and criterion, exact occasion/comparison and current epistemic standing.
- **Disclosure question:** explicit, sufficiently calibrated interrogative in the relevant positive UL, UR, LL or LR disclosure, including correlated disclosures as needed. The four positions are not exclusive buckets; all apply to the same R.
- **Discriminators:** alternatives and what attributable observation, native normative source, access or evidence would support/undermine each; distinguish factual validity from testing/verification.
- **Hypothetical trigger:** specified *unobserved* but plausible new fact, evidence or situated change that would justify **reopening this exact variable**, not automatically prove a favored answer or launch an uncommissioned experiment.
- **Reentry:** accountable next action/consumer, destination, source/currentness requirements, alternative routes and sufficiency/stopping condition.

If the author cannot frame this, the deliverable is **QF MALFORMED — REPAIR SEAT/PGO OR SPECIFY THE VARIABLE**, not completion via UNKNOWN. This is a normative completeness obligation; no new mandatory database schema or enforcement mechanism is authorized merely by describing it.
