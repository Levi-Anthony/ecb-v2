- H = specified putative containing whole, with identity boundary B_H;
- C = candidate heterogeneous referents and/or relationships proposed as qualifying constituent basis;
- O = native-domain organization and relations among C and H, represented with existing typed-relation machinery where possible;
- chi = relevant situation/occasion/conditions for the comparison;
- G = PGO controlling inquiry purpose, success criteria, relevance and evaluation, which may also contribute to referent or role identity if independently established;
- A = current active focal attention, a navigation variable distinct from H and C;
- P = an authored PROPOSITION of constitution involving H, C, O and B_H under chi;
- W = the attributable qualified ACCOUNT of P, including sources, evidential access, standing, qualifications and QF.

**Candidate proposition, not self-certified truth:**

    P := “The organized basis (C under O) constitutes H-as-H at (B_H, chi), with direction C → H.”

**Qualified account:**

    W := (P, sources/evidence, mapper/frame/access, standing, supported distinctions, unresolved questions).

**Investigation of P:** Independently inspect (i) individuated participants/boundary, (ii) relevant organized realization, (iii) whether that basis qualifies to constitute H-as-H, (iv) structural direction under exact compared predicate and alternatives. Claims of factual constitution demand appropriate standing; failure to establish one dimension can yield explicit QF/UNKNOWN.

**Focal invariance, provisional:** If the target referents, boundary/identity criteria, native relation predicates and relevant actual conditions remain the same, changing A alone preserves the underlying relationship being investigated. It may change the scope of available evidence, the currently shown fourfold disclosure and the worker's qualified W. To compare W across mappers/frames, preserve attribution and source context; A alone never constitutes an event.

**Important:** P is only a representation of an asserted candidate; it does not materialize an actual relation.
