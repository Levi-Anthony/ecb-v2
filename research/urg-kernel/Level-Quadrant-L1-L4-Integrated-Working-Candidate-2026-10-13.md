## 2. The integrated candidate in ordinary language
**Level** is a warranted directed *structural interpretation* of actual organized constitution between specific situated referents and relationships. It does not arise merely from a label, role nomination, function's utility, causal dependence, an ablation verdict, mapper attention or general PGO relevance. A relation must have identifiable endpoints, independently attributable organized participation/contribution, occasion/boundary conditions when consequential, and evidence/standing sufficient for its declared inference. Its vertical direction is differentiated from causal influence, horizontal neighbor participation, outcome importance, and other types of dependence.
**PGO** controls why and to what resolution investigation proceeds: what magic-wand end state counts as done, what supports/undermines it, what can validate the goal and verify the actual outcome, what alternatives matter, and what unknowns remain consequential. It does not manufacture a relation.
**Quadrant v3** discloses four irreducible perspectives/functions of any fixed focal referent, at fixed appropriate boundary: UL proper determination; UR determinate manifestation; LL field articulation; LR enacted organization. Changing Quadrant perspective does not change referent. Changing focal attention by itself does not mutate previously warranted relationship endpoints or their actual constitution. Reseating creates a new inquiry subject, not a new world.
**Partiality** is integral: stable referents and typed claims permit any newly relevant physical, computational, normative, experiential, social, etc. constituents/relationships to be investigated without having represented the entire world already.

## 3. Decision ledger by L-condition
### L1 — Constituency
**Historical claim:** C is admitted constitutive under B; C is drawn from a set already labeled constitutive.  
**DECISION:** EXCISE this as substantive proof; preserve as circularity anti-pattern.  
**Why:** A pre-labelled set or Boolean certifies its own conclusion; neither a PGO stipulation nor passing a structural checker establishes actual constitution.  
**Preserved:** Neutral candidate nomination, role/configuration evidence, explicit unknowns, structural assessment only after warranted relation discovery.  
**Consequence:** No `LEVEL_WITNESSED` from `constituency=true` alone. PGO determines what to inspect, never who counts as a constituent.

### L2 — Organization
**Historical claim:** identity-relevant O over a constitutive basis is an independent universal witness requirement.  
**DECISION:** RETAIN actual organization and constraint topology as indispensable *information*, but not as a separately proven universal first-class primitive or Boolean gate.  
**Why:** Same nominal contributors can produce different outcomes under different coupling, authority or sequencing (redundant power control; committee majority/unanimity; coordinated vs scripted tango). Existing typed roles, constraints and relations can express those differences. Conversely trivial aggregates need not have rich mechanism-specific organization. Identity can persist while organization/performance changes.  
**Preserved:** Actual arrangement/role evidence and consequential constraints, domain-realization differences, role/identity distinction.  
**Rejected:** `organized=true` self-certification; universally requiring O to change R's identity rather than perhaps its realization or PGO compliance.  
**Consequence:** Organization is investigated at needed resolution; structural and performance questions remain distinct.

### L3 — Whole dependence
**Historical claim:** R-as-R depends on qualifying C realized under O.  
**DECISION:** RETAIN typed, outcome-specific dependence inquiry, but NO independent universal L3 necessity/sufficiency or success gate has been established.  
**Why:** A functioning whole can have redundant, replaceable, standby or alternative participants. Individually nonfatal ablation does not make those participants irrelevant. “R-as-R depends” is ambiguous between exact configuration, continuing identity, actual capability, operational success and validity; if defined tautologically it cannot serve as independent evidence.  
**Preserved:** Distinguish actual contribution; counterfactual necessity; contextual sufficiency; realized/stipulated/potential; identity continuity; explicit alternative and occasion.  
**Consequence:** A PGO failure does not automatically dissolve the referred whole; direct constitution and actualization of intended outcome are different target claims.

### L4 — Constitutive asymmetry / direction
**Historical claim:** lower basis is not constituted by same R in same reciprocal constitutive relation; a genuine reciprocal case is unestablished/unknown.  
**DECISION:** RETAIN the *directional discrimination* and exact-comparison safeguard; reject broader readings that prohibit reciprocal causal, functional, control-loop, or role-identity dependence. L4 remains a candidate relationship-qualification responsibility, not an independently proven primitive.  
**Why:** Vehicle detection can influence signal phase, while signal indication influences driver response; these feedback loops are reciprocal functional participation, not inverse whole/part direction. A situated role can derive its identity from its containing organization while its enactment helps constitute an episode, but identity-dependence and constitutive dependence are different predicates. If the *same* referents and *same* constitutive predicate genuinely yield incompatible reciprocal direction, return UNKNOWN/QF instead of inventing an altitude.  
**Consequence:** Neither direction of causal arrows nor direction of attention proves Level. Local vertical relation can coexist with horizontal siblings, reciprocal influence and functionally significant roles.

L5 recoverable boundary remains obligatory as general referent/inquiry precision; L6 no value/prestige/size/rank leakage remains inferential discipline. Their *relocation* from separate constitutive facts is WORKING, not ratified. No global numerical altitude, automatic mereological transitivity, or intrinsic rank is introduced.

## 4. Focal navigation is not structural mutation
Under fixed adequate PGO, stable referents/identities/relations and occasion, change active inquiry subject across D (driver), S (signal), Rᴳ (situated driver-role) or E (intersection encounter). The actual relations among independently identified D/S, the signal's display activity within E, D's enactment of role Rᴳ, and E's organization within journey J should remain unchanged. What changes is the inquiry projection, active question and fourfold disclosure.  
**DECISION:** Treat referent seating and Level relationship endpoints as distinct, cross-indexed objects. Do not encode “the current focus” as what *constitutes* anything.  
**Why:** A reorientation of attention does not cause a physical/social relation to occur or cease; it may change what the mapper knows or seeks. Actual configurations may change with stable PGO; PGO itself may change with stable objects. These are not interchangeable.  
**Evidence:** Constructed fixed-case traversal through D, S and Rᴳ preserved five nominated relations; this is a bounded *invariance demonstration*, not universal proof.  
**Falsifier:** Under identical target relations, predicates, conditions and warranted source, changing only the active focus changes the purported truth/standing of an otherwise identical Level claim.

## 5. The situated role and the goal
A goal **as a defining commitment/constraint of a particular role** can be a constituent of that situated role; successful outcome is a separate actualization/assessment condition. A role can be structurally more encompassing than its defining goal or responsibilities *when warranted*. This does not mean the signal must be a part of that role, nor that importance proves Level. Role identity, person occupying it, role enactment, target outcome and actual success require distinguishable references.  
**Decision:** Permit this candidate role composition with explicit PGO and conditions; no universal relation `role > goal > signal`.

## 6. UL-first generative fourfold inquiry
**Construction:** “As traffic signal S, during encounter E under grocery-journey PGO G, I need to produce lawful phase indications and respond to relevant conditions; I cannot independently accomplish D's safe arrival.” Place this **first-person authored construction as a UL-centered inquiry instrument** for S. Do not demand literal consciousness or assert it supplies independently witnessed experiential evidence.  
**Traverse fixed S:** UL interrogates the particular occurrence as itself (non-substitutable instantiation and relevant epistemic-access conditions); UR interrogates actual indication and response; LL interrogates domain-native articulated meaning (red/green lawful permissions and compatible operations); LR interrogates enacted coupling, coordination, detection, traffic participation.  
**Decision:** RETAIN as working operational inquiry method; the account *generates* independently evaluated Quadrant questions and receives their corrections. Preserve fourfold irreducibility; do not confuse constructed voice with actual source standing, and avoid repetitive epistemic disclaimers once the construction status is declared.  
**Consequence:** Reveals S's local responsibility versus the journey's entire intended outcome, promotes disciplined bidirectional information flow, and exposes a Question Forward naturally. This one example is a positive behavioral specimen, not a protocol reliability PASS.  
**Potential failure:** UL is treated as a bucket for imagined behavior or as merely UR restated in first-person without the positive proper-occurrence question. Conversely, U(L) generativity never licenses collapsing LL/LR or allocating neighboring referents permanently to a quadrant.

## 7. Preserved PGO specimen and actualization distinction
**Constructed test:** D leaves at 5:05 p.m.; intended safe lawful uninterrupted arrival at grocery store by 5:30; ordinary signals/stops permitted; at 5:12 S is red, D perceives/interprets and brakes V, signal cycles lawfully, D proceeds and arrives at 5:23 with required conditions stipulated.  
**Definition of done:** Actual destination arrival within time and all safety/lawfulness/interruption constraints satisfied.  
**Support:** Vehicle mechanisms, driver decisions/perception, meaningful signals, functioning controller/detection, organization of road users, relevant legal/social meanings.  
**Undermine:** Mechanical failure, noncompliance, misperception, conflicting movements, delays violating constraints.  
**Eval:** Distinguish actual observed/stipulated occurrence from requirements, infer only conditional conclusions for invented cases; retain unknowns. Specific stops can add time while supporting safety, so net value depends on end-state criteria.  
**Referents:** D person, S signal, V vehicle, E this signal-mediated encounter, J journey, G intended satisfaction criterion, Rᴳ driver-role. G the desired success condition is not identical to J the possibly unsuccessful episode.  
**Consequence:** S is a neighboring/co-participating referent relative to D; signal-display activity may contribute constitutively to E; S's physical installation is not thereby part of D. Both descriptions persist when focus changes; outcome relevance alone does not entail constitution.
