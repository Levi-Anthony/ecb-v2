## 1. Shape decision

Freeze the accepted four-direction grammar as **four partial directional predicates with paired-but-nonexclusive relations and a separate modal/activation dimension**.

Do not represent the universal source grammar as two signed scalar axes.

Do not require a universal quantitative intensity.

Do not claim metaphysical exhaustiveness.

## 2. Directional account

For a seated referent R under boundary B, declared conditions c, and a transformation account τ:

`Dir(R,B,τ,c) = (A,C,T,D)`

Each component is one of:

* `SUPPORTED`
* `NOT_ESTABLISHED`
* `UNKNOWN`

where:

* A = Agency / self-preservation
* C = Communion / self-adaptation
* T = Transcendence
* D = Dissolution

A directional account is multi-label.

## 3. A — Agency / self-preservation

### Positive characteristic

A is SUPPORTED when the relevant transformation is characterized by preservation, restoration, reinforcement or defense of R's identity-bearing organization, boundary integrity or autonomous continuity against perturbation/competing change.

Witness fields should establish, at the semantic level:

* focal R is explicit;
* an identity-bearing organization/boundary criterion is explicit enough for the use;
* the transformation preserves/restores/reinforces that criterion or counters a change that would defeat it;
* the account does not depend on intention, morality or consciousness.

### Anti-definitions

Agency is not:

* selfishness;
* isolation;
* no environmental exchange;
* immobility;
* one capability;
* PGO preference.

## 4. C — Communion / self-adaptation

### Positive characteristic

C is SUPPORTED when the relevant transformation modifies R's participatory coupling, behavior, coordination or relational posture in response to relevant co-relata/field conditions so that R fits, coordinates, exchanges or participates differently while retaining identity continuity as R.

Witness fields should establish:

* same-R continuity is preserved for the directional claim;
* a relevant participatory relation/field is explicit;
* R's relational posture/behavior/coordination changes in relation to that field;
* the account does not rely on moral/social interpretation.

### Anti-definitions

Communion is not:

* collectivism;
* loss of agency;
* physical merger;
* moral cooperation;
* mere contact;
* any external relation without transformation.

## 5. T — Transcendence

### Positive characteristic

T is SUPPORTED when R, its organized contribution or its recoverable lineage participates in the formation/stabilization of a newly individuated composite organization that can become a distinct referent beyond R alone.

Witness fields should establish:

* source R / lineage is explicit;
* a candidate composite is explicit;
* the candidate composite has an individuation/organization criterion beyond mere aggregation;
* R/lineage contributes materially to that organization;
* any Level claim is separately routed to <issue id="587e1735-2630-4f1b-b018-4c4cc3c0da97" href="https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-221/sense-formalization-urg-kernel-level-quadrant-composition-optional">ECO-221</issue>.

### Anti-definitions

Transcendence is not:

* “better”;
* spirituality;
* increased complexity alone;
* arbitrary composition;
* R receiving a higher numeric Level;
* any capability composition.

### Level interface

T may produce `COMPOSITE_CANDIDATE(R+)`.

It does not produce `LEVEL_WITNESSED` by itself.

Run <issue id="587e1735-2630-4f1b-b018-4c4cc3c0da97" href="https://linear.app/ecos-ops/issue/ECO-221/sense-formalization-urg-kernel-level-quadrant-composition-optional">ECO-221</issue> on R+ and its constitutive basis.

## 6. D — Dissolution

### Positive characteristic

D is SUPPORTED when the transformation relaxes, removes or dismantles identity-bearing constitutive organization such that R moves toward or reaches cessation as R.

Witness fields should establish:

* source R / identity criterion is explicit;
* identity-bearing organization is being weakened/removed, or the available/mobilized transformation is of that class;
* constituent/source lineage remains distinguishable from deletion/loss of evidence;
* no moral or developmental judgment is inferred.

### Anti-definitions

Dissolution is not:

* moral failure;
* accidental destruction only;
* deletion of the record describing R;
* every state change;
* developmental regression by definition.

## 7. Pair law — opposition without exclusivity

Preserve the historical paired structure:

`Pair(A,C)`
`Pair(T,D)`

Interpret Pair as:

> **countervailing structural orientations in the accepted directional grammar.**

Do not infer:

* mutual exclusion;
* complementarity;
* fixed-sum conservation;
* one signed scalar;
* equal measurement scale.

Therefore:

`A=SUPPORTED ∧ C=SUPPORTED` is legal.

`T=SUPPORTED ∧ D=SUPPORTED` is legal.

The pair relation captures structural tension/contrast, not a universal numeric algebra.

## 8. Mode / activation contract

Direction is the coordinate.

Modes are views/standing over the same coordinate.

For direction d:

### Tendency

`Tendency_d(R,τ,c)`

Neutral structural description that τ has or would have d-signature.

### Capacity

`Capacity_d(R,c)`

A materially supported d-signature transformation is available to R under c.

### Drive / pressure

`Drive_d(R,t,c)`

A supported d-capacity is actively mobilized/exerting directional pressure at t under c.

### Realized event

`Realizes(τ,d)`

An observed/stipulated event τ instantiates d.

This is an evidence/event relation, not a fourth mode or fifth coordinate.

### Same-R continuity

### Quadrant route

Direction classification and Quadrant classification are independent coordinates.

`DIRECTION_UNCOVERED(R,τ,QF)`

Therefore the contract claims **operational closure**, not metaphysical exhaustiveness.

## 11. Direction invariants

**D1 Focal indexing** — every direction claim is indexed to R/lineage and relevant boundary/conditions.
**D2 Multi-label preservation** — more than one direction may be supported.
**D3 Pair non-exclusivity** — paired orientations are not scalar complements by default.
**D4 Mode nonidentity** — tendency, capacity and drive are modes/views of the same direction, not primitive types.
**D5 Event nonpromotion** — a realized event is evidence of directional instantiation, not a new coordinate.
**D6 Level noncollapse** — T does not grant Level; D does not imply lower numeric Level.
**D7 Quadrant noncollapse** — Direction does not determine Quadrant.
**D8 Capability noncollapse** — capability identity does not determine Direction and vice versa.
**D9 PGO noncreation** — relevance does not manufacture direction.
**D10 Unknown preservation** — absent evidence does not force a direction.
**D11 Lineage preservation** — T/D claims preserve source lineage through referent formation/cessation.
**D12 Uncovered route** — material nonfit is represented explicitly rather than forced into the four labels.
**D13 Domain-semantic humility** — domain-native causal/dynamic semantics remain authoritative for their own phenomena; URG Direction situates, it does not replace them.
