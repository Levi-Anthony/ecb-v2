Individual does not mean an isolated atom or human person. Relational does not mean that every fact involving a relation belongs to a Lower disclosure. A collective can be explicitly seated as a focal whole; its own fourfold inquiry and its relation to the former focus remain distinct. Changing levels or following a participatory relation does not move the original record to another quadrant by itself. These definitions are explicit in the [current v3 contract](Quadrant-Disclosure-Contract-v3.0.md).

The original qualitative UL distinction remains intact: evidence, reconstruction and a new execution can characterize an occurrence without becoming acquaintance by instantiation or transferring its occurrence identity. The two structural coordinates neither replace the positive functions nor classify natural-language material without accountable characterization.
