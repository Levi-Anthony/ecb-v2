## 3. Level contract

### 3.1 Constitutive basis

Let:

`C_B(R)`

denote the family of referents/relations admitted as constitutive of R under B.

A **Level witness** is:

`W_L = (C, O, d)`

where:

* `C ⊆ C_B(R)` is a qualifying constitutive basis;
* `O` is identity-relevant organization over that basis;
* `d` records the warranted constitutive-dependence/asymmetry claim.

Write:

`C ≺_L R [B; O]`

when a valid witness exists.

### 3.2 Level witness conditions

A witness is valid only if:

**L1 Constituency** — C is admitted as constitutive under B.
**L2 Organization** — O is relevant to R being this R.
**L3 Whole dependence** — R-as-R depends on a qualifying realization of C under O.
**L4 Asymmetry** — the compared lower basis is not constituted by this same R in the reciprocal constitutive relation being claimed.
**L5 Boundary visibility** — B is recoverable.
**L6 No rank leakage** — Level does not by itself imply value, prestige, physical size, complexity, maturity or a universal numeric altitude.

### 3.3 Result type

A Level inquiry returns one of:

* `LEVEL_WITNESSED(W_L)`
* `LEVEL_NOT_ESTABLISHED(reason)`
* `LEVEL_UNKNOWN(QF)`

Do not coerce uncertainty into a relation.

### 3.4 Token-survival nonrequirement

L4 is a **constitutive-direction** condition, not an indefinite token-survivability test.

A specific constituent may be short-lived or system-dependent.

If the compared referents are mutually constitutive in the relevant sense, the candidate Level relation remains unestablished/unknown rather than being forced.

### 3.5 Locality

Level is a local relation indexed to R/B and the stated comparison.

No global integer altitude is required.

A witnessed chain:

`A ≺_L B` and `B ≺_L C`

may support relative altitude only when the intermediate identities/boundaries and relation meanings are compatible.

No unconditional global transitivity axiom is adopted.

## 4. Quadrant contract

For fixed R and B:

`Q_B(R) = S_B × A`

where:

`S_B = {Constitutive, Participatory}`

and:

`A = {Governing, Determinate}`.

### 4.1 Seat

**Constitutive seat (C):** an adequate answer addresses R under the current constituent boundary B.

**Participatory seat (P):** an adequate answer addresses the same R in relation with one or more relevant co-relata not counted as constituents under B.

A constituency change is not ordinary traversal.

### 4.2 Answer burden

**Governing burden (G):** an adequate answer establishes what permits, constrains, requires or excludes what may/must/cannot be the case.

**Determinate burden (D):** an adequate answer establishes what condition/configuration/event/change/relation/result obtains.

The same content can serve different burdens.

Words such as “rule,” “dependency,” “possible,” “actual,” or “observed” do not determine classification.

### 4.3 Four obligations

* **UL = (C,G)**
* **UR = (C,D)**
* **LL = (P,G)**
* **LR = (P,D)**

All four are structurally available as inquiry obligations.

Unknown evidence affects answer standing, not quadrant availability.

### 4.4 Compound question rule

Classifier result is:

* one position;
* `DECOMPOSE(q1...qn)`;
* `QUADRANT_UNKNOWN(QF)`.

Do not force a compound or semantically underdetermined question into one cell.

## 5. Cross-axis composition

Level and Quadrant are nonidentical.

A Level relation is **content/structure that can itself be inquired into**.

Examples:

* “What constitutive dependencies govern R?” → UL.
* “Which constitutive dependencies currently obtain?” → UR.

Therefore:

**CQ1** Level is not permanently assigned to one quadrant.
**CQ2** Quadrant traversal does not create/erase Level.
**CQ3** Boundary revision can invalidate both Level witness and Quadrant seat classification.
**CQ4** Enrichment can change what is known without changing R/B.
**CQ5** PGO can change relevance without manufacturing constitution.

## 6. Typed transformations

### T1 TraverseQ

Precondition: same R and B.

Changes:

* question / seat / burden.

Preserves:

* R;
* B;
* current Level witness unless affected evidence forces requalification.

### T2 Enrich

Precondition: new material is already admissible under B.

Changes:

* account/evidence/relation detail.

Preserves:

* R;
* B.

May require:

* requalification of affected claims.

### T3 ReviseBoundary

Changes B.

Requires:

* explicit continuity mode:
  * same R / revised boundary-account;
  * different existing R;
  * newly individuated composite;
* redisclosure/requalification of Level;
* reclassification of Quadrant seat.

### T4 Reseat

Changes R.

Requires:

* source/destination identities;
* continuity/formation basis;
* recomputation/redisclosure of Level and Quadrant;
* source history preserved.

### T5 Reorient

Changes G.

May change:

* relevance;
* active questions;
* sufficiency/reentry.

Does not by itself:

* create or erase constitution.

### T6 ChangeFrame

Changes F.

Requires:

* frame/access-dependent evidence and mappings requalified where consequential.

Does not by itself:

* change R, B or Level.
