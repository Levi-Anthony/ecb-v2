## 2. Proposed Level clause — normative candidate, not effective law

Candidate referents, roles and relationships are nominated **without presupposing constituency**. Recover their native organization, actual or explicitly stipulated participation, and the applicable positive realization or individuation criterion; independently qualify the criterion's applicability and satisfaction for **this specific whole**. Retain (a) organizational significance to that whole, (b) dependence on a qualifying **collective organized basis** while allowing alternative and replaceable individual participants, and (c) exact constitutive direction, with any reciprocal claim examined only for the **same relevant relata and constitutive predicate**. Distinguish constitution from causal/functional contribution, success under the PGO, mere collection membership, local peers, role identity and the act of refocusing the mapper. Preserve source, evidential standing, whole/boundary/grain, frame, occasion and comparison; do not infer rank, value or global numeric altitude. Return a witnessed Level relation only on positive semantic warrant. **Operational use / status checks:** For a particular new case, begin with the positive PGO qualification instructions (§1), then the inquiry sequence (§3), and check the governing source/standing crosswalk (§6).
