## Positive characteristic functions — provisional candidate labels

**Label-set standing: PROVISIONAL.** Proper determination, Determinate manifestation, Field articulation and Enacted organization are one candidate label set among several. No set has been selected. The descriptions below support ongoing discrimination and inquiry; their use in the installed structural carrier, authored cases or projections does not ratify this label set or exclude other candidates.

| Coordinate | Candidate function label — PROVISIONAL | Characteristic function and discriminator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UL — Upper Left | Proper determination | What obtains in/as the particular instantiation, with acquaintance by instantiation distinguished from information about its consequences. The proper qualitative character is not exhausted by adding an identity stamp to a manifestation record. Arbitrarily accurate reconstruction, inference or behavioral equivalence can be rich and useful without becoming that acquaintance or transferring occurrence identity. A perfect functional twin is another instantiation. This does not require ignorance to be preserved, privileged testimony, or a consciousness claim about every referent. |
| UR — Upper Right | Determinate manifestation | The referent's differentiated configuration, variation and response under specified conditions. A model can reconstruct consequences and response profiles; claimed actuality, counterfactual response and prediction retain their respective basis. Observability is an evidence route, not the generator. UR is positively characterized rather than defined as public UL residue. |
| LL — Lower Left | Field articulation | Organized relations of distinction, equivalence, implication and compatibility through which this referent's determinations acquire consequential significance in a field. In conceptual/representational domains this includes historically compounded meaning and norms beyond any participant's present beliefs or pooled records. The account must identify the referent's participation in that field, the relevant distinctions and what follows from them. Merely describing the mapper's concepts does not establish the focal referent's LL. |
| LR — Lower Right | Enacted organization | The actual organization of participation through which determinations are produced, carried, combined, sustained or transformed: operative arrangements, practices, couplings, exchanges and propagated effects. Stable operative arrangements count as well as events; a model of a connection does not establish its enactment. Constraints on this organization can be investigated here without relabeling LR as LL. |

Nearest consequential discriminators:

- UL/UR: acquaintance in/as the occurrence versus a differentiated account of manifestation. Recording the occurrence's identity is not acquaintance. Better reconstruction does not eliminate UL.
- LL/LR: articulated relations specifying consequential distinctions versus the actual arrangements realizing participation. A valid/accepted command and a connected actuator can vary independently.
- UR/LR: focal configuration/response versus organization of its participation. Hold R/B fixed; use the positive question rather than assigning a whole instrument or institution to a box.
- UL/LL: this occurrence's proper determination versus the field in which its distinctions participate. A participant's current belief is not the whole field.

For a nonconceptual domain, LL requires an explicit domain-native articulation account and its discriminating consequences. Physical coupling alone does not establish that account. If it is not yet warranted, retain an identified LL obligation with Question Forward. This local unknown does not erase the universal design commitment or demand metaphysical proof before useful installation.

## Structural axes and decomposition

Quadrant coordinates cross **Individual/Relational indexing** with **Left/Right disclosure** over one situated referent. The Universal Referent Grammar (URG) is the shared structural language; these are its disclosure coordinates, distinct from the operations that follow relationships between referents.

| Coordinate | Individual/Relational index | Left/Right side | Candidate function label — PROVISIONAL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UL — Upper Left | Individual | Left | Proper determination |
| UR — Upper Right | Individual | Right | Determinate manifestation |
| LL — Lower Left | Relational | Left | Field articulation |
| LR — Lower Right | Relational | Right | Enacted organization |

**Individual** indexes the individuated focal whole in/as its present instantiation: its proper determination and differentiated manifestation. Individuated does not mean human, atomic, isolated, or a single constituent. An organized collective may itself be explicitly seated as a focal whole; its further participation remains recoverable. Both Upper disclosures can concern constituent organization without changing this index.

**Relational** indexes the shared field and organization of participation in which that same focal whole's determinations acquire significance and have effects. The field is not the sum of individually enumerated records, and does not become the new focal referent by ordinary traversal. A constituent fact is relational in the broad sense without thereby belonging to a Lower disclosure: classify the actual positive function and declared index. Spatial extent and counting objects cannot supply that judgment.

**Left** discloses proper determination or articulated significance, respectively indexed to the individual or relational field. **Right** discloses differentiated manifestation or enacted organization under the same respective indexing. Interior/exterior are upstream terms for this distinction; this contract retains the sharpened positive functions as their operational characterization. Evidence visibility, public availability, private testimony, sentence modality and governing authority do not determine this coordinate. An actual determination can be Left, and a constraint on enacted organization can be Right.

Vertical **constitutive decomposition** follows whole–part organization and witnessed Level relations to constituents/containing wholes. Horizontal **participatory decomposition** follows typed relations to co-relata and their coordination. Each traversed or newly focal referent again has all four disclosure obligations. These decomposition directions do not generate Upper/Lower or Left/Right coordinates. A lower-level component is not thereby a Lower-quadrant disclosure; a higher-level collective is not thereby a Relational disclosure of the earlier focal referent.

The retired constitutive/participatory crossing compressed structural relationship inquiry; governing/determinate compressed answer modality and was used as a substitute for Left/Right. Removing their coordinate standing retains warranted constitution, participation, actual claims and constraints in existing Level, native-relation and attributable claim machinery, with exact participants, conditions and basis. No generic `qualifiers.seat` or `qualifiers.burden` field remains in the current carrier. Record a warranted relation through `relation_refs`, not a free-standing relational label. A compound question is decomposed into recoverable sub-inquiries as needed.

This contract supersedes v2's generic qualifiers, missing explicit Individual/Relational axis, and current-record shape. It preserves v2's four positive functions and common account requirements. It also supersedes the v1 seat × burden generator and its quadrant classification/coverage rules. Level locality/asymmetry, typed changes, source/standing and independent semantic families retain their accepted meanings. TraverseQ changes either or both disclosure coordinates over fixed R/B. A change of focal subject or boundary invokes the existing explicit typed change and requalification path.

The full URG core composes this successor with the independently standing Level, Direction, State, Line, Stage and Type contracts. State may be disclosed through different functions; a State record does not automatically count as UR coverage. Likewise Level dependencies, Type distinctions or Directional processes do not automatically constitute a particular disclosure. Useful connections require a stated relation and basis.
