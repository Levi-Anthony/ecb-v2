## Inferred requirements to test

These are task-derived candidate requirements, not claims that the user explicitly named them:

1. **Semantic version is part of the use basis.** A grammar change can alter meaning without changing query, referent, boundary, orientation or frame; assessment identity must therefore change.
2. **Coverage and resolution are independently enforced.** Four examined positions cannot erase an unresolved compound inquiry or a second unresolved record.
3. **References need a consuming resolution path.** Nonblank text proves syntax, not recoverability; test the writer-to-reader handoff at the actual use boundary.
4. **Currentness is selected, not inferred from proximity.** Prominent successor text must clearly subordinate conflicting historical directions at every active entrypoint.
5. **Round-trip and failure symmetry.** Verify write → read → interpret → project, plus stale/partial/legacy/unknown cases; no success-only qualification.
6. **Recursion preserves the same obligations.** A reseated model/report is assessed afresh and cannot inherit the original occurrence's coverage or standing by label.
7. **Repair evidence is not retroactive qualification.** Corrected documents and new tests do not promote old deployment, observation or custody claims.
8. **A replayable closure can survive loss of this conversation.** Exact sources, what changed, evidence, residuals and next action must be locally discoverable and mutually consistent.
