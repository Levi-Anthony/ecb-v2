Standing: successor implementation under the Principal-directed replacement Move. Names are working handles; mechanical and semantic evidence are reported separately in the replacement receipt.

## Origin and purpose

The Principal noticed the installed generator's compression defect. That discovery initiated the bleeding-edge Upper Left (UL) work and subsequent positive Quadrant definition hardening. The assistant's comparison with the installed classifier corroborated the mechanism; it did not originate the discovery or supply a new reason that first made the defect actionable.

Exact Principal clarification: “Yes, it was me noticing that specific compression defect that led to this bleeding edge UL, and then subsequent quadrant definition hardening”. The preceding exchange recommended one integral replacement on the enduring main path, retaining useful old distinctions independently and propagating consequential dependencies.

The defect is that constitutive/participatory seat crossed with governing/determinate burden determines the old Quadrant label and thereby compresses distinct positive disclosure functions. An actual proper determination becomes old UR; an actually obtaining articulated field can become old LR; a constraint on enacted organization becomes old LL. These are source-derived discriminator cases, not claims of new empirical observations.

The governing acceptance criterion is recovery of those lost distinctions and their mutually generative relationships. A two-axis factorization, final Namecrafting or proof of metaphysical universality is not an installation prerequisite. The Principal's universal fourfold axiom remains the design commitment: any referent discloses four different, irreducible, co-arising and directly correlated information types.

## Shared situation

Reuse `kappa=(R,B,G,F)`: focal referent R; boundary/individuation B; governing orientation G (PGO Namecrafting open); and independently attributable mapper/frame/access F. Whole/part witnesses, occasions/conditions, evidence and standing remain recoverable through existing records. This is not a new persistence ontology.

All four disclosures index the same situated referent. Source identity, claim standing, explanatory frame, evidence access and subject identity are independent of disclosure identity. Missing content does not remove an obligation. Shared evidence may support several disclosures; these are not exclusive bins for facts or objects.

## Positive characteristic functions

| Coordinate | Working handle | Characteristic function and discriminator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UL — Upper Left | Proper determination | What obtains in/as the particular instantiation, with acquaintance by instantiation distinguished from information about its consequences. The proper qualitative character is not exhausted by adding an identity stamp to a manifestation record. Arbitrarily accurate reconstruction, inference or behavioral equivalence can be rich and useful without becoming that acquaintance or transferring occurrence identity. A perfect functional twin is another instantiation. This does not require ignorance to be preserved, privileged testimony, or a consciousness claim about every referent. |
| UR — Upper Right | Determinate manifestation | The referent's differentiated configuration, variation and response under specified conditions. A model can reconstruct consequences and response profiles; claimed actuality, counterfactual response and prediction retain their respective basis. Observability is an evidence route, not the generator. UR is positively characterized rather than defined as public UL residue. |
| LL — Lower Left | Field articulation | Organized relations of distinction, equivalence, implication and compatibility through which this referent's determinations acquire consequential significance in a field. In conceptual/representational domains this includes historically compounded meaning and norms beyond any participant's present beliefs or pooled records. The account must identify the referent's participation in that field, the relevant distinctions and what follows from them. Merely describing the mapper's concepts does not establish the focal referent's LL. |
| LR — Lower Right | Enacted organization | The actual organization of participation through which determinations are produced, carried, combined, sustained or transformed: operative arrangements, practices, couplings, exchanges and propagated effects. Stable operative arrangements count as well as events; a model of a connection does not establish its enactment. Constraints on this organization can be investigated here without relabeling LR as LL. |

Nearest consequential discriminators:

- UL/UR: acquaintance in/as the occurrence versus a differentiated account of manifestation. Recording the occurrence's identity is not acquaintance. Better reconstruction does not eliminate UL.
- LL/LR: articulated relations specifying consequential distinctions versus the actual arrangements realizing participation. A valid/accepted command and a connected actuator can vary independently.
- UR/LR: focal configuration/response versus organization of its participation. Hold R/B fixed; use the positive question rather than assigning a whole instrument or institution to a box.
- UL/LL: this occurrence's proper determination versus the field in which its distinctions participate. A participant's current belief is not the whole field.

For a nonconceptual domain, LL requires an explicit domain-native articulation account and its discriminating consequences. Physical coupling alone does not establish that account. If it is not yet warranted, retain an identified LL obligation with Question Forward. This local unknown does not erase the universal design commitment or demand metaphysical proof before useful installation.

This contract supersedes the Quadrant generator in Level-Quadrant Formal Contract v1.0 section 4, its Quadrant classification examples in section 5, and the corresponding classifier/coverage rules. The full URG core composes this successor with the independently standing Level, Direction, State, Line, Stage and Type contracts. State may be disclosed through different functions; a State record does not automatically count as UR coverage. Likewise Level dependencies, Type distinctions or Directional processes do not automatically constitute a particular disclosure. Useful connections require a stated relation and basis.

## Common account and machine carrier

A current `kind: quadrant` record declares `disclosure_contract: ecos:quadrant-disclosure:v2` and one result:

- `QUADRANT_POSITION`: explicit `disclosure` (UL/UR/LL/LR), `content_ref`, `characterization_ref` and `conditions_ref`. Characterization identifies the attributable positive-function discrimination, not a self-certifying label. Existing context and fidelity are required.
- `QUADRANT_UNKNOWN`: a `qf_ref`; `disclosure` may retain a known obligation whose answer is unresolved.
- `DECOMPOSE`: at least two distinct `component_refs`, preserving recoverable sub-inquiries.

Optional `qualifiers` hold seat and burden. Optional `relation_refs` point to existing native-relation claims. No new generic edge or universal transformation algebra is introduced. The same referent/situation and independently standing source/claim envelopes are maintained.

Structural validation verifies the explicit contract, fields and lawful context. It does not perform semantic classification, dereference content, determine correct domain articulation, certify acquaintance, or establish semantic truth. Semantic adapters remain responsible for attributable judgments. Current inquiry coverage consumes the explicit disclosure field only after structural, same-situation and exact account-recovery checks described below.

Reseat retains source/destination identities and continuity basis. Generation of a new organized whole invokes the existing constitutive Level witness; refocusing alone does not create Level.

## Consumer enforcement — inquiry 0.3.0

The semantic disclosure contract remains v2. The inquiry producer/consumer protocol advances to 0.3.0 because its assessment basis now includes both contract editions and requires exact account recovery. `Disclosure.account_editions` declares one expected native digest per referenced content, characterization and conditions account. `fetchEvidence` independently recovers each account with identity, nonempty content, source/custody and CURRENT standing. Missing, ambiguous, stale or mismatched accounts do not supply coverage. Exact consumed accounts are retained in the full typed result and full projection; a reduced projection explicitly returns HOLD. Exit recovery rejects changed content or metadata even when an upstream digest incorrectly remains unchanged.

This verifies that the consumer receives the writer's declared account, not that the account's semantic interpretation is true. The attributable evaluator remains responsible for domain validity and the account's declared-use standing. The ordinary BRAIN adapter has no such producer yet; its HOLD is unfinished functionality, not a successful semantic execution. Local qualification supplies authored accounts explicitly and does not infer acquaintance from them.

Unknowns and compound records carry their own obligations irrespective of other coverage. Duplicate record identities cannot be counted. Preservation checks contract, edition and binding before writing; historical projections require their own historical reader and cannot be relabeled as current. These checks apply afresh when the model, report, receiver or transformation becomes focal.
