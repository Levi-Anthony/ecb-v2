## 7. Conditions for promoting independent structure

Promote a semantic difference into an additional independent formal slot, gate, primitive or consumer rule only when its *distinct job* is established. Possible affirmative warrants include:

1. a concrete same-situation pair whose consequential difference cannot be correctly represented and qualified through the existing situated/native relation semantics without loss;
2. a specific consumer-relevant failure to retrieve or apply a governing obligation, with demonstrated gap in current representation/consumer behavior;
3. a ratified, stable, mechanically decidable architectural liability for which distinct enforcement materially improves reliability at acceptable informational cost (per governing `docs/invariants.md`); repeated production failure is **not** a mandatory prerequisite.

For any promotion, first show which current control is insufficient, the consequential result of that insufficiency, and why a smaller suitable alternative will not preserve the required capability. The same evaluation applies to any proposed relocation out of deterministic enforcement: compact notation does not license losing a requirement.

A distinction may demand **separate semantic evaluation** while requiring **no separate data structure**. Conversely one compact stored record may be insufficient to ensure independent claims remain recoverable. Representation economy and enforcement liability are evaluated separately.
