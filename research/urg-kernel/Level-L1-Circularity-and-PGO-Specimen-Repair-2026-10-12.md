## 2. Frozen anti-pattern specimen: L1 circularity

**Historical source wording:**

- `C_B(R)` denotes "the family of referents/relations admitted as constitutive of R under B".
- `C ⊆ C_B(R)` is declared a qualifying constituent basis.
- **L1:** "C is admitted as constitutive under B."

**Bad proof:**
1. A mapper presumes or marks `A` to be constitutive, adding it to `C_B(R)`.
2. L1 asks whether `A` is admitted as constitutive; the answer is necessarily affirmative by the prior label.
3. A structural checker receives `constituency=true` and may report `LEVEL_WITNESSED` when other Boolean fixture conditions are also asserted.
4. The consumer mistakes this field-completeness result for independently warranted semantic constitution.

**Defect:** circular presupposition / self-certified admission, compounded by possible checker-standing promotion. Merely renaming "constituent" as "organized contributor" and then accepting an unexplained contributor label would reproduce the defect. PGO selection never manufactures participation or proof.

**Current replacement responsibility (not a new independently proven L1 primitive):** nominate **candidate contributions and relationships without prejudging their constituent status**; investigate their actual roles and organization in realizing the specified focal outcome under the explicit PGO, including source, occasion and evidence standing. Only after that may a structural Level interpretation receive whatever warrant the recovered relation justifies. Where unresolved, return an identified question rather than `LEVEL_WITNESSED`. The adequacy of the full successor Level relation and of L2–L4 remains under inquiry.

**Checker limitation:** current `check_level_quadrant_contract.py` and its retained fixture editions test declarations under supplied Boolean premises only. A reported `LEVEL_WITNESSED` in that bounded structural fixture output means **structurally complete under the stipulated labels**, not an actual semantic Level proof. Do not use it to certify admission or to qualify a source claim; update or replace the mechanical contract only through its separately qualified implementation route.

## 3. PGO specification for invented examples — required before inference

Every constructed thought experiment, counterfactual or derivation must first **state its active PGO with enough realistic discriminating resolution for that example**. PGO is universal governance; "PGO-relative" adds no distinguishing meaning. The formal long-form PGO schema and naming remain unsettled: these are adequacy questions, not mandatory permanent fields.

A sufficiently specified specimen generally makes recoverable:

1. **Point of view, initial contrast, focal referent** — who is mapping, what exact R/whole/outcome is investigated, which alternative or disputed claim.
2. **Purpose and intended result** — why this inquiry matters, actual functional requirements and success/failure/decision consequences.
3. **Seat, boundary and containing whole** — individuation, same/different referent and persistence mode, relevant components/roles at the investigated grain.
4. **Situation** — occasion, operative versus merely possible/represented states, scope of environmental conditions and native-domain assumptions.
5. **Evidence and alternatives** — what is stipulated (constructed) versus observed, how to examine actual functional/operational roles, relevant counterfactual alternatives, and mapper/source standing.
6. **Sufficiency, Question Forward and stopping** — what would confirm/disconfirm the intended discrimination, and which inadequacies require reseating/reorienting instead of asserting a relation.

These are **illustrative discriminator categories**, not a universal 6-field PGO template and not permission to move from assumed requirement to actual relationship. The PGO itself remains improvable through inquiry under the currently operative PGO.

When an omitted dimension could change the structural determination, stop that determination and repair the PGO or return a specific unresolved question. Do not treat the ambiguity as evidence that SIGMA must invent a universal ontology or independent primitive.

## 4. Previously confused advertisement case: diagnostic repair

The prior question "Is an advertisement a constituent of website availability?" was ambiguous because "website availability" conflated an operative service capability, a measured availability rate under changing workload, a possible website-business success outcome, and perhaps an availability-supporting marketing system. A demand increase can cause a measured failure without thereby establishing that advertising is part of the power-delivery architecture. Under a differently seated focal whole that includes demand generation or load shaping, its role can be different. This is an **R/B, function, granularity and occasion clarification**, not a generic exclusion of advertising from all possible whole–part relationships.

A valid subsequent comparison first fixes one PGO for all variants and reports any necessary change of seat/PGO explicitly; it must not preordain inclusion/exclusion merely through the declared boundary.

## 5. Next specimen: fixed PGO-ELEC-01 (constructed, not empirical)

**Mapper / inquiry:** Register B compiler inspecting functional realization and vertical/sibling organization; mathematical examples are invented and their assumptions are not claims about a real website.

**R:** one specified website service's **operative ability** to serve authorized GET requests to `/health` for a representative client during a fixed 10-minute test interval, with a requirement of at least 999 successes per 1,000 attempts at a stipulated 100-request/minute load, **including tolerance of up to one interruption of either independently sufficient power source during that interval**. This resilience requirement is a *constructed stipulation*, not evidence that any device actually meets it. R is neither the server hardware, the site's business success nor an individual response occurrence; those may be reseated separately.

**Containing whole / seat:** an end-to-end service-delivery system with hosting, power, routing, DNS, network and client endpoints. Investigate the contribution of A and B as candidate power paths and of a hypothetical advertising-driven demand change; retain distinct exact source/path and system identities, even when the monitored availability result is unchanged.

**Purpose / contrast:** establish which A/B configurations participate in the operative realization of the stipulated single-power-path-fault-tolerant availability capability, and how to distinguish constituent role, tested standby readiness, intended-but-unverified capacity, disconnection and external load influence. Compare observed service availability with stated functional power requirement; inspect newly discovered redundancy/affordances.

**Working boundary / domain assumptions:** Retain infrastructure, DNS, network and hosting as stable except for the specified variables; keep the fixed request-rate envelope and one-outage resilience requirement constant in all five variants; an actual change in load or intended outcome requires separate PGO review; A and B are independently capable of meeting the ordinary stipulated electrical load when successfully coupled and healthy; a specified transfer mechanism exists where explicitly declared. Whether a path is operative is not stipulated unless a variant says so. Advertising is initially a candidate cause of demand/load variation, not preclassified as constituent or nonconstituent.

**Occasion and evidence:** All variants use the same conceptual test interval and service requirement. Record exactly whether assertions are stipulated for a *fictional* scenario, domain consequences deduced conditionally, or require live observation; no invented real-world performance evidence.

**Stopping / contrast conditions:** Correctly distinguish (i) connected supplying path, (ii) verified hot standby, (iii) installed untested backup, (iv) disconnected nominal backup, (v) load-increasing advertisement. Do not infer token necessity from a negative single-path ablation, semantic proof from structural Boolean checks, or actual capability from merely intended function. Return UNKNOWN on missing actual-state/evidence conditions; reevaluate PGO if the meaning of availability or intended outcome silently changes.

No automatic assertion about every domain or a new PGO schema follows from this specimen.
