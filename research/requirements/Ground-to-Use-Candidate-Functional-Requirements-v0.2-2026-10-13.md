Missing local content does not erase the obligation; use an actionable, domain-native QF. Existing structural machine coordinates retained, English function labels provisional. Genuine Stage is the PGO-relevant Line-indexed developmental projection of a positively warranted Level relation, possibly after enrichment/re-seating. A change in Q/Γ regime alone does not confer Stage. [Source](https://linear.app/ecos-ops/document/eco-185-state-line-stage-shape-repair-v02-pgo-enrichment-re-seating-72cd51d5f51d).

External review's premature schema/Stage reduction is not governing. [Protocol](Ground-to-Use-Requirements-Validation-Protocol-v0.2-2026-10-13.md).
4. **Ground Path:** current bounded isolated source-level tests show false native predicate admitted and contradicted claim locally still ADMIT after global HOLD. Repairs to enforcement remain prospective. [Shape](../formal-semantics/SIGMA-Ground-Path-Evaluator-to-Reliance-Seam-Shape-2026-10-13.md).

**Status:** NEW PROPOSED, source-backed. **Proposed functional requirement:**  
> For each consequential projection of a seated referent, the Ground-to-Use Capability shall preserve four individually addressable, correlated disclosure accounts (UL, UR, LL, LR), each carrying its positively qualified local result or its calibrated Question Forward.

**Derivation:** Quadrant v3 structural axiom; Principal Oct 13 explicit correction.

**Positive and contrary validation:** Fixed R/B/PGO source→consumer projection; four distinct coordinates; underqualified LL retains QF; traversal/reseat preserves source and new fourfold obligations.  
**Decision:** Carry in v0.2 proposal queue; explicitly decide retain/merge/repair at full-set validation before assigning accepted G2U-FR number.

## C. Existing repair queue RV-01…RV-12 (all PROPOSED)
- **RV-01** (repairs FR-001,002): For each consequential inquiry, the Ground-to-Use Capability shall determine whether its Inquiry Context satisfies the applicable PGO sufficiency criteria before permitting consequential claim reliance.
- **RV-02** (repairs FR-010): For each claim proposed for positive consequential reliance, the capability shall require a predicate-specific qualification result that establishes the claimed relationship between the applicable native criterion and its examined grounds.
- **RV-03** (repairs FR-014,015): For each dependent claim, the capability shall preserve the necessary, sufficient, and defeating relationships among its qualifying grounds under the applicable conditions.
- **RV-04** (repairs FR-016,019-024): When mutually incompatible claims apply to the same relevant subject and conditions, the capability shall withhold simultaneous unqualified reliance eligibility pending a qualified disposition of their incompatibility.
- **RV-05** (repairs FR-019-021): Following a disqualifying standing change, the capability shall prevent the affected claim from being authorized for subsequent consequential use under the defeated qualification.
- **RV-06** (repairs FR-030): For each consequential use request, the capability shall determine the applicability of the claim's current qualification to the requested consumer, purpose, conditions, and occasion.
- **RV-07** (repairs FR-013-016): When a claim's qualification depends on stipulated premises, the capability shall preserve the applicable conditional standing in consequential consumer results unless separately qualified grounds establish the broader asserted claim.
- **RV-08** (repairs FR-004): For each reported actual-world occurrence, the capability shall distinguish the attributable occurrence report from any independently qualified claim that the reported event occurred.
- **RV-09** (repairs FR-027): When an unresolved variable affects a proposed consequential action, the capability shall permit that action only within the applicable Question Forward's qualified interim-action conditions.
- **RV-10** (repairs FR-007): For each source proposed as currently governing, the capability shall qualify its operative standing independently of its archival availability, authenticity, or historical standing.
- **RV-11** (repairs FR-028): For each consequential result delivered to an authorized consumer, the capability shall expose a usable route to the claim's native criterion, examined grounds, qualification outcome, limitations, and applicable challenge or reopening action.
- **RV-12** (repairs FR-032): When a proposed inquiry closure fails the active PGO's closure criteria, the capability shall withhold closed standing for that inquiry.

## D. Prior 35 functional requirements — immutable authored draft in this register (NOT accepted baseline)
These original v0.1 statements are preserved for source comparison. The review's dispositions attach by ID below; proposed repair RV candidates do not implicitly replace original text.

- **G2U-FR-001:** The Ground-to-Use Capability shall register an Inquiry Context for each consequential inquiry.
- **G2U-FR-002:** When an Inquiry Context lacks information required to determine the applicable qualification criteria, the capability shall withhold consequential claim admission for that inquiry.
- **G2U-FR-003:** The capability shall register each submitted claim with its proposition, subject referent, assertion type, origin, scope, and applicable conditions.
- **G2U-FR-004:** When a human or connected source submits an account of an actual-world occurrence, the capability shall preserve that account as attributable evidence with its reported occasion, observation method, and source.
- **G2U-FR-005:** When a governance decision is submitted, the capability shall preserve the decision event with its attributable actor, declared authority, decision scope, and applicable edition.
- **G2U-FR-006:** Before assigning governing standing to a submitted decision, the capability shall evaluate the decision's authority under the applicable governance rules.
- **G2U-FR-007:** When retrieving source material for consequential use, the capability shall identify its current or historical standing relative to the applicable source-governance record.
- **G2U-FR-008:** For each claim proposed for consequential reliance, the capability shall identify the native qualification criterion applicable to that claim's subject, scope, conditions, and intended use.
- **G2U-FR-009:** For each claim proposed for consequential reliance, the capability shall identify the grounds offered to satisfy its native qualification criterion.
- **G2U-FR-010:** Before assigning positive consequential reliance standing, the capability shall obtain a qualification assessment of the exact claim against its applicable native criterion and grounds.
- **G2U-FR-011:** The capability shall retain each Qualification Assessment with its tested proposition, criterion, examined grounds, method, evaluator, occasion, result, scope, and recorded limitations.
- **G2U-FR-012:** When a claim asserts that a rule or source applies to a particular subject, the capability shall assess that applicability independently of the rule's or source's existence.
- **G2U-FR-013:** When an inquiry uses a stipulated premise, the capability shall register the premise's stipulated status, origin, governing context, and applicability limits.
- **G2U-FR-014:** When a conclusion depends on a stipulated or otherwise qualified premise, the capability shall preserve the applicable premise qualifications in the conclusion's standing.
- **G2U-FR-015:** When a claim has independently sufficient alternative grounding routes, the capability shall preserve each route's dependency conditions separately.
- **G2U-FR-016:** When every applicable claim-local qualification obligation is satisfied, the capability shall assign reliance standing appropriate to the claim's declared scope and assertion type.
- **G2U-FR-017:** When native qualification establishes a negative finding, the capability shall record that finding with its supported scope.
- **G2U-FR-018:** When a required claim-local qualification cannot be established, the capability shall withhold unqualified reliance eligibility for the affected claim.
- **G2U-FR-019:** When a previously reliance-eligible claim fails a subsequently applicable blocking qualification, the capability shall revoke that claim's eligibility for the affected use.
- **G2U-FR-020:** When a claim's qualifying basis changes, the capability shall reassess the standing of claims that depend on the changed basis.
- **G2U-FR-021:** When a claim's consequential reliance standing changes, the capability shall reflect the changed standing in current consumer projections containing the affected claim.
- **G2U-FR-022:** When an inquiry contains an unresolved or defeated claim, the capability shall preserve the standing of other claims whose qualifications do not depend on that claim.
- **G2U-FR-023:** When all blocking inquiry obligations have reached dispositions permitted by the active PGO, the capability shall identify the inquiry as ready for its declared use.
- **G2U-FR-024:** When an applicable blocking obligation lacks a PGO-permitted disposition, the capability shall identify the inquiry as held for its declared use.
- **G2U-FR-025:** When a decision-bearing variable remains unresolved, the capability shall provide a Question Forward satisfying the defined QF information contract.
- **G2U-FR-026:** Upon receiving evidence of an event satisfying a Question Forward's reopening condition, the capability shall initiate reassessment of the identified unresolved variable.
- **G2U-FR-027:** When a Question Forward permits interim action, the capability shall identify the actions permitted under that Question Forward's governing conditions.
- **G2U-FR-028:** For each claim supplied for consequential reliance, the capability shall provide a Consumer Projection identifying the proposition, native criterion, qualification result, supporting grounds, scope, reliance standing, and available challenge route.
- **G2U-FR-029:** Upon an authorized consumer's request for a claim's qualifying evidence, the capability shall provide access to the recoverable evidence at its identified source edition.
- **G2U-FR-030:** Before delivering a claim as currently reliance-eligible, the capability shall determine its standing using the applicable current qualification state.
- **G2U-FR-031:** When a consumer retrieves a superseded claim result, the capability shall identify that result's superseded standing.
- **G2U-FR-032:** When an inquiry is proposed for closure, the capability shall evaluate the proposed closure against the active PGO's success and stopping criteria.
- **G2U-FR-033:** For each accepted inquiry closure, the capability shall record the result classification, achieved scope, qualification basis, limitations, and residual obligations.
- **G2U-FR-034:** When newly admitted evidence satisfies a closed result's applicable reopening condition, the capability shall reopen the affected result for reassessment.
- **G2U-FR-035:** For each consequential transformation of a claim or qualified result, the capability shall retain a recoverable relationship between the input qualification state and the resulting qualification state.

- G2U-QCV-02 and FR-025 share QF semantics: retain *one* accountable functional obligation when reconciling, with Quadrant-specific required condition in shared data contract or acceptance criterion.

- Preserve positive all-four coverage and actual Stage/Level inclusion without claim of omniscience; claims about universal empirical completeness are not implications of those contracts.
