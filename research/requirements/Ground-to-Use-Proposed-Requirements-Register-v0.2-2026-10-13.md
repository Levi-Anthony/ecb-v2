## New positive Quadrant requirements — proposed
G2U-QD-01 / Fourfold coverage preservation
For each situated focal referent governed by an adequate Inquiry Context, the Ground-to-Use Capability shall maintain four distinct, correlated and individually recoverable disclosure obligations (UL, UR, LL, LR) across inquiry, decomposition, transformation and consumer projection.
SOURCE: Quadrant Disclosure Contract v3, current fourfold design commitment.
VERIFY: V1; any absent current answer must not make corresponding quadrant disappear.
STATUS: ADD / proposed; trace to G2U-FR-001, 025, 028, 035. No new primitive authorized.

G2U-QD-02 / Discoverable unresolved disclosure
When a required disclosure lacks a positively qualified result, the capability shall make the missing disclosure discoverable with a native, actionable Question Forward that identifies its discriminating criterion, evidence, reopening example, owner and return route.
SOURCE: Quad v3 and URG QF contract + Principal QF correction.
VERIFY: V1; bare UNKNOWN, absent position or self-referential QF fails.
STATUS: ADD / proposed; intersect FR-025 — use semantic incorporation/merge if v0.2 revised FR-025 covers exact behavior.

G2U-QD-03 / Correlated discovery
When a qualified disclosure generates a consequential question about another disclosure of the same referent, the capability shall preserve the correlated question and its source, conditions, standing and applicable QF rather than reducing it to the originating disclosure.
SOURCE: Quad v3 mutually generative pair obligations.
VERIFY: V2; original fixed R/B and independent positive discriminators.
STATUS: ADD / proposed; inspect possible merge with FR-035 (loss-aware transformation).

## New developmental inclusion requirements — proposed
G2U-SD-01 / Stage as Line-indexed Level
For each claim of genuine Stage standing under an adequately specified developmental PGO, the capability shall preserve the relevant Line and an independently qualified Level relationship at the lawfully enriched and seated referent.
SOURCE: ECO-185 Shape Repair v0.2 ACCEPTED Register B — Stage entails Level, Level does not entail Stage at an arbitrary PGO.
VERIFY: V3; Stage without a native Level witness cannot be admitted; Q/Gamma regime difference alone is insufficient.
STATUS: ADD / proposed; NOT a new theory ratification.

G2U-SD-02 / Developmental constitution and asymmetry
When a developmental Stage claim asserts that a later organized stage incorporates an earlier stage, the capability shall preserve the PGO-qualified constitutional basis, earlier/later identity and continuity, relevant transformation, and limits of the inclusion claim.
SOURCE: ECO-185 Stage-as-Level repair and selected Level successor / Line / Direction.
VERIFY: V3, V4, V5; later organized whole can preserve earlier organization; genuine Level unrelated to current Line need not be Stage.
STATUS: ADD / proposed; review overlapping FR-014, FR-035.

## Earlier relevance requirements — preserved proposed
G2U-RC-01 / Relevance-independent recovery
For each retained consequential claim, the Ground-to-Use Capability shall preserve authorized historical retrieval independently of the claim's current relevance or reliance eligibility.
VERIFY: V10; subject to actual authorized retention and erasure constraints.
STATUS: candidate; possible refinement of FR-031.
G2U-RC-02 / Requalification on renewed use
When a retained claim is selected for renewed consequential use, the capability shall determine its reliance eligibility under the currently applicable inquiry context before permitting that use.
VERIFY: V10; resurfacing alone cannot restore warrant.
STATUS: candidate; possible merge with RV-06 / FR-030.
