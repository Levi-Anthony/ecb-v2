## 3. Non-negotiable validation posture
**Positive first, not precaution first.** Reconstruct the strongest *actually intended, source-supported* formulation, preserve its useful function and what it entails, then challenge it with a purpose-matched discriminator. Do not weaken an assertive architectural commitment into an optional question in order to contest an exaggerated version of it.

**No straw-man surrogate:** Every alleged falsifier specifies (a) exact quoted target, (b) seated R/B, (c) mapper and sufficiently realistic PGO, (d) source/edition/standing, (e) a genuine predicted consequence of that target, and (f) the actually discriminating evidence. A counterclaim that the target does not imply omniscient factual completion, total vocabulary coverage, or universal metaphysical proof does not falsify fourfold coverage when none of those were asserted.

**Adequate PGO before adjudication.** Record point of view, initial contrast, focal referent and containing whole, active mapper/access, conditions/occasion, positive magic-wand result, native helps/hinders examples (nonexhaustive), criterion, discriminating evidence, decision/stop, and consumer. When constructed experimental worlds matter, distinguish experiment PGO-I from situated-world PGO-S. If missing resolution causes confusion, enrich the PGO and re-seat lawfully before multiplying types, declaring orthogonality, or assigning failure.

**Preserve earned authority and scope.** Distinguish accepted contract, selected compilation proposal, experiment, scenario stipulation, historical superseded account, live implementation and observed connected-consumer behavior. The Stage⇒Level contract and Quadrant v3 axiom are controlling *for their established scope*. Do not turn absence of an unrelated universal proof into generic standing downgrade. Do not elevate a document hash or a recorded fixture into source truth or consumer success.

**D&I is constructive.** Differentiate independently warranted jobs and integrate their generative relationships. Independence of characteristics is not prohibition of composition. For Stage, a genuine Stage has an enriched, source-qualified Level witness; a Level does not automatically warrant a Stage without a Line and PGO. For Quadrants, fourfold coverage is an affirmative generative structural design commitment, not just four optional tags.

## 4. Seven gates (evaluate requirement and set)
- **G0 Situated purpose:** Identify right referent, PGO, active positive purpose, consumers and boundaries. Fail only on an actual PGO resolution defect that changes the resulting qualification.
- **G1 Source and necessity:** Trace each requirement to controlling Principal decision, source contract, necessary derived function or stakeholder need; indicate standing and supersessions.
- **G2 Individual quality:** One necessary normative behavior per statement; stable terms, bounded condition, measurable observable result; don't mandate a table, algorithm, specific data model or architecture without source necessity.
- **G3 Positive behavioral completeness:** Demonstrate intended positive success, negative/defeat handling, consequential unknown/QF, lawful conditional stipulation, recovery, change over time, and **fourfold coverage and generative discoverability**. A correct QF preserves an unresolved position; it is not a disguised absence.
- **G4 Set integrity:** Audit duplication, contradiction, missing causal/decision links, alternatives AND/OR dependencies, state dimensions, claim-specific versus whole-inquiry standing, and retention of necessary positive affordances. Check Stage⇒Level and Level⇏Stage under actual developmental PGO.
- **G5 Realizability and allocation:** Map required functions to existing semantic/projection/evaluator/consumer capabilities; retain implementation freedom unless a new structure is genuinely needed. Don't infer implementation from expressibility or source custody.
- **G6 Consumer/stakeholder validation:** Test an explicitly named consumer against a fresh discriminating application of recovered meaning, including currentness and a permitted next action, not merely repetition of supplied answers; obtain authorized stakeholder baseline acceptance separately.

## 5. Required test design / anti-attenuation instruments
For each candidate requirement, capture: ID; exact text; origin and edition; status (retain/repair/merge/reallocate); PGO/scope; positive behavior; contrary case; existing carrier; actual consumer; verification method and expected outcome; overlaps and dependencies; final decision; reentry trigger.

**Positive Quadrant coverage:** Under fixed R/B/G/F, require all four distinct, correlated disclosure accounts to be addressable in consequential output. Each is either locally positively qualified or explicitly unresolved via calibrated disclosure-specific QF. Traverse or lawfully reseat to another referent and demonstrate new fourfold obligations, origin/destination fidelity, and no misclassification of Level as Lower Quadrant.

**Discoverability and QF:** A missing native Participatory Left articulation must generate a question naming native ρ, sources, comparison, consequential field distinction, explicit hypothetical reopening trigger, owner and stop. An inapplicable or incomplete report must not erase the disclosure obligation. Recovered relation can generate a new warranted inquiry into other quadrants without claiming the others automatically proved.

**Positive developmental constitution:** Use ECO-185 fully enriched instructional-dressing PGO, or equally qualified domain, and show a later Stage preserving/reorganizing earlier constitutive capacity via a real Level receipt at the correct seat. Use separately: (a) chronology-only or status-change regime with no Level witness => not Stage, (b) genuine Level irrelevant to active developmental Line => Level without Stage, (c) hidden Level-bearing composite requiring lawful reseating.

**Ground Path native reliance:** Authentic source/true span => qualified ADMIT; authentic source/false span => NOT ADMIT; false discovered on reconcile => affected member loses reliance in admitted/current projection while unrelated qualified members survive. Verify exact source/criterion and attached standing, not just a second evaluator assertion.

**Lifecycle recovery:** Preserve a qualified but currently irrelevant claim without turning it false; retrieve under new PGO; requalify for actual use; distinguish resurfacing, requalification, reinstatement and authorization. Include both justified refusal of reactivation and justified positive reopening.

**Consumer recovery:** Give a named consumer exact received editions and a purpose-complete task; assess a novel case not answered verbatim in the seed. Record what the consumer actually saw, decision and limits, assess against predeclared criteria, and keep source-to-claim ground accessible.

## 6. Fail and closure rules
- **Must fail baseline admission:** a source-authorized positive requirement is silently weakened/dropped; contradictory claims can both be relied on; false native claim passes ADMIT; a defeated claim still reaches a subsequent relying consumer; historical edition is silently governing; fourfold coverage is lost; genuine Stage is declared without Level after full legitimate PGO enrichment; bare unresolved quadrant is treated as coverage; a required consumer outcome cannot be discriminated.
- **Repair, not universal skepticism:** if a formulation has an actual local defect, repair its exact wording/dependency/consumer consequence without weakening unrelated earned claims. If the missing evidence is local and decision-bearing, supply calibrated QF. Do not demand proof of propositions the original governing assertion never entailed.
- **PASS (candidate validation):** every important requirement is positively necessary, singular and verifiable; all bounded scenarios have determinate permitted outcomes; all four disclosure obligations remain recoverable; all genuine developmental Stage claims carry their Level witness under stated PGO; contradictory/defeated claims do not retain improper reliance; QFs make residual discovery actionable; traceability and interface allocation are recoverable. **Ratification** still requires an explicit Principal decision.
- **Residual:** record exact variable, native disclosure question, hypothetical evidence triggering reopening, next owner/action and stop; no standalone UNKNOWN/HOLD as permanent escape.
