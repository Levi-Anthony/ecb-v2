## Adversarial discipline: do not straw-man or attenuate
For every allegedly overstrong claim: recover exact claim, its R/B/PGO/F, positive function and current earned standing; state its strongest SOURCE-SUPPORTED meaning; identify actual entailments before testing; propose one genuinely discriminating positive case and one strongest coherent negative case; enrich and re-seat before adjudication. Do not introduce universal omniscience, arbitrary global ontology, or irrelevant proof burdens then reject that invented claim. Lack of universal empirical proof is not a reason to demote an explicitly adopted Register B working axiom. Retain qualified limitations ONLY where they change the exact proposition's reliance.
