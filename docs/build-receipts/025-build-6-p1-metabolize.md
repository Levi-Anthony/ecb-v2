# P1 Metabolize

P1 completed and was independently reconstructed before this Metabolize. The protected human entrance worked, the exact decision was committed through the human session, the restricted executor performed the authorized succession once, and cold recovery plus independent canonical reconstruction matched the intended result.

The human operating experience does not receive the same PASS.

## Direct human evidence

Levi reported that he is operating several abstraction layers below the architecture and on substantial trust in the system's first principles and operational ideals. At the end of the proving episode he could not readily explain what P1 is or distinguish M1 from M2. The multi-day effort through BUILD 5–6 created substantial fatigue and made it difficult to judge the ceremony in isolation.

The human entrance itself worked, which is positive evidence for the protected route. However, Levi described the experience as cludgey and reported both high friction and a strong `why am I doing this?` feeling. He explicitly compared the experience against the v1 human-approval button, which had already produced significant friction, and judged the present ceremony to feel roughly an order of magnitude more burdensome. He is not yet clear how often this ceremony would recur in ordinary operation and expects the interaction to become smoother and more intelligible.

## Interpretation

FACT: The architecture successfully separated human authority, protected decision admission, restricted execution, recovery and independent reconstruction in the first live succession.

FACT: The current operator-facing vocabulary and ceremony do not yet provide adequate orientation for the human. M1/M2/P1 are implementation/control labels, not meaningful operating concepts to Levi at this point.

FACT: Manual policy copy/paste, digest/predecessor inspection and multi-step protected approval are tolerable as a first proving episode but are not accepted as the default steady-state ECOS experience.

INFERENCE: The problem is presently an operator-interface and orchestration problem, not evidence by itself that the underlying authority architecture is wrong. The same integrity laws should be preserved while hiding implementation choreography that the human does not need to perform or understand.

INFERENCE: A normal human decision should eventually feel like one intelligible decision: what is being proposed, why it matters, what changes if accepted, what remains unchanged, and one protected acceptance act when actual human authority is required. Exact bytes, digest/predecessor matching, custody, recovery and execution should normally be performed or verified below that surface unless exposing them is necessary to the human's decision.

UNKNOWN: The minimum implementation needed to achieve that steady-state experience has not been selected here. This Metabolize does not authorize a UI redesign, new primitive, or broader automation.

## Feed-forward requirement

Do not normalize the BUILD 6 proving ceremony as the ordinary ECOS human workflow.

Carry forward a usability constraint: human authority must remain real and protected without making the human act as a database/repository operator. Internal stage labels such as M1/M2/P1 should not be required knowledge for ordinary use. The interface should establish orientation and rationale before the decision, minimize transcription and manual verification, make recurrence expectations clear, and require a deliberate approval only when a genuine human-authority boundary is crossed.

This is a requirement on the operating layer, not permission to weaken exactness, recovery, provenance, or the separation of decision from effect.

## Closure implication

The friction is material evidence and must be represented in BUILD 6 closure review. It does not automatically invalidate BUILD 6, whose released contract explicitly used the first lawful succession as an implementation/proving episode and left human Metabolize pending. Closure Sense must determine whether BUILD 6's own released output contract is satisfied while carrying the operator-experience deficit forward as an unresolved requirement rather than falsely declaring the current ceremony production-ready.
