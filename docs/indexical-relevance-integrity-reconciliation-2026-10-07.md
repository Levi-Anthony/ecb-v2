## Semantic basis versus execution envelope

The repair rejects one monolithic “Context” object.

The **semantic/indexical basis** is query, intended use, authenticated actor and situated R/B/G/F context. It determines the `inquiry_basis_ref`.

A separate **execution envelope** records effective discovery seeds, caller-declared work reference, candidate/depth/projection bounds and return route. These can change a materialized projection without being the same semantic event as a change in G or R/B.

## Projection-delta interpretation

A delta is descriptive evidence, not a transition record. **R_OR_B** means focal identity or individuation boundary changed. **G_OR_F** means governing orientation or mapper/frame/access changed. State-record, evidence/standing and execution-envelope changes remain independently visible. Any can require requalification; only an attributable evaluator determines consequence.
