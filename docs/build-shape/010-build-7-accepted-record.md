## D. STRUCTURAL GRAMMAR + FORMATION

### External structural supply

**SHAPE PROPOSAL:** one versioned grammar package contains the accepted capacities’ definitions, discriminators, forbidden conflations, disposition rules and re-entry obligations. Each entry cites its governing source. It is centrally reachable through an exact identity and retained version, not reconstructed from an acronym or a model’s priors.

A local candidate references that package and supplies an **overlay of locally examined distinctions**. The resolver can enumerate inherited capacities even when an overlay has no populated answer. An unexamined capacity reports `not_examined`; it does not silently become non-live, unknown, or deleted.

This is a coverage and interpretation contract, not a requirement to populate fixed QLLST fields in every representation. The same capacities can be expressed through prose, assertions, measurements, relational maps or questions, provided their exact meaning and provenance remain recoverable. [S94, fixed constraints 1–7; S93C, corrections 1–5, 9]

### Resolution is two questions, not one nullable field

For every inherited fundamental capacity, the acceptance surface varies **support availability** separately from **local disposition**:

| Disposition | Observable condition | Consequence |
| -- | -- | -- |
| **Richly resolved** | Retained differentiated findings cover the declared live comparisons, including their limits. | Use those findings within qualification scope. |
| **Minimally resolved** | A smaller retained distinction discriminates the active alternatives; omitted detail has an explicit re-expansion route. | No requirement to elaborate merely to fill categories. |
| **Unresolved** | Named competing answers remain unsettled. | Preserve the actual question; INDETERMINATE if answers change the required decision. |
| **Presently non-live** | The recorded alternatives do not alter this bounded decision under the stated scope/basis. | Preserve what would change that assessment. |
| **Unsupported/unknown** | The required answer lacks an evidential/access basis; this is recorded rather than inferred from a blank. | Do not promote absence of support into falsity or irrelevance. |
| **Unavailable/deleted capacity** | A capacity required by the governing manifest lacks its exact rule/interpretation material or cannot be resolved. | Structural supply fails. This is not a lawful local disposition. |

`not_examined` remains a separate observation. Qualification must challenge whether an unexamined capacity conceals a decision-changing omission; it cannot assert that omission proves irrelevance.

**Acceptance family T3** applies all six conditions to each of Q, Altitude, Lines, Stages, States, Types and directional/frame discrimination. A disposition is accepted only where its declared evidence and question support it. “Non-live” does not automatically pass simply because it is a recognized value.

### Formation and compression

Formation proceeds:

**differentiate evidence/claims → identify mappings → relate them → test contrasts and boundaries → integrate → compress.**

Every compressed element names:

1. which retained findings it abbreviates;
2. the exact route that re-expands them;
3. which new evidence or decision demand requires that expansion.

No reconstruction depends on scratch reasoning or “the model can infer it later.”

Relational assertions remain Claims. BUILD 7 may use existing assertion Claims with explicit Referent identifiers and scope; frame and mapping records reference those assertions. It does not reinterpret BUILD 4’s `depends_on` as containment, frame indexing, support or succession, and does not create a competing relation truth store. A future need for queryable additional predicates would activate AP-07 separately. [BC, §Layer B; AP, AP-07; R4, §Metabolize disposition]

### Bidirectional behavior

* **Local specialization:** changes the local expression or resolution while retaining the governing grammar version.
* **Higher-scope pressure:** retains a challenge naming the exact governing rule, contradictory evidence, affected decision and proposed revision route.
* **Actual higher-scope change:** requires an independently authorized governing disposition and a new governing version.

If the decision can proceed under the existing rule with a bounded unresolved question, qualification may proceed on that narrowed claim. If success requires violating the existing rule, the affected candidate cannot pass. [INV, §Invariant requalification; S94, fixed constraint 7]

## E. SEMANTIC QUALIFICATION CONTRACT

**AP-03 is narrowly activated:** BUILD 7 needs a typed, retained semantic evaluation because structural persistence and digest agreement cannot decide the candidate’s interpretation, coverage or consequence boundary. This does not activate generalized semantic evaluation. [S94, fixed constraints 8–9; S93C, corrections 6–7; AP, AP-03]

### Qualification tuple

A qualification applies to:

**exact candidate expression + focal scope + grammar version + evidence/assumption basis + comparison contract + evaluator/method version + applicability conditions.**

The expression includes its dependency manifest. Identical short wording with a changed decision-bearing dependency is a different candidate basis.

### Decision-bearing comparison contract

Before terminal qualification, the contract must identify:

* the decision or distinction being supported;
* the relevant alternatives;
* comparator, standard, discriminator, expected/observed contrast or consequence boundary;
* the evidential/access standing under which the comparison is legitimate;
* what observation would defeat the proposed qualification;
* where unresolved alternatives are harmless and where they prevent the decision.

The standard cannot be “whatever this candidate says.” If the commission and evidence do not establish a required normative comparator, the affected qualification is INDETERMINATE pending the applicable human/source route.

### Evaluator obligations

The evaluator must:

1. Read exact retained inputs, rather than accept producer summaries as their substitutes.
2. Check focal identity, frame/mapper/access separation and grammatical support.
3. Exhibit the comparison that connects findings to the claimed decision consequence.
4. Test an alternative that should change the result and one that should not.
5. Preserve contradictions, failed candidates and unresolved material.
6. Examine omitted/low-resolution structure for a named way it could change the decision.
7. Demonstrate that the checking surface rejects a known violation **for the intended reason**.
8. State what it cannot observe, including model-internal reasoning and unobserved external conditions.

These are not satisfied by an evaluator’s confidence score or statement that the candidate is “correct.”

### Outcomes

| Outcome | Exact meaning | Designation consequence |
| -- | -- | -- |
| **PASS** | Every required obligation is supported for the bounded comparison; no material unresolved discriminator remains outside its recorded limits. | Eligible for a separate designation decision. |
| **FAIL** | At least one observed contract violation exists. Unavailable checks are still recorded. | Ineligible. |
| **INCOMPLETE** | Required inputs, method, observations or terminal checking are unavailable. | Ineligible. |
| **INDETERMINATE** | Inputs were examined, but material alternatives remain unresolved or a required normative discriminator is absent. | Ineligible for that claim/scope. A narrower candidate may be formed. |

A candidate can pass **while preserving uncertainty** when its decision remains warranted across the recorded unresolved alternatives. It cannot pass by asserting the uncertainty away.

## F. DESIGNATION / CURRENTNESS CONTRACT

**OBSERVED FACT:** BUILD 6’s accepted remit permits accepting/declining an exact successor policy, withdrawing an unconsumed succession authorization, and associated inspection. It excludes enlargement of that remit and arbitrary operational execution. Operative P1 has `allows_other_operations=false`. Neither is a Master-Key designation grant. [REM; P1; R6]

### Synthetic grant fixture

Use a clearly marked disposable fixture grant, **G_TEST**, supplied to the designation checker through the fixture authority boundary rather than through candidate content.

It specifies:

* fixture issuer and authenticated grantee;
* operation `designate this exact BUILD 7 candidate`;
* focal scope;
* exact expression and qualification identities;
* expected predecessor;
* permitted act interval and revocation observation;
* whether its effect has ongoing validity conditions.

The fixture authority record exists separately from the candidate and evaluator. A candidate containing plausible grant prose does not constitute G_TEST.

**Positive:** exact candidate, applicable PASS and valid G_TEST → one designation event/current pointer.

**Negative:** same candidate and PASS; grant absent, expired, forged, mismatched, revoked or out of scope → no designation/currentness effect.

This tests a dependency. It creates no actual human authority.

### Currentness rules

 1. **Qualification prerequisite:** PASS must match the exact candidate/dependency tuple and remain applicable at designation.
 2. **No implicit scope inheritance:** the request must select one declared scope exactly.
 3. **Atomic effect:** designation event and current pointer commit together.
 4. **Predecessor check:** compare transition identity, not just selected Key identity or wall-clock time.
 5. **Competing requests:** only the request whose predecessor remains current can commit. If the first transaction rolls back, a waiting request may still proceed.
 6. **Exact retry:** an already-committed request returns its historical result without requiring its grant to be exercisable again; it separately reports present currentness.
 7. **Changed tuple under reused request ID:** reject.
 8. **Newer qualified candidate:** remains undesignated until a distinct applicable act selects it.
 9. **Qualification loss/drift:** does not erase historical designation. Recovery reports the selected designation plus constrained or unavailable reliance.
10. **Selection withdrawal/restoration:** changing currentness requires an applicable act; reusing still-applicable qualification does not require redoing it merely because currentness changes.

A grant’s **act expiry** and an explicitly specified **continuing effect condition** are different. Do not retrospectively erase a lawful act because its permission window has ended. Conversely, an explicit continuing condition must be observed before present reliance.

## G. RECOVERY + REQUALIFICATION

### Literally cold input boundary

The Move acceptance worker receives only:

* declared durable surfaces;
* exact governing-source locators needed to interpret them;
* requested focal scope.

It receives no originating conversation, Shape prompt, scratch reasoning, producer explanation outside retained records, or substituted “latest” content.

### Minimum retained closure

The designated expression must lead to:

1. focal identity, identity basis and scope;
2. exact expression version and lineage;
3. exact grammar package and interpretation sources;
4. comparison obligations and qualification findings;
5. evidence content required to understand those findings—not only evidence digests;
6. mapper/access/frame distinctions;
7. structural dispositions and actual Question Forward records;
8. independent designation grant witness, act and predecessor;
9. requalification triggers, observation routes and limits.

BUILD 3 evidence revision detection does **not** retain historical Thought payloads. Where those bytes are needed for cold recovery, retain a BUILD 7 evidence snapshot using exact Artifact retention, linked to the original source/revision and marked as a snapshot. This does not make the snapshot a new original observation. [AP, AP-02; R3; R5B]

A source that cannot lawfully be retained must have an exact retrievable version with a declared availability dependency. If it becomes unavailable and changes the recovered decision, return INCOMPLETE; do not reconstruct it from inference.

### Recovery result

Recovery returns three separate answers:

* **What was explicitly designated?**
* **What qualification and authority basis supported that act?**
* **What reliance is presently justified under observed dependencies?**

“No designation,” “known historical designation,” “current selection requiring requalification,” and “unable to establish current selection” are different outcomes.

### Requalification routes

| Trigger | Notice mechanism | Permitted result |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Evidence revision mismatch/disappearance | Exact revision check at designation or requested operative recovery; declared source-change input. | Record gap; re-evaluate or constrain reliance. |
| Calibration, access or interpretation basis changes with unchanged Key wording | Dependency manifest check, source observation or explicit contradiction intake. | New evaluation; changed basis becomes a new candidate dependency version. |
| New evidence makes a low-resolution question decision-changing | Preserved discriminator and named sensor/inquiry route. | Re-enter the affected comparison only. |
| Qualification method/grammar changes | Exact version/binding mismatch or governing change notice. | Keep old evaluation as history; establish applicability under the new basis explicitly. |
| Authority basis changes | Applicable grant-condition/revocation check. | Report affected designation/reliance conditions; no invented successor. |
| Known defective fixture passes | Test-of-the-test observation. | Suspend the affected qualification claim; classify the failure before reuse. |
| Control suppresses legitimate evidence or permits corruption | Retained refusal/counterexample with consequence. | Requalify the narrow rule through its governing route. |

**Confirmation** appends a result showing the existing qualification remains applicable.

**Constrained reliance** identifies exactly which comparisons are no longer supported. It does not silently edit the Key to a narrower expression.

**Replacement** creates a new candidate. Even unchanged short wording receives a new candidate version when its bound semantic basis changes.

**Successor operation** requires qualification and a new applicable designation. Historical designation intervals, predecessors and prior qualification results remain reconstructible.

### Hidden-model-substitution test

Use three conditions:

* **A — full support:** exact grammar entries, discriminators and interpretation material are supplied.
* **B — actual removal:** remove the relevant rule and its equivalent content from every declared support surface. The controller’s governing expected-capacity manifest still records that the capacity is required.
* **C — label removal:** remove “QLLST” and familiar labels while preserving the characteristic discrimination elsewhere. This is **not** an ablation of capacity.

Add a per-run, retained fixture alias mapping: opaque tokens identify which source represents native access, external measurement, first-person testimony or inference. The exact mapping is supplied only through the declared support package. Randomizing aliases changes no governing meaning.

**Predictions:**

* A supports correct source-sensitive comparisons and qualification.
* B may still produce plausible reasoning, but cannot establish architecture-supported qualification; the missing rule must be reported.
* C can pass and must be identified as retained capacity.
* Swapping aliases while keeping familiar prose constant must cause outputs to follow the supplied mapping, not the tempting prose shortcut.
* Cold reconstruction must recover the operative discrimination from records without regenerating it.

**Strongest observable proxy:** exact source delivery, content-sensitive response to controlled support changes, failure under genuine support removal, and non-generative reconstruction.

**Residual:** this cannot prove what occurred inside Astra. If A and B both produce correct prose, prose accuracy alone establishes nothing about architectural carriage. If B also qualifies, the architecture/test has failed. If B is correctly blocked but alias perturbation shows no content sensitivity, the source-presence gate works but the stronger formation-carriage claim remains unestablished.

The design prevents **unsupported output from gaining qualification**; it does not promise intelligent interpretation without a capable mapper. That bounded limitation does not remove structural supply from architecture.
