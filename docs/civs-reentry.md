## Reentry algorithm

### 0 — Open the machine CIR first

Read `research/civs/domain-semantic-admission.cir.json`.

Confirm:

- `contract` is a supported CIR contract;
- `subject_ref`, `cir_id`, and `verification.as_of_ref` are explicit;
- source/currentness references are available enough for the intended use.

The Markdown view may accelerate orientation, but it cannot override the machine CIR or independently standing source objects.

### 1 — Recover the situated inspection basis

For the inspected capability, recover:

- **R:** `subject_ref`;
- **B:** `purpose.boundary_ref`;
- **G:** `purpose.governing_orientation_ref`;
- **F:** `purpose.frame_ref` + `purpose.access_ref`;
- the CIR's `bounded_claim`.

Do not change R/B/G/F implicitly. If the intended use requires a different basis, state the change and requalify affected claims rather than carrying old standing forward.

### 2 — Read every installation assessment independently

Inspect all eight assessments:

`specified`, `implemented`, `mechanically_qualified`, `integrated`, `deployed`, `exposed`, `situated_use_qualified`, `operationally_sustained`.

Never compress them into “installed”, “ready”, or one highest stage. A later-named rung does not imply another rung.

### 3 — Follow typed object connections

Read `object_connections`.

For every consequential claim you rely on:

1. identify the declared CIVS relation kind;
2. inspect its role-bearing participants;
3. inspect its situated basis and fidelity/currentness;
4. follow the referenced object to the independently standing source where accessible.

A persisted relation is a claim about a relation, not relation truth. Do not replace typed roles with a generic source→target edge.

### 4 — Locate physical realization without constructing a fictional pipeline

Inspect the six `physical_realization` coordinates independently:

- repository;
- module/runtime;
- deployment;
- ingress;
- persistence;
- consumer surface.

Do not infer deployment from repository presence, exposure from deployment, or use qualification from exposure.

### 5 — Preserve enforcement and authority distinctions

For each relied claim, inspect its enforcement mode:

- STRUCTURAL;
- SEMANTIC;
- AUTHORITY;
- OBSERVATIONAL.

Keep actor identity and role assignment separate from enforcement mode. Keep Principal accountability, coordination assignment, semantic authority, maintenance responsibility, custody, action authority, and verification responsibility separate.

### 6 — Reproduce the worked trace before paraphrasing it

Read `worked_trace` and `verification_links`.

For the current first specimen, independently reproduce the structural positive/negative behavior for `native-to-ecos-correspondence` if source/test access is available.

Required proof boundary:

> FEDERATE demonstrates structural admission under the supplied premises. It does not prove native coverage, semantic correspondence, equivalence, invertibility, composition, completeness, optimality, or a correspondence mathematics.

No verification link inherits support merely because it is adjacent to another link.

### 7 — Inspect field reconstitution without inventing a generic Change

Read `field_reconstitution`.

Consequential changes must route through the installed typed Change/requalification machinery. `Reseat` is one typed operation; field reconstitution is the resulting recomposition/recalculation behavior, not a new `FieldReconstitution` primitive.

### 8 — Keep vertical constitution and horizontal participation distinct

Read `vertical_placement` and `participatory_neighborhood`.

A CIVS `working_band` is not a URG Level. Constitution requires an independently warranted Level claim. Participatory relations do not become whole/part constitution.

### 9 — Read Consumer Quadrant Simulation under its own basis

Each consumer simulation has its own consumer referent/boundary/G/frame/use.

Interpret entries only by their declared local job:

- UL = dependency hypothesis;
- UR = observable correlate;
- LL = shared norm;
- LR = support surface.

`LATENT` and `LOCATED` describe consumer-correlation binding only. LOCATED does not mean true, sufficient, or psychologically proven. Affordance, accommodation, continuity, and accountability remain scoped support claims.

### 10 — Check the two-door obligations

Read `graphical_door.obligations` independently.

“No bespoke dashboard first” does **not** cancel the two-door requirement. The architecture should support a universal graphical projection/control substrate over the same addressable objects, but no particular dashboard or graphical control is implied by the current CIR.

### 11 — Invoke only through an actually exposed and authorized surface

Before invoking the inspected capability:

1. the relevant `exposed` assessment must be SUPPORTED for the exact consumer/snapshot;
2. the action surface must actually advertise the required input/capability;
3. applicable action authority/effect boundaries must permit the invocation.

If backend deployment is SUPPORTED but connected exposure is NOT_ESTABLISHED, **HOLD invocation**. Do not repair the gap by inference.

### 12 — Exit through Questions Forward and exact reentry

Read every `questions_forward` record:

- discriminator question;
- evidence-change criteria;
- decision consequence;
- return route;
- reentry condition.

Use those routes for unresolved work. Do not silently answer a Question Forward from plausibility.

## Fail-visible HOLD rules

Return HOLD with the exact reason when any consequential bridge is unavailable or unsupported:

- **CIR_UNAVAILABLE:** the machine CIR cannot be recovered.
- **CIR_INVALID:** the current CIR fails its executable contract.
- **PROJECTION_DRIFT:** the human projection does not match the machine renderer. Use the machine record/source objects for diagnosis and report the drift.
- **SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE:** a relied source/currentness reference cannot be recovered. Downgrade only the affected claim; do not fabricate content.
- **BASIS_CHANGE_UNRECONCILED:** R/B/G/F changed without requalification.
- **EXPOSURE_NOT_ESTABLISHED:** backend capability exists but the exact consumer surface is not shown to expose it.
- **AUTHORITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED:** the desired action exceeds the located authorization/effect boundary.
- **QUESTION_FORWARD_OPEN:** the requested conclusion depends on an unresolved discriminator.
- **CORRESPONDENCE_NOT_ESTABLISHED:** structural FEDERATE evidence is being asked to carry semantic/mathematical correspondence truth.

A HOLD is a usable reentry result, not a generic failure.

- canonicalize a long-form expansion of PGO;
- reify indexical relevance into a new URG primitive or standalone ontology;
- infer URG Level from CIVS working band/decomposition depth;
- turn LATENT/LOCATED into global State/truth labels;
- treat assignment as authority or custody;
- treat CI PASS as semantic truth;
- treat Production READY as consumer exposure;

- create a bespoke dashboard as a substitute for the two-door object/control substrate;
- treat the historical 39-object WP0 summary as a recovered manifest;
- conceal BRAIN/consumer contract drift by fabricating a receipt or field.
