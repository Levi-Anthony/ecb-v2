# ADR-005 — Cross-cutting integrity and promotion discipline

## Context and evidence

Repeated vertical slices have exposed cross-cutting failure modes whose handling otherwise depends
on rediscovery: confusing identity with meaning, a recorded operation with a checked result, checking
with acceptance, and a passing observation with proof of the intended obligation. BUILD 5A's D1
acceptance-specification defect also shows why the checking surface itself must be examined without
automatically blaming the implementation or silently rewriting governing requirements.

The [dated evidence promotion record](../build-evidence.md#promotion-record--2026-09-05-americaphoenix)
provides the source-to-law map: existing invariants, BUILD 0–5A receipts, FH-01–FH-07, intersecting
E01–E22 entries, and the pinned pre-hardening BUILD 5B Sense/Shape candidate. That candidate is
provenance only at `ea549649ad9993b0f89674227a2846423ce97076`; it has not acquired canonical authority.
Historical evidence entries and execution claims are unchanged.

## Exact promoted laws and governing locations

[Constitutive invariants](../invariants.md#cross-cutting-integrity-and-promotion-discipline) now govern:

1. **No silent promotion across dimensions:** target-dimension standing needs its explicit basis and
   governing route; persistence, execution, registration, or checking cannot confer it alone.
2. **Shared seam ≠ shared semantics:** shared technical machinery must preserve distinct obligations,
   scope, and meaning; it does not establish universal domain validity.
3. **Verification ≠ truth/authority:** identified checker/method/version, exact identified subjects,
   predeclared obligations, observations, and limits bound the result's evidentiary force.
4. **Role non-collapse:** operative roles and their distinct bases remain recoverable where they can
   change obligations, interpretation, or permission; one role does not confer another's standing.
5. **Proof sensitivity:** PASS supports its intended proposition only with contract-relevant
   discrimination of violation by that checking surface; otherwise retain observational/indeterminate
   standing. A happy path or an unrelated negative control is insufficient.
6. **Invariant requalification:** contrary slice evidence may trigger explicit preserve/narrow/amend/
   supersede/retire disposition through the governing route; no silent local exception is permitted.

The [Build Contract](../build-contract.md#build-sequence) now treats BUILD 0–10 as the current operative
dependency hypothesis, allowing earned decomposition/reordering only through governing disposition.
Its [standing pressure rule](../build-contract.md#standing-cross-cutting-adversarial-pressure) requires
each consequential Shape to disposition duplicate/replay, concurrency, stale state/basis, partial
failure/rollback, restart/reconstruction, wrong identity/role, wrong version, basis drift/revocation,
checker bypass/false PASS, retry/idempotency, time/order ambiguity, and unauthorized mutation.
Each applicable item maps to an explicit acceptance obligation or Shape constraint. Deferral requires
both a safe-deferral reason and an expiry condition. This is inquiry/disposition, not universal
implementation of all mechanisms. The governing files carry the operative text and limits; this ADR
cannot expand them.

## Why promotion is required now

Human review authorized durable laws before the recovered BUILD 5B Shape becomes authoritative.
Leaving them only in evidence entries or individual receipts would let a later consequential Shape
omit already-earned distinctions without even recording a disposition. Promotion supplies a bounded
review obligation and an evidence-driven change route without installing speculative machinery.
This is a cross-cutting hardening pass, not a new Build or a reopening of BUILD 5A.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Leave everything as candidate evidence:** would not constrain consequential Shape closure or
  require handling a previously encountered pressure. The human has now authorized the narrow laws.
- **Promote whole E07/E20/FH-04 entries:** would import unselected validator, role, and method assumptions.
  Only the named syntheses are promoted; the original records retain their historical standing.
- **Require a mechanism for every pressure in every Build:** violates structure-is-earned and confuses
  an inquiry surface with an implementation checklist. Applicability and leased deferral are explicit.
- **Treat the Build sequence as immutable ontology:** conflicts with already-earned 5A/5B decomposition.
  Treating it as optional advice is also rejected; the current order remains operative until changed.
- **Permit informal exceptions or unreviewable permanent laws:** the former silently removes protections;
  the latter suppresses contrary vertical-slice evidence. Explicit requalification preserves both
  enforceable present constraints and an accountable change route.
- **Use ADR prose as independent governing authority:** rejected. The human separately authorizes the
  narrow amendments in invariants/build-contract; this ADR is their provenance only.

## Reversibility and requalification

The laws are documentation-only but govern future decisions. Reversal or changed scope requires
explicit governing preserve/narrow/amend/supersede/retire disposition citing the contrary evidence and
affected obligations. Preserve previous text, this decision, and its evidence in history. An ADR alone
or a local implementation exception cannot change a law; existing invariants and hard-stop behavior
remain operative until authorized disposition. Closed Builds are not reopened automatically.

## Reopening evidence

Requalify if a real vertical slice shows a law conflates independent dimensions, cannot distinguish
lawful shared machinery from semantic collapse, overstates what a checker can discriminate, requires
an unearned ontology/mechanism, or prevents an earned dependency change. Expiry of a deferral requires
renewed disposition of that pressure. A checker passing a contract-relevant known violation is evidence
against its proof claim, not automatic permission to weaken the governing obligation.
