these exact versions? Recording an operation does not answer it.

## Findings and unresolved decision surface

**F1 — Stable subject identity is already required; physical version identity is not selected.**
The Artifact and what it represents cannot be collapsed. Independently inspected versions and receipts
need stable Referent identity under the governing invariant. Whether there is an additional enduring
Artifact-family subject, and how versions bind to it, remain Q1.

**F2 — Retained payload is a new, narrow obligation.** A Thought digest or Event history cannot stand
in for versioned Artifact payload. AP-02 is active for this seam without reopening its frozen Thought
projection or claiming historical Thought reconstruction.

**F3 — Verification scope must precede a claim of passing.** E07 puts checker evidence access and
producer/checker failure independence on the decision surface. E15 is methodological evidence for
declaring obligations before judging correspondence, not a SIGMA→ECOS correctness requirement. A separate
process, function, model, or agent label alone proves none of those. No specific independence mechanism
is selected, and no general independent-validator doctrine is promoted.

**F4 — Verification must remain distinct from authority.** A bounded check may establish correspondence
under declared rules. It cannot establish the truth of C, human acceptance, warrant, or designated
currentness. Within BUILD 5B, Transformation Receipt means evidence of a bounded check against
declared obligations on exact identified inputs/outputs. It does not mean human or governance
acceptance, warrant, authority, authorization, standing, or currentness. E07’s “accepted receipt”
remains unpromoted candidate vocabulary and imports no BUILD 6 authority.

**F5 — Publication and lineage contain the consequential concurrency questions.** Immutable input and
output do not by themselves bind a result to the correct operation or prevent forged success. Shape
must identify who can write a result and what can be observed if production, checking, or publication
fails. No choice of transaction, controller, trigger, or signature is made here.

**F6 — The additive decomposition is plausible, not yet implementation-proven.** The candidate consumes
TR1 without rewriting it. Shape must still show that Artifact provenance does not create a competing
canonical relation truth store or require redesign of the standing-transition mechanism.
