# SSMM episode

Explicit user instructions and current governing sources outrank this skill. SSMM organizes an episode; it does not by itself create warrant, authority, or a reason for the episode to begin.

## Sense

Recover what is actually being encountered.

- Identify focal object, altitude, telos, Master Key, relevant sources, standing, and contrast signals.
- Ask **What is this?** before selecting a solution.
- Preserve calibrated questions where the evidence is incomplete.
- Do not infer the intended Move from the phase title or nearby implementation.

Sense closes when the decision surface is sufficiently legible to Shape without smuggling in an answer.

## Shape

Develop the minimum discriminating structure needed to choose or bound a Move.

- Differentiate hypotheses, assumptions, evidence, controls, exclusions, and authority.
- Locate FCA rather than maximizing Control everywhere.
- Identify falsifiers, acceptance obligations, non-goals, and open apertures.
- Resist answer-shaped prompts and premature physicalization.

Shape closes when the candidate Move is bounded enough to execute and evaluate without hiding consequential uncertainty.

## Move

Perform only the warranted consequential action.

- Respect scope, authority, and stop conditions.
- Physicalize deterministic obligations only when they have earned it.
- Preserve semantic freedom where interpretation remains decision-relevant.
- Do not broaden the Move because adjacent work is convenient.

## Metabolize

Compare what happened with what the episode was trying to establish.

- Separate observation from evaluation.
- Check negative controls / discriminating sensitivity when claiming PASS.
- Record unexpected evidence, drift, failures, and surviving apertures.
- Decide whether to close, propagate, revalidate, reopen, or leave dormant.

## Fractal use

“Tiny SSMM” means a small/local projection of the same grammar, not separate machinery. Scale the expression to consequence; do not instantiate every phase ceremonially when the distinction is already trivial.

## Gotchas

- Trigger/origination, routing, and SSMM are distinguishable. SSMM answers how an activated episode develops, not necessarily why it became live or where it should go.
- Metabolize is not permission to retroactively change what the test meant.
- A phase may remain open when closure would destroy information.
