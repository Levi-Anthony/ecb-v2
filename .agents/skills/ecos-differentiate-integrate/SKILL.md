# Differentiate and Integrate

Explicit user instructions and current governing sources outrank this skill. Differentiation is an inquiry operation, not automatic schema promotion.

## Procedure

1. Name the **currently fused referent or term** and the practical work the fusion has been doing.
2. Recover **why the fusion was useful**. Do not assume imprecision means the earlier concept was simply wrong.
3. Identify the **contrast signal** that makes differentiation necessary: experiential difference, counterexample, failure mode, conflicting obligations, distinct causal role, or inability to reason cleanly.
4. Differentiate the candidate functions using discriminators such as:
   - what changes when this function is removed;
   - what it causes versus what it constrains;
   - what information it needs;
   - what failure its absence permits;
   - where its effect appears at different altitudes.
5. Test whether the distinction is best understood as a separate primitive, mode, pole, role, phase, mechanism, or merely a useful projection. Prefer the weakest ontological claim supported by the evidence.
6. Reintegrate: identify the **shared upstream property or Master-Key-relative function** that explains why the differentiated functions belonged together.
7. Preserve both levels of truth: the distinction must remain available without erasing the integration that makes the original concept coherent.
8. Label standing explicitly: **FACT / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / OPEN**.

## Reification checks

Before naming a new primitive, ask:

- Does the distinction change decisions, failure behavior, or causal explanation?
- Can the functions vary independently?
- Does a separate persistent object or schema improve integrity, queryability, transactionality, or reconstruction?
- Is a tidy symmetry seducing us into inventing structure?
- Could the distinction remain a dynamic expression of one capacity?

## Integration check

A valid D&I result should answer both:

- **Why must these be distinguishable?**
- **Why was it reasonable to experience or describe them together?**

If only the first is answered, the work stopped at differentiation.

## Gotchas

- Do not convert a useful 2×3 or other matrix into ontology because the diagram is elegant.
- Experiential difference is evidence of functional distinction, not proof of primitive independence.
- Shared upstream function does not erase downstream causal differences.
- Keep candidate names provisional when the referent is clearer than the vocabulary.
