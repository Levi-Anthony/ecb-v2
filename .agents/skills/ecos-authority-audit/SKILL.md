# Authority and standing audit

Explicit user instructions and current governing sources outrank this skill. The audit detects category collapse; it does not itself grant standing or warrant.

## Procedure

For each consequential claim or action, identify separately:

1. **Referent / subject** — what is being talked about?
2. **Evidence** — what observations or sources bear on it?
3. **Assertion / claim** — what proposition is actually being made?
4. **Verification** — what checker/method tested what bounded proposition?
5. **Confidence** — how certain is the evaluator?
6. **Standing** — what epistemic/governance/action status has been explicitly assigned?
7. **Warrant** — what valid basis authorizes which actor to perform which operation in which scope?
8. **Authority / authorization** — who or what can legitimately designate or change the consequential state?
9. **Currentness** — what has been explicitly designated operative for this scope?
10. **Capability** — what can the runtime/actor technically do?

Do not infer one dimension from another without an explicit promotion basis.

## Promotion audit

Before saying material became governing/current/authorized, answer:

- What was its prior standing?
- What target standing is claimed?
- What explicit route permits that promotion?
- Who/what had authority to perform it?
- What evidence records the transition?
- Can the prior state still be reconstructed?

## Verification audit

A PASS has force only for the proposition the checker can discriminate. Require a contract-relevant negative control or another explicit demonstration of sensitivity before treating happy-path success as proof.

## Retrieval audit

Never treat:

- newest;
- highest-ranked retrieval;
- most polished artifact;
- most recent chat;
- successful persistence;
- successful execution;

as currentness or authority by itself.

## Gotchas

- One participant may perform several roles, but the roles and their bases must remain distinguishable.
- Shared storage or identity seams do not create shared semantics.
- An ADR records a decision; it does not automatically authorize a constitutional change unless the required authority route exists.
- Technical capability is especially dangerous when it is mistaken for permission.
