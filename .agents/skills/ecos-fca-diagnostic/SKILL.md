# FCA diagnostic

Explicit user instructions and current governing sources outrank this skill. FCA judgments are Master-Key-relative and do not create authority.

## Orient first

Establish:

**focal object → containing altitude → active Master Key → FCA distribution**

Do not analyze FCA in the abstract if the governing purpose and altitude are unknown.

## Counterfactual tests

For each claimed capacity, identify the concrete Master-Key-relevant consequence of its removal.

### Control

Ask: **What violating possibility appears if this constraint or causal organization disappears?**

Control is not established merely because a rule, boundary, schema, or force exists.

### Freedom

Ask: **What necessary possibility disappears if this degree of variation is removed?**

Freedom is not absence of constraint. Identify the variation required for participation, exploration, adaptation, expression, or development.

### Awareness

Ask: **What consequential drift can persist without registration if this sensing capacity disappears?**

Awareness is not automatically correction, routing, authority, or action.

## Dynamic distribution

Do not assume equal amounts or fixed locations of FCA. Identify where each capacity is load-bearing now and whether the distribution changes across phases, roles, or altitudes.

Test for the Fish Studying Water failure: is a local mechanism being preserved after it has ceased serving the containing Master Key?

## Mobilization hypothesis

Current Shape hypothesis, not settled canon:

- Freedom can disclose as preserved possibility and, when mobilized, as initiative/exploration.
- Awareness can disclose as registration and, when mobilized, as attention/orientation.
- Control can disclose as bounds/regulation and, when mobilized, as directed actuation/trajectory shaping.

Treat these as differentiated expressions unless evidence earns separate primitives. Do not create a six-part ontology from the symmetry alone.

Read `references/pressure-registry.md` when activation pressures, Overshoot, Drives, triggers, or routing are implicated.

## Non-gesture test

For every FCA claim, state:

- functional referent;
- relation to Master Key;
- where it is instantiated or expressed;
- failure its absence permits;
- altitude at which the claim holds.

If those cannot be stated, mark the FCA mapping provisional rather than pretending completion.
