## Drives

Known upstream concept; underdefined in the current compilation.

Candidate functional category: **activation / motive pressure**.

Do not equate Drives with triggers. A trigger can establish *why now* while a Drive may establish a continuing tendency or pressure.

## Contrast signal

Working functional category: **origination / inquiry activation condition**.

A contrast signal is a difference that can make an existing frame, model, expectation, or state insufficient and thereby nucleate inquiry. Do not assume every difference is consequential; Master-Key relevance still matters.

## Trigger

Working functional category: **activation eligibility condition**.

A trigger answers when a dormant question, aperture, revalidation, or process becomes live. Triggers may be temporal, semantic, relational, evidentiary, dependency-based, or externally generated.

## System clock

Candidate mechanism, not primitive.

A genuine system clock generates temporal triggers without depending on a human remembering to invoke it. A manually ticked clock is an invocation surface with the human still supplying the clock function.

## Routing

Strongly identified function; exact architecture remains open.

Routing answers where an activated signal or episode belongs. Do not collapse routing into Awareness merely because Awareness detects the signal, or into SSMM merely because the routed work may enter an SSMM episode.

## Current dynamic projection

`latent field → activation pressure / contrast / trigger → mobilized FCA participation → SSMM episode → metabolization → dissipation / propagation / renewed trigger`

Do not promote this sequence into ontology or a universal linear state machine. Its value is currently explanatory and diagnostic.
