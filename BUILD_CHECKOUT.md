## PURPOSE

Define the minimum persistent typed-relation Claim that lets ECB assert one referent-to-referent
relation as a Claim while preserving the installed Claim, Evidence Link, and Referent distinctions and
creating no competing relation truth store.

## GOVERNING DISTINCTION

`ECB asserts that A stands in relation R to B ≠ A stands in relation R to B`

Keep distinct:

- relation Claim ≠ Evidence Link;
- assertion Claim ≠ typed relation Claim;
- asserted relation ≠ relational fact;
- confidence ≠ standing;
- relation assertion ≠ support, currentness, or supersession;
- relation assertion ≠ co-reference or entity resolution;
- directional position ≠ permanent ontological class;
- native binding loss ≠ Claim erasure;
- dependency assertion ≠ standing propagation; and
- classification output ≠ governing truth.

## INVARIANTS SERVED

- evidence ≠ assertion;
- confidence ≠ standing;
- standing ≠ warrant;
- capability ≠ warrant or authorization;
- relevance ≠ authority;
- current ≠ newest;
- identity ≠ description;
- unknown ≠ nonexistent;
- directional position ≠ permanent ontological class; and
- every persistent first-class Claim has stable Referent identity.

## FOCAL OBJECT

The smallest persistent typed-relation Claim required to express one referent-to-referent relation as a
Claim under a single relation truth store.

Not the focal object: a relation ontology, predicate taxonomy, graph traversal surface, co-reference or
entity-resolution mechanism, relation lifecycle, or inverse/symmetry machinery.

### First candidate

The initial candidate paired `depends_on` with the Sense fixture endpoints Claim C and the GT01 Thought
Referent, represented as a `claims` extension table.

**It failed its own second examination on two counts.**

First, `depends_on(C, T)` where T is a Thought asserts that a Claim depends on evidence. That is the
evidentiary relation already recorded by Evidence Link L. Two canonical representations of one fact
would violate falsifier 5 and blur falsifier 2. The endpoints, not the predicate, were wrong.

Second, the extension-table form could not enforce its own inclusion dependency immediately and
non-deferrably: nothing structural prevents a `claims` row of kind `relation` existing without its
endpoint row. Repairing that required either a deferred constraint, which BUILD 3 deliberately avoided,
or a trigger inverting insert control.

Both defects were repaired before the surviving candidate was formed. The record is retained because it
shows the duplication hazard is a live property of this build, not a hypothetical.

### Selected semantic representation

- **Second claim kind:** `relation`. It matches the Build Contract's "assertion or typed relation" and
  the glossary without inventing vocabulary. AP-01 narrows to exactly `assertion` and `relation`.
- **Predicate:** `depends_on`, meaning **the subject Claim's validity is conditional on the object
  Referent**. AP-07 narrows to exactly this one value.
- `depends_on` asserts neither endpoint's truth, support, confidence, currentness, standing, or
  authority, and triggers no propagation. It is falsified if any of those is read from it or produced
  by asserting it.
- Direction is `subject → object`. Reversal asserts a different proposition and is never equivalent.
- A relation Claim is an assertion by ECB, recorded with `origin=ecb_inference` and
  `epistemic_standing=unassessed` exactly as an assertion Claim is.

### Selected physical representation

`public.claims` gains exactly three columns and no table or function is added:

```text
subject_referent_id uuid null
predicate           text null
object_referent_id  uuid null
```

Constraint changes on `public.claims`:

```text
DROPPED  claims_kind_assertion          (claim_kind = 'assertion')
DROPPED  claims_proposition_nonempty    (length(btrim(proposition)) > 0)
ALTERED  proposition                    drop NOT NULL
ALTERED  claim_kind                     default 'assertion'

ADDED    claims_kind_vocabulary
         check (claim_kind in ('assertion', 'relation'))

ADDED    claims_kind_exclusive_shape
         check (
           (claim_kind = 'assertion'
             and proposition is not null
             and length(btrim(proposition)) > 0
             and subject_referent_id is null
             and predicate is null
             and object_referent_id is null)
           or
           (claim_kind = 'relation'
             and proposition is null
             and subject_referent_id is not null
             and predicate is not null
             and object_referent_id is not null)
         )

ADDED    claims_predicate_vocabulary
         check (predicate is null or predicate = 'depends_on')

ADDED    claims_depends_on_not_self
         check (not (predicate = 'depends_on'
                     and subject_referent_id = object_referent_id))

ADDED    claims_subject_referent_fkey  foreign key (subject_referent_id)
           references public.referents (id) on update restrict on delete restrict not deferrable
ADDED    claims_object_referent_fkey   foreign key (object_referent_id)
           references public.referents (id) on update restrict on delete restrict not deferrable
```

`scope` remains `not null` and non-empty for both kinds. The same-UUID `claims_referent_fkey` is
unchanged. There is deliberately **no** foreign key from an endpoint to `claims` or `thoughts`: endpoints
address Referents, so loss of an endpoint's native record cannot erase the relation Claim, while
`ON DELETE RESTRICT` prevents the Referent itself from disappearing beneath the assertion.

There is deliberately **no** uniqueness constraint over the endpoint/predicate triple.

`prepare_claim` is rewritten to register `NEW.id` in `public.referents` without conflict suppression and
to assign only `origin`, `epistemic_standing`, and `asserted_at`. It no longer overwrites `claim_kind`.
It remains `SECURITY INVOKER` with an empty `search_path`, schema-qualified, non-callable, and takes no
lock, because endpoint existence is a foreign-key obligation rather than a trigger read.

Service-role insert columns become `id, proposition, scope, claim_kind, subject_referent_id, predicate,
object_referent_id`. `claim_kind` is declared caller intent, coherence-checked by constraint; `origin`,
`epistemic_standing`, and `asserted_at` remain database-assigned and ungrantable. A BUILD 3-shaped
insert of `(id, proposition, scope)` still produces an identical assertion Claim through the
`claim_kind` default. RLS stays enabled with no policies; `PUBLIC`, `anon`, and `authenticated` gain
nothing; no `UPDATE` or `DELETE` is granted.
